[c1-l010] Iucugo. *Duas, ou mais letras, ou palauras que*
[c1-l010 note] Jukugo is explained as two or more written characters or words functioning together. The definition continues with their combined sense and literary use.

[c1-l011 note] The characters or words together have a shared meaning and are used in China. There is a visible separation in juntas tem. The final Chi is capitalized and continues as na below, without a supplied hyphen.
[c1-l012 >] *na, & em Iapão elegantemente, como ſentẽça.*
[c1-l012 note] Completes China, adds Japan, and describes elegant use like a maxim or sentence. The initial of Iapão is capital I, not lowercase l.
[c1-l013] Iucu mẽ. i, Fucǒ nemuru. *Dormir a todo leuar.*
[c1-l013 note] Jukumen denotes deep sleep, explained by fucǒ nemuru. The damaged m has faint left-side traces and can resemble n; the supplied NINJAL index corroborates the lexical identification after visual reading. The baseline’s internal space is retained, and the small mark over o is read as a caron. [c1-l014] Iucuqua. Iucuxita conomi. *Fruita madura.*

[c1-l017] Iucuxo. i, Suminaretaru tocoro. *Morada*
[c1-l017 note] Jukusho is a long-inhabited residence, suminaretaru tocoro. The OCR’s initial long s is corrected to the visible capital I.

[c1-l018 note] The dwelling is one occupied for a long time. Jucuxo starts the ensuing saying about attachment to such a place.

[c1-l019 note] Bǒjigataxi expresses difficulty in forgetting: one does not easily forget a familiar residence. The Portuguese explains this directly.
[c1-l020 >] *do lugar onde morou muito tempo.*

[c1-l021] Iucufu. Iuyano qirumono. *Certo habito, ou*
[c1-l021 note] The garment entry is printed Iucufu and explained as what a Confucian scholar wears. Its consonant order is retained rather than silently changed to a more familiar modern form.
[c1-l022 >] *veſtido de philoſophos moraes q̃ ha na China.*
[c1-l022 note] Completes the description as the dress of moral philosophers in China. Philoſophos is one printed word, not the OCR’s philoſo phos.
[c1-l023] Iucufu. Inochi ſaiuai. *Vida, & proſperida-*
[c1-l023 note] Another Iucufu denotes life and prosperity, inochi ſaiuai. Despite sharing the printed headword with the garment entry, it has a distinct meaning.
[c1-l024 >] *de, ou bẽs deſta vida.*
[c1-l024 note] Completes proſperidade and adds the goods or blessings of this life. The abbreviated bẽs and historical desta are preserved. The second pass resolves the visibly tall ſ/t ligature here as long ſ.
[c1-l025] Iufiô. Vyeqino cozuye. *Pontas dos raminhos*
[c1-l025 note] Iufiô is identified by vyekino kozue, the tips of a tree’s small branches. The uncommon headword is retained graphically; its meaning comes from the explicit bilingual gloss rather than an imposed modern reading.
[c1-l026 >] *das aruores.*
[c1-l026 note] Das aruores completes the branch-tip explanation. This short continuation precedes a separate religious headword.
[c1-l027] Iufô. Noriuo vquru. *Receber ley.*

[c1-l028] Iugacu. i, Gacumon ſuru. *Eſtudar. ¶* Iu-

[c1-l029 >] gacuno fito. *Eſtudantes.*
[c1-l029 note] Completes jugacuno fito, a student or students. The Portuguese uses the plural Estudantes. The second pass resolves the visibly tall ſ/t ligature here as long ſ.
[c1-l030] Iugan. i, Iucon. *Amizade. Vt,* Fito

[c1-l032] Iugi. Vqetamotçu. *Receber, & guardar.*
[c1-l032 note] Jugi means receiving and keeping something, vqetamotçu. Its initial is capital I rather than long ſ.
[c1-l033 >] *Vt,* Buppôuo jugi ſuru. *Receber a ley*

[c1-l035] Iugô. i, Ien acuno gôuo vquru. *Receber*
[c1-l035 note] Jugoo concerns receiving the consequences of good and bad deeds. Ien acuno gôuo vquru is the Japanese explanation; initial I in Ien carries its historical consonantal value.
[c1-l036 >] *o pago dos bens, ou males que hum fez.*
[c1-l036 note] The Portuguese completes the idea as payment or recompense for good or evil someone has done. The canonical wording is not replaced by modern doctrinal terminology.
[c1-l037] Iǔgoracu. *Quinze veas. Vt,* Iuniqe jǔgo
[c1-l037 note] Juugoraku denotes fifteen vessels in the medical account. The example juxtaposes twelve and fifteen channels; the initial I corrects OCR F.
[c1-l038 >] racu. *12, & 15. veas do corpo de que*
[c1-l038 note] Racu completes the Japanese phrase, followed by twelve and fifteen bodily vessels. The printed veas is retained rather than equated mechanically with modern anatomical veins.
[c1-l039 >] *falão os medicos.*
[c1-l039 note] The relative clause says these are the vessels discussed by physicians. This qualification locates the preceding numbers within a historical medical description.
[c1-l040] Iugue. Vyeqino xita. i, Qino moto. *Pè*
[c1-l040 note] Juge means the base or underside of a tree, vyeqino xita and qino moto. Pè begins the Portuguese phrase completed below.

[c1-l041 note] Completes the foot of the tree, then gives juge xeqixǒ, juxtaposing the tree’s base and the top of a stone. The Portuguese explanation follows.
[c1-l042 >] *pè da aruore, ou ſobre a pedra.*
[c1-l042 note] The example means being at a tree’s foot or on a stone. The two locations are alternatives in the gloss, not a single physical structure.
[c1-l043] Iuguio. Von iri. *Vinda de peßoa nobre a al-*
[c1-l043 note] Jugyo is an honoured person’s arrival at a house, paraphrased as von iri. The divided al-gũa continues on the following row.
[c1-l044 >] *gũa caſa.*
[c1-l044 note] Completes algũa caſa, some house. The row closes the visit entry before the ox-picture entry.
[c1-l045] Iǔguiǔ no zzu. *Hũas figuras de bois pintadas*
[c1-l045 note] Juugyuu no zzu names a set of painted ox figures. The first long-u mark was mistaken for n by OCR; both marked u vowels are retained from the scan.
[c1-l046 >] *ſobre que os Ienxùs dão certos pontos de me-*
[c1-l046 note] The figures furnish meditation topics for the Ienxùs, Zen religious. The initial of the embedded sect-name is read as I rather than OCR t; the grave on u is retained.
[c1-l047 >] *ditaçoẽs.*
[c1-l047 note] Ditaçoẽs completes meditaçoẽs. The tilde is printed over e in this occurrence and is not moved to match modern meditações.
