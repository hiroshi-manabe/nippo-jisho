[c1-l001 >] *custureira, ou bom official de mãos, ou bõ frẽ-*
[c1-l001 note] Continues the preceding page's hand-skill definition: a good seamstress, manual craftsperson, or archer. The faint nasal mark in bõ is retained; the archer-word continues below.
[c1-l002 >] *cheiro. ¶* Teno qijta ſaicu. *Official de*
[c1-l002 note] Completes the archer-word and introduces te no kiita saiku, a skilled craftsperson. The Portuguese explanation runs onto the next row.
[c1-l003 >] *boas mãos, ou destro. ¶* Fiǒxino qijta fi
[c1-l003 note] Finishes the dexterous-craftsperson gloss, then begins an example about someone with a good sense of rhythm. No hyphen is supplied after fi.
[c1-l004 >] to. *Homem bem compaſado, & q̃não ſae*
[c1-l004 note] To completes hito. The Portuguese describes a well-timed performer; bem corrects OCR hem, while the printed single-s compaſado is retained.
[c1-l005 >] *do tom no bailar, tanger, cantar, &c.*
[c1-l005 note] Completes the rhythm example with dancing, instrumental playing, and singing without departing from the proper measure or tone.
[c1-l006 >] *¶* Cuſuriga qiqu. *Obrar a mezinha.*
[c1-l006 note] Kusuri ga kiku means a medicine takes effect, demonstrating a sense of kiku other than hearing.
[c1-l007 >] *¶* Cagiga qicanu. *Não obedecer a embar-*
[c1-l007 note] Kaji ga kikanu describes a vessel failing to respond to its rudder. The article a is separated from the divided embarcação.
[c1-l008 >] *cação ao leme. ¶* Reôga qiqu. *Fazer*
[c1-l008 note] Completes the rudder example and begins successful hunting or fishing, whose Portuguese object follows below.
[c1-l009 >] *boa caça, ou peſcaria tomando algũa couſa.*
[c1-l009 note] Explains success as actually catching something while hunting or fishing; the source's broad sense is preserved.
[c1-l010 >] *¶* Reôno qijta fito. *Homem que ſempre*
[c1-l010 note] Describes a person successful in the hunt; the relative clause is completed on the next physical row.
[c1-l011 >] *toma algũa couſa na caça. ¶* Faga qijta
[c1-l011 note] Finishes the successful-hunter example and begins another involving favorable dice throws.
[c1-l012 >] fito. *Homem que deita bem os dados ou lhe*
[c1-l012 note] Hito completes the Japanese phrase; the Portuguese describes someone who casts dice successfully or gets favorable results.
[c1-l013 >] *ſae ſempre como deſeja. ¶ Item, per met.*
[c1-l013 note] Completes the desired-results explanation and explicitly introduces a metaphorical extension with per met.
[c1-l014 >] *Homem bem afortunado, ou que ſempre ganha*
[c1-l014 note] The figurative meaning is a fortunate person who consistently gains or wins; the field of activity follows below.
[c1-l015 >] *na mercancia, & em outras couſas.*
[c1-l015 note] Completes the favorable-fortune sense with success in trade and other matters.
[c1-l016] Qiqi. *O ouuir. ¶* Cono naua qiqiga
[c1-l016 note] Introduces the noun kiki, hearing, with an example about how a name sounds. The broad initial Q is not a separate word.
[c1-l017 >] yoi. *Eſte nome ſoa bem nos ouuidos.*
[c1-l017 note] Completes kono na wa kiki ga yoi: the name sounds pleasant to the ears.
[c1-l018] Qiqiauaxe, ſuru, eta. *Conferir ouuindo.*
[c1-l018 note] Kikiawase is comparing or checking by listening. The printed principal ending ſuru is retained rather than regularized from a modern conjugation.
[c1-l019] Qiqi ayamari, u, atta. *Entender mal o que*
[c1-l019 note] Kiki ayamari introduces mishearing or misunderstanding what is said. The source separates the two Japanese components.
[c1-l020 >] *ſe diz, ou ouuir hũa couſa por outra.*
[c1-l020 note] Completes the definition as hearing one thing in place of another.
[c1-l021] Qiqichigaye, uru, eta. *Ouuir hũa couſa*
[c1-l021 note] The verb kikichigae likewise means mishearing. Qiqi replaces the malformed OCR Quqi, supported directly by the repeated form below.
[c1-l022 >] *por outra.*
[c1-l022 note] Completes the verb's explanation, hearing one thing for another; it remains a separate short continuation.
[c1-l023] Qiqichigaye. *O ouuir hũa couſa por outra.*
[c1-l023 note] Gives the corresponding noun for mishearing, separately from the preceding verb and its principal forms.
[c1-l024] Qiqidouoi. *Couſa que ſe entende mal, ou que*
[c1-l024 note] Describes something difficult to understand by hearing. The opening Q is restored; the historical douoi spelling is not modernized.
[c1-l025 >] *ſe não percebe bem.*
[c1-l025 note] Completes the difficult-hearing definition as something not clearly perceived or understood.
[c1-l026] Qiqigatera. *Com ocaſião de ouuir, ou pera ouuir.*
[c1-l026 note] Kikigatera means on the occasion of hearing or in order to hear. The extra u in OCR Quiqigatera is absent from the scan.
[c1-l027] Qiqigoto. *Couſa pera folgar de ouuir.*
[c1-l027 note] Describes something pleasant or gratifying to hear, not simply any audible matter.
[c1-l028] Qiqi idaxi, ſu, aita. *Acertar de ouuir, ou*
[c1-l028 note] Kiki idashi concerns happening to hear or discovering through hearing. The damaged d is supported by the repeated example and NINJAL; a second-pass comparison resolves two u types in Portuguese ouuir, not an internal t.
[c1-l029 >] *ouuindo deſcobrir. Vt,* Feiqeno cotouo
[c1-l029 note] Completes the discovery gloss and starts a quotation about the Heike. The Japanese example begins after italic Vt.
[c1-l030 >] axij ſamani mǒſu monoga araba, ichinin
[c1-l030 note] Continues the conditional quotation: should someone speak badly of the Heike, the question is whether anyone hears of it.
[c1-l031 >] qiqiidaſanu fodo coſo are, &c. Feiq.
[c1-l031 note] Completes the quotation's negative hearing condition. Feiq. is the upright citation label, not part of the Japanese sentence.
[c1-l032 >] *Lib. 1. Auendo alguem que falaſſe mal do*
[c1-l032 note] Identifies book 1 and begins the Portuguese rendering of someone speaking ill of the Heike. The scan supports two long-s strokes in falaſſe.
[c1-l033 >] *Feique, em quanto hum não acertaua de ouuir*
[c1-l033 note] Continues the translation: the speaker remained safe while nobody happened to hear. Feique is an embedded Japanese name; the garbled one-letter typeface split is removed.
[c1-l034 >] *lhe valia, &c.*
[c1-l034 note] Concludes the translation by saying that the absence of a hearer served the speaker well.
[c1-l035] Qiqi ire, ruru, eta. *Dar ouuidos, & admi-*
[c1-l035 note] Kiki ire means giving ear or admitting something. The Portuguese admitir begins at the right edge.
[c1-l036 >] *tir. ¶* Mimini qiqi iruru. *Idem. Vt,*
[c1-l036 note] Completes admitir, gives mimi ni kiki iruru as the same expression, and introduces a literary example.
[c1-l037 >] Xôxôno togauoba miminimo qiqi ire ta-
[c1-l037 note] Begins an honorific statement about not giving ear even to minor faults. The final verb is physically divided after ta-.
[c1-l038 >] mauazu. Taif. *Lib. 27. Nem hum*
[c1-l038 note] Completes tamawazu and gives the printed upright citation label Taif. The Portuguese translation begins 'not a single' and continues below; the label is not silently expanded or respelled.
[c1-l039 >] *minimo peccado ouuia, nem admitia.*
[c1-l039 note] Finishes the translation as neither hearing nor admitting even the smallest fault.
[c1-l040] Qiqin. Vye, uru. *Fome gèral. Vt,* Qi-
[c1-l040 note] Kikin denotes general famine, explained through hunger. The initial Q replaces OCR g, and the example begins the same word across the line break.
[c1-l041 >] qinga yuqu. *Auer eſta fome.*
[c1-l041 note] Completes the example about a famine occurring, with Portuguese Auer retained in its historical spelling.
[c1-l042] Qiqinarai, ǒ, ǒta. *Ouuindo a prender.*
[c1-l042 note] Kikinarai means learning by hearing. The printed spacing a prender is provisionally retained rather than silently regularized to aprender.
[c1-l043] Qiqinare, ruru, eta. *Acustumar a ouuir.*
[c1-l043 note] Kikinareru means becoming accustomed to hearing. The Portuguese spelling Acustumar is preserved.
[c1-l044] Qiqinaxi, ſu, aita. *Ouuindo tomar algũa cou*
[c1-l044 note] Kikinashi concerns interpreting what one hears in a particular way. Cousa continues below without a supplied hyphen.
[c1-l045 >] *ſa em boa, ou em mà parte.*
[c1-l045 note] Completes the interpretation as taking something in a good or bad sense; the grave in mà is preserved.
[c1-l046] Qiqinauoxi, ſu, oita. *Tornar a ouuir a cou-*
[c1-l046 note] Kikinaoshi means hearing or construing something afresh. Second-pass comparison with adjacent ouuir forms establishes two u types here, correcting the draft's shorter reading.
[c1-l047 >] *ſa differentemente do que ſe tinha ouuido.*
[c1-l047 note] Completes the definition as hearing something differently from before. Two f types are visible in differentemente, correcting the draft's single f.

[c2-l001] Qiqinigue. *Fugir ſoo com a fama dos inimigos.*
[c2-l001 note] Kikinige means fleeing merely on hearing reports of the enemy. The g in nigue is visible, replacing the OCR's ç.
[c2-l002 >] *Vt* Qiqinigueuo ſuru.
[c2-l002 note] Gives kikinige wo suru as the illustrative expression. Q belongs to the upright Japanese word, not to italic Vt or a separate O.
[c2-l003] Qiqinuqi, u, uita. *Ouuir bem atè o cabo.*
[c2-l003 note] Kikinuki means hearing something properly through to the end. The printed shorter past ending uita is retained.
[c2-l004 >] *¶* Fitono zonbunuo qiqinuqu. *Ouuir*
[c2-l004 note] Begins the example of hearing out another person's thoughts or opinion; the Portuguese translation continues below.
[c2-l005 >] *o parecer dalguem bem, & inteiramente.*
[c2-l005 note] Completes the example as hearing someone's opinion carefully and completely.
[c2-l006] Qiqio. Cayeru ſaru. *Tornada, ou ida pera*
[c2-l006 note] Cayeru saru explains returning or going away, with the destination continued below. The faint trace between e and u supports r on enlargement, so Cayeru is retained rather than the first-pass Cayeu; the abraded r remains a local caveat.
[c2-l007 >] *ſua caſa, &c.*
[c2-l007 note] Completes the homeward-return definition with 'one's own house'.
[c2-l008] Qiqiǒ. Qiǒye cayeru. *Tornar ao Miaco.*
[c2-l008 note] Kyō e kaeru explains returning to the capital. The apparent italic O in the draft is actually the initial Q of Qiǒye; Miaco is embedded in Portuguese.
[c2-l009] Qiqio. Voqite iru. *O eſtar a leuantado.*
[c2-l009 note] Okite iru means being up or risen. The unmarked-o headword differs from the preceding return-to-capital entry; the source spacing a leuantado is retained.
[c2-l010] Qiqiǒ. l, Qiqei. *Eſpelho. ¶ Item, Bom*
[c2-l010 note] Gives a mirror and, by extension, a good model or example, with Qiqei as an alternative form.
[c2-l011 >] *exemplo. ¶* Iennin no qiqiǒuo mana-
[c2-l011 note] Begins an example about learning from virtuous people. Initial I in Iennin has the dictionary's consonantal value; it is not replaced by modern J or Z.
[c2-l012 >] bu. *Imitar o exemplo dos ſanctos.*
[c2-l012 note] Bu completes manabu, and the Portuguese translates the example as imitating holy or virtuous people.
[c2-l013] Qiqiǒ. *Hũa erua, ou flor, & araiz da meſ-*
[c2-l013 note] Introduces kikyō as an herb, its flower, and its root. The attached araiz and divided meſma are retained as printed.
[c2-l014 >] *ma erua. ¶* Qiqiǒ iro. *Cor deſta flor*
[c2-l014 note] Completes the plant description and introduces kikyō-iro, the flower's color.
[c2-l015 >] *q̃ he en tre azul, & roxo. ¶ Item, Hũa me-*
[c2-l015 note] Places the flower's color between blue and purple, then starts a medicinal sense. The unusual printed en tre spacing is retained.
[c2-l016 >] *zinha que ſe faz da raiz deſta erua.*
[c2-l016 note] Completes mezinha and explains that the medicine is made from the plant's root.
[c2-l017] Qiqi ſadame, uru, eta. *Ouuindo determi-*
[c2-l017 note] Kiki sadame concerns determining a matter through hearing. The headword's printed internal space is preserved.
[c2-l018 >] *nar, ou confirmarſe no que ouuio.*
[c2-l018 note] Completes determinar and adds becoming certain of what one has heard.
[c2-l019] Qiqi ſumaxi, ſu, aita. *Acabar de ouuir*
[c2-l019 note] Kiki sumashi concerns attentive or complete hearing; the Portuguese qualification follows on the next row.
[c2-l020 >] *bem, ou ouuir bem, & perfeitamente.*
[c2-l020 note] Specifies hearing well and thoroughly, completing the preceding entry rather than introducing another example.
[c2-l021] Qiqiſute, tçuru, eta. *Não acabar de ou-*
[c2-l021 note] Kikisute is not hearing a matter out, especially through disregard. The Portuguese verb is physically divided.
[c2-l022 >] *uir, como fazendo pouco caſo da couſa.*
[c2-l022 note] Completes the gloss by explaining that the listener makes little of the matter.
[c2-l023] Qiqitate, tçuru, eta. *Ouuir informando-*
[c2-l023 note] Kikitate means hearing by making inquiries or obtaining information. The Portuguese reflexive phrase continues below.
[c2-l024 >] *ſe do q̃ paßa, como o que ouuio algũa conjura*
[c2-l024 note] Describes learning what is happening, with a conspiracy as the first example. Conjuração is divided without an added hyphen.
[c2-l025 >] *ção ainda encuberta, ou o lugar onde algum la-*
[c2-l025 note] Completes the concealed-conspiracy example and begins another about a thief's hiding place.
[c2-l026 >] *drão ſe eſcondeo, &c.*
[c2-l026 note] Completes ladrão and the example of finding out where a thief has hidden.
[c2-l027] Qiqitçucuroi, ô, ôta. *Ouuir, & informar-*
[c2-l027 note] Kikitsukuroi likewise concerns hearing and informing oneself. Both printed long-vowel marks in the abbreviated principal forms are circumflexes.
[c2-l028 >] *ſe do que paßa, ou do modo, &c.*
[c2-l028 note] Completes the definition as learning what happens or how it happens.
[c2-l029] Qiqitçuqe, uru, eta. *Ouuir de nouo o que não*
[c2-l029 note] Kikitsuke begins with the sense of newly hearing something previously unknown. Ouuir replaces the malformed OCR Ouair.
[c2-l030 >] *ſabia, ou eſtaua encuberto. ¶ Item, Cuſ-*
[c2-l030 note] Completes the hidden-or-unknown sense and starts another: becoming accustomed to hearing something.
[c2-l031 >] *tumarſe a ouuir. Vt,* Qiqitçuqeta coto
[c2-l031 note] Completes custumar-se and introduces an example about a familiar matter one has heard before.
[c2-l032 >] gia. *He couſa que estou cuſtumado a ouuir.*
[c2-l032 note] Gia completes the Japanese assertion; the Portuguese confirms that this is something the speaker is accustomed to hearing.
[c2-l033] Qiqitogame, uru, eta. *Notar ouuindo.*
[c2-l033 note] Kikitogame means noticing or taking exception while hearing. The headword's initial Q is restored from the OCR's italic O.
[c2-l034] Qiqitogime, uru, eta. *Ouuir bem, & in-*
[c2-l034 note] Kikitogime is a separate entry for hearing properly and completely. Its printed gi is not changed to match the preceding game form.
[c2-l035 >] *teiramente.*
[c2-l035 note] Completes inteiramente, emphasizing completeness of hearing.
[c2-l036] Qiqitouori, u, otta. *Ouuir tudo.*
[c2-l036 note] Kikitoori is hearing everything. The historical ou sequence and abbreviated inflectional forms are preserved.
[c2-l037] Qiqitouoxi, ſu, oita. *Ouuir atè ocabo.*
[c2-l037 note] Kikitooshi means hearing through to the end. The scan has Ouuir with two u types and the attached ocabo.
[c2-l038] Qiqivaqe, uru, eta. *Ouuindo diſcernir, ou*
[c2-l038 note] Kikiwake concerns discriminating or understanding through hearing, rather than merely receiving sound.
[c2-l039 >] *entender a couſa.*
[c2-l039 note] Completes the definition as understanding the matter heard.
[c2-l040] Qiqi vaqimaye, uru, eta. *Idem.*
[c2-l040 note] Kiki wakimae is assigned the same meaning as kikiwake. The printed internal space remains.
[c2-l041] Qiqivotoxi, ſu, oita. *Eſcapar algũa couſa*
[c2-l041 note] Kikiotoshi means missing something in hearing. The scan's v replaces OCR r, with the corrected headword also supported by NINJAL.
[c2-l042 >] *por ouuir, ou deixar de ou. algũa couſa.*
[c2-l042 note] Explains leaving something unheard. The visibly shortened ou. is retained rather than editorially expanded to ouuir; its damaged following punctuation is not treated as missing words.
[c2-l043] Qiqivoyobi, u, ôda. *Ouuir como de longe,*
[c2-l043 note] Kikioyobi is hearing from afar or coming to hear of something. The opening Q replaces the OCR's detached lowercase o.
[c2-l044 >] *ou vir à noticia dalgũa couſa.*
[c2-l044 note] Adds coming to knowledge of a matter; the grave in à is preserved.
[c2-l045] Qiqixiri, ru, itta. *Saber de ouuida, ou en-*
[c2-l045 note] Kikishiri means knowing by hearing, with a further sense concerning understanding speech.
[c2-l046 >] *tender a lingua estranha, &c. Vt,* Yǔ co-
[c2-l046 note] Completes entender and specifies a foreign language, then starts a quotation about not understanding spoken words.
[c2-l047 >] tobamo qiqixirazu, &c. Feiq. *Lib. 1.*
[c2-l047 note] Completes kotoba mo kikishirazu and gives the upright Heike citation label with italic book reference. The Portuguese translation begins on the following page; Nem below is a catchword.
