[c1-l001] Fiqimi, ru, ita. *Ver, & prouar a alguem.*
[c1-l001 note] Hikimiru is inspecting or testing a person. Initial F is damaged but fits both the alphabetic sequence and the following example.
[c1-l002 >] *Vt.* Fitono cocorouo fiqi miru. *Idem,*
[c1-l002 note] Hito no kokoro o hiki miru is testing someone's heart or intentions. Idem indicates the same meaning as the preceding gloss.
[c1-l003] Fiqimogui, u, oida. T*irarcom força, ou ar-*
[c1-l003 note] Hikimogu is tearing off by force. The faint past ending oida is supported by the remaining type and the parallel NINJAL headword, not an invented separate o da.
[c1-l004 >] *rancar. Vt,* Yubiuo fiqimogu. *Arran-*
[c1-l004 note] Yubi o hikimogu gives fingers as the object of tearing off. The Portuguese arrancar continues at the row break.
[c1-l005 >] *car os dedos.*
[c1-l005 note] Os dedos completes the finger example; this is a physical continuation of arrancar.
[c1-l006] Fiqimono. *Instrumento muſico. Improp.*
[c1-l006 note] Hikimono is a musical instrument, qualified by Improp. The explanatory qualification limits the use rather than naming another instrument.
[c1-l007] Fiqimono. *Traues da caſa.*
[c1-l007 note] A second hikimono denotes a house's beams. Traves is a building term, unrelated to the music sense above.
[c1-l008] Fiqimono. *Couſa de comer que ſe reparte por*
[c1-l008 note] The food sense of hikimono refers to something distributed among diners. The timing follows in the continuation.
[c1-l009 >] *todos depois de estarem à meſa.*
[c1-l009 note] Everyone is already at table before this food is distributed, completing the preceding definition.
[c1-l010] Fiqimono. *Obra feita ao torno.*
[c1-l010 note] The fourth hikimono is lathe-turned work, linking it to hiku's turning sense.
[c1-l011] Fiqimoyouoxi, ſu, oita. *Perſuadir, ou ex*
[c1-l011 note] Hikimoyoosu is persuading or stirring others to action. Ex begins excitar, not executar, in the next row.
[c1-l012 >] *citar a outros como pera ir ao campo, &c.*
[c1-l012 note] Going out to the field is an example of the action encouraged. Citar completes excitar and resolves the OCR cutar.
[c1-l013] Fiqimucaye, uru, eta. *Puxando virar algũa*
[c1-l013 note] Hikimukaeru means pulling or turning something to face another object. The following citation illustrates carts.
[c1-l014 >] *couſa direita, ou de fronte doutra. Vt,* Ya-
[c1-l014 note] The object is placed directly facing another; Ya- begins yagate in the cited Japanese sentence.
[c1-l015 >] gate Ioſephno von curumauo Iacobno võ
[c1-l015 note] Yagate Joseph no on kuruma o Jacob no on... describes Joseph's carriage relative to Jacob's. Biblical names are embedded in Japanese syntax and retain their printed Latin spellings.
[c1-l016 >] curumano mayeye fiqimucaye tamaitçu-
[c1-l016 note] Kuruma no mae e hikimukae tamaitsutsu brings the carriage before the other. The final tsutsu is split across physical rows.
[c1-l017 >] tçu. *2.* part*.* Goſag. *Logo fizerão ro-*
[c1-l017 note] The citation is to part 2 of Goſag. Logo begins the Portuguese rendering of the carriage movement.
[c1-l018 >] *dear o carro de Ioſeph pera diante do carro de*
[c1-l018 note] Joseph's carriage is turned in front of another carriage; the owner's name finishes on the next row.
[c1-l019 >] *Iacob.*
[c1-l019 note] Jacob completes the counterpart to Joseph. The middle c was an OCR d, not another proper name.
[c1-l020] Fiqimuqe, uru, eta. *Puxando por algũa cou*
[c1-l020 note] Hikimukeru means turning a thing by pulling it. Couſa is divided at the line end.
[c1-l021 >] *ſa virala.*
[c1-l021 note] Virala completes the turning action with its attached feminine object pronoun.
[c1-l022] Fiqimuſubi, u, unda. *Atar hũa couſa com*
[c1-l022 note] Hikimusubu is tying one thing to another. A long-s type, not l, gives the intelligible musubu compound.
[c1-l023 >] *outra. ¶* Yuoriuo fiqimuſubu. *Fazer*
[c1-l023 note] Yuori o hikimusubu is glossed as making a hut. The spelling Yuori is retained rather than silently changed to the expected iori.
[c1-l024 >] *choupana.*
[c1-l024 note] Choupana, a hut, completes the example of constructing a shelter by binding material together.
[c1-l025] Fiqin. Tobu tori. *Paßaro que voa.*
[c1-l025 note] Hikin is paraphrased as tobu tori, a flying bird. This is a separate Sino-Japanese noun amid hiki- compounds.
[c1-l026] Fiqinabiqe, uru, eta. *Dobrar, ou inclinar.*
[c1-l026 note] Hikinabikeru is bending or inclining, extended to inclining someone's heart in the next example.
[c1-l027 >] *¶* Cocorouo fiqinabiquru. *Dobrar, &*
[c1-l027 note] Kokoro o hikinabikuru describes moving or winning over a heart. The old -kuru ending is preserved.
[c1-l028 >] *mouer o coração.*
[c1-l028 note] Mover o coração completes the figurative persuasion explanation.
[c1-l029] Fiqinaraxi, ſu, aita. *Fazer ſoar os inſtrumẽ*
[c1-l029 note] Hikinarasu is making musical instruments sound. The repaired initial F avoids the nonsensical eikin arasu kana sequence.
[c1-l030 >] *tos, &c. Vt,* Biuano catachiuo tçucuri,
[c1-l030 note] Biwa no katachi o tsukuri describes making the shape of a biwa, followed by how it is strung.
[c1-l031 >] vmano vouo caqe fiqi naraxi, &c. Fox*.*
[c1-l031 note] Uma no o o kake hiki narashi uses horse-tail hair as strings, then sounds the instrument. Fox. is a source label, not part of the sentence.
[c1-l032 >] L*ib. 4. Fazendo hũa forma de viola, &*
[c1-l032 note] Book 4 is visibly cited, not a paragraph mark. The Portuguese begins making a viola-like form.
[c1-l033 >] *pondo lhe ſedas de caualo por cordas a fez*
[c1-l033 note] Horse hairs are put on it as strings. Sedas de cavalo describes that material, not silk cloth in this context.
[c1-l034 >] *ſoar.*
[c1-l034 note] Soar completes making the reconstructed instrument sound, corresponding to hiki narashi.
[c1-l035] Fiqinauoxi, ſu, oita. *Endereitar a couſa pu-*
[c1-l035 note] Hikinaosu is straightening something by pulling. The inflectional u has no secure accent to preserve from the OCR grave.
[c1-l036 >] *xando, ou mudala a outro lugar.*
[c1-l036 note] Moving the thing to another place is an alternative application of the same verb.
[c1-l037] Fiqinobe, uru, eta. *Puxando estirar. Vt,*
[c1-l037 note] Hikinoberu is stretching by pulling. Its example comes from a Christian narrative rather than everyday clothing.
[c1-l038 >] Iennhoninno xuſocuuo fiqinobete xitani
[c1-l038 note] Iennhoninno xusocuuo fiqinobete xitani means stretching out the pious woman’s hands and feet, with xitani introducing what is placed beneath. Enlargement resolves the damaged second n in Iennhonin; NINJAL independently lists Iennhonin (zennyonin). The Portuguese Sancta supports that identification.
[c1-l039 >] fiuo tacaxe, &c. *1. part.* Goſag. *Eſ-*
[c1-l039 note] Hi o takase describes having a fire kindled. Part 1 of Goſag. is cited; the omitted i in fiuo was a meaningful OCR loss.
[c1-l040 >] *tendendo os pes, & mãos da Sancta, & pon*
[c1-l040 note] The Portuguese describes stretching the saint's feet and hands, confirming the Japanese limb pair.
[c1-l041 >] *dolhe fogo de baixo, &c.*
[c1-l041 note] Fire is placed below her. This finishes the narrative illustration of stretching, not a new entry.
[c1-l042] Fiqinoboxe, ſuru, eta. *Puxando fazer ſobir.*
[c1-l042 note] Hikinoboseru is causing something to rise by pulling. Nobose is causative/transitive in this compound.
[c1-l043] Fiqi*n*ocori, u, otta. *Reſtar nas contas de*
[c1-l043 note] Hikinokoru is what remains in accounts. De begins depois in the following line.
[c1-l044 >] *pois de deſcontar, ou deitar em deſpeſa, &c.*
[c1-l044 note] The remainder follows deductions or expenditure. Deſcontar is visible and restores the accounting infinitive obscured by OCR.
[c1-l045] Fiqinoqe, uru, eta. *Apartar, ou afastar.*
[c1-l045 note] Hikinokeru is setting aside or moving away, transitive in contrast with the next entry.
[c1-l046] Fiqinoqi, u, oita. *Apartarſe, ou afa-*
[c1-l046 note] Hikinoku is moving oneself away. Its reflexive Portuguese equivalents differentiate it from hikinokeru.
[c1-l047 >] *ſtarſe.*
[c1-l047 note] Starſe completes afastar-se, the second reflexive equivalent. The line is a body continuation despite its short length.

[c2-l001] Fiqinoxe, ſuru, eta. *Puxando por alguẽ em-*

[c2-l005 >] *tante, Catana, &c.*

[c2-l006] Fiqio. i, Dǒrinai coto. *Sem rezão, ou ſem*

[c2-l007 >] *justiça.*

[c2-l008] Fiqiô. *Vide.* Fiqeô.

[c2-l009] Fiqiocu. *Paßos de garganta, ou quebros da*
[c2-l009 note] Hikyoku describes musical ornaments or fine turns of the voice. The Portuguese throat metaphor should not be read literally as steps.
[c2-l010 >] *voz particulares, ou certas delicadezas como*
[c2-l010 note] Particular vocal turns and refinements continue the musical explanation; delicadezas is a single word.
[c2-l011 >] *fanteſias no tanger. ¶* Fiqiocuuo tçucu-
[c2-l011 note] Fantesias in instrumental playing is the comparison. Hikyoku o tsukusu begins an example of musical mastery.
[c2-l012 >] ſu. *Eſmerarſe na arte de cantar, ou tanger*

[c2-l013 >] *instrumento muſico.*
[c2-l013 note] Instrumento musico completes the object of playing and closes the musical entry.
[c2-l014] Fiqiqiri, u, itta. *Puxando cortar, ou ſerrar.*

[c2-l015] Fiqiſagari, u, atta. *Ficar a tras indo ou-*
[c2-l015 note] Hikisagaru is falling behind while other people advance. The relative movement follows in the next row.
[c2-l016 >] *tros diante.*
[c2-l016 note] Outros diante finishes 'others ahead', explaining the backward position of the subject.
[c2-l017] Fiqiſaqe, uru, eta. *Raſgarſe, ou deſpeda-*
[c2-l017 note] Hikisakeru here is tearing apart in an intransitive sense. The long-s type supplies saqe, not laqe.
[c2-l018 >] *çarſe.*
[c2-l018 note] Despedaçar-se completes the reflexive tearing explanation. The damaged vowel is read a in the verb, with uncertainty from the weak impression.
[c2-l019] Fiqiſaqi, u. aita. *Raſgar, ou deſpedaçar.*
[c2-l019 note] Hikisaku is the transitive counterpart, tearing something apart. Multiple damaged initial types caused the OCR raqa sequence.
[c2-l020] Fiqiſaraxi, ſu, aita. *Puxando, ou arras-*
[c2-l020 note] Hikisarasu is dragging something out for public exposure. The following corpse example confirms sarasu rather than tarasu.
[c2-l021 >] *tando por em publico. Vt,* Ca*b*aneuo fiqi
[c2-l021 note] Kabane o hiki... introduces a corpse as the object of exposure; arrastando completes the Portuguese dragging action.
[c2-l022 >] ſaraſare, &c. *Puxando pello corpo morto*

[c2-l023 >] *deixalo no campo ſem ſer enterrado.*
[c2-l023 note] The body is left in the open unburied, explaining the exposure sense without adding content to the diplomatic text.
[c2-l024] Fiqiſobame, uru, eta. *Chegar, ou afaſtar*
[c2-l024 note] Hikisobameru is drawing something toward one's side or moving it aside. Afastar is the Portuguese infinitive, not afaftar.
[c2-l025 >] *pera algũa parte como quem foſſe pera tomar*
[c2-l025 note] The illustrative scenario begins with someone going to take an object; the next line introduces the competing action.
[c2-l026 >] *algũa couſa, & eu a tomaſſe primeiro, & afas-*
[c2-l026 note] The speaker takes it first and moves it away. The subordinate tomaſſe prepares the parallel afastaſſe.
[c2-l027 >] *taſſe pera minha ilharga, &c.*
[c2-l027 note] Moving it toward one's own side completes the scenario. Taſſe, not toſſe, completes the preceding verb.
[c2-l028] Fiqiſobiqi, u, ijta. *Meliùs, ficojiri, u. Le-*
[c2-l028 note] Hikisobiku is dragging along, with hikojiru recommended as the better expression. The latter is printed in the italic gloss despite being Japanese.
[c2-l029 >] *uar a raſto. X.*

[c2-l030] Fiqiſoconai, ǒ, ǒta. *Errar no tanger ins-*
[c2-l030 note] Hikisokonau is making a mistake while playing an instrument. The open-o inflectional marks are visible, and inſ- begins instrumento.
[c2-l031 >] *trumento muſico. Item, per met. Encami-*
[c2-l031 note] After the musical error sense, per met. introduces a figurative meaning of misleading someone.
[c2-l032 >] *nhar mal, ou peruerter a alguem.*
[c2-l032 note] Directing someone wrongly or corrupting them completes the figurative counterpart to faulty playing.
[c2-l033] Fiqiſuguri,u, utta. *Eſcolher como boa gẽte,*

[c2-l037] Fiqiſuye, uru, eta. *Puxando por, ou aßen-*
[c2-l037 note] Hikisueru is pulling someone into position or seating them. The example below is coercive rather than ordinary hospitality.
[c2-l038 >] *tar a alguem como preſo, ou malfeitor em algũ*
[c2-l038 note] A prisoner or wrongdoer is placed at a particular location. The description qualifies whom the verb acts upon.
[c2-l039 >] *lugar pera o matar, &c.*
[c2-l039 note] The location is for killing the prisoner, completing this historical usage example.
[c2-l040] Fiqitachi, u, atta. *Por ſe em feição de ſe aco*
[c2-l040 note] Hikitatsu describes a group getting ready to withdraw or flee. Its full F initial is present despite the OCR omission.
[c2-l041 >] *lher, ou fugir muita gente como do arrayal,*
[c2-l041 note] Many people may flee from camp; the comma continues into the cited example rather than ending the entry.
[c2-l042 >] *&c. Vt,* Vôjeino fiqitachitaru cuxe
[c2-l042 note] Vǒjeino fiqitachitaru cuxe introduces the habitual retreat of a large body of people. Cuxe is くせ (癖), habit/custom: xe is the regular Jesuit spelling for セ, historically pronounced roughly シェ. This is not an anomalous x or an error for s/ſ. Likewise je regularly represents ゼ, historically roughly ジェ. The modern kana reading must not be used to modernize the printed consonant.
[c2-l043 >] nareba, fitocayeximo cayeſazu, &c. Tai-
[c2-l043 note] Nareba connects the cause; hitokaeshi mo kaesazu describes not turning them back even once. The divided Tai- begins the citation label.
[c2-l044 >] feiq. *Lib. 7. Sendo coſtume de muita*
[c2-l044 note] Taifeiq., book 7, is the visibly printed citation form. The Portuguese begins explaining the tendency of a large group.
[c2-l045 >] *gente que eſtã posta em fugida, não ouue faze-*
[c2-l045 note] The people are already in flight, and could not be made to return. The construction continues into faze-los below.
[c2-l046 >] *los tornar a tras, nem hũa ſò vez.*
[c2-l046 note] Not even once could they be turned back. Só is a Portuguese limiting word, not fô, and the scan supports a long-s initial.
[c2-l047] Fiqitacuri, u, utta. *Enrolar puxando alinha*
[c2-l047 note] Hikitakuru is winding in a line by pulling it. The gloss continues at f195; the intervening de peſ- below is its repeated catchword, not an extra body continuation.
