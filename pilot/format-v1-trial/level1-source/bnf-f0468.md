[c1-l004] Taixu. Vôqini mamoru. i, Cunino nuxi.
[c1-l004 note] Taishu is explained through 'guard greatly' and kuni no nushi, the lord of a country. These Japanese explanatory equivalents remain roman, distinct from the Portuguese on the next row.

[c1-l005 note] The Portuguese defines the lord as ruling one kingdom or more, completing the territorial sense of taishu rather than the drinking sense below.
[c1-l006] Taixu. Vôzaqe. *O beber muito. Vt,* Tai-
[c1-l006 note] This homograph taishu concerns heavy drinking, with oozake as its Japanese equivalent. The example is divided after Tai-; the italic Vt introduces it.

[c1-l007 note] Tai-shu de gozaru describes being a heavy drinker. The Portuguese offers both being a wine drinker and drinking much, with the latter alternative continuing below.

[c1-l009] Taixut. Xrizoqi izzuru. *Tornarſe, ou irſe.*
[c1-l009 note] Taishutsu is withdrawing or leaving, matching tornar-se or ir-se. The cramped Xrizoqi is retained as printed rather than silently expanded to expected shirizoki; the following izzuru supplies 'go out'.
[c1-l010] Taiya. *A noite antes da comendação que fazẽ*
[c1-l010 note] Taiya is the night preceding a memorial observance for a deceased person. The relative clause continues onto the next two rows; the abbreviation tilde in fazẽ is retained.

[c1-l011 note] The observance is for the deceased and includes a banquet for Buddhist clergy and others. Bõ- begins Portuguese bonzos, so its initial B belongs with the italic gloss, not a separate roman Japanese span.
[c1-l012 >] *zos, & outros.*
[c1-l012 note] Zos completes the divided word for Buddhist clergy, followed by 'and others'. The brief physical continuation remains separate and is not a new headword.
[c1-l013] Taiyacu. i, Tacai cuſuri. *Mezinha de pre-*
[c1-l013 note] Taiyaku is an expensive, valuable medicine, clarified by takai kusuri. The abraded consonant in cuſuri is read as long s from its remaining trace and the medicine gloss, not preserved as the OCR's meaningless cuuri.
[c1-l014 >] *ço, & boa.*
[c1-l014 note] Completes preço, giving a medicine of value and good quality. Both this fragment and the hyphenated beginning belong to the Portuguese definition.
[c1-l015] Taiyei. Vôqina itonami. *Grande ocupação,*
[c1-l015 note] Taiyei, explained as ookina itonami, is a large undertaking or occupation. The example concerns costly enterprises rather than merely being personally busy.
[c1-l016 >] *ou negocio, & de gaſto como de obras, con-*
[c1-l016 note] The undertaking involves business and expenditure, with construction works and a banquet as examples. Con- is the beginning of convite on the next physical row.

[c1-l019 note] Taiyoo and taiyoona describe equivalence or matching form. The printed y letters correct the OCR's Taijǒ and tajǒna; l introduces the alternative adjectival form.

[c1-l023 note] Taiyoo is now the philosophical pairing of substance and accident. Its circumflex contrasts with the preceding entry's caron, and the abbreviated q̃ continues the explanation below.

[c1-l026 note] Taiyuu is great bravery, matched by ookina isami and grande valentia. The scan shows the long-vowel mark in Vôqina that the OCR omitted.
[c1-l027 >] *preſteza.*
[c1-l027 note] Presteza adds readiness or alacrity to the bravery gloss. This short Portuguese continuation belongs to taiyuu and carries no new headword.
[c1-l028] Taiza. Mucai za. *Eſtar no meſmo andar, ou*
[c1-l028 note] Taiza, with mukai za, describes sitting opposite or on the same level. The Portuguese continues from being on the same floor or level to facing one another.

[c1-l030 note] Taizai denotes staying or detention; todokoori and imasu explain remaining in a place. The printed circumflex and comma are retained rather than normalizing the historical form.
[c1-l031] Taizocu. Xôguatno imiǒ. i, Xǒguat. *Pri-*
[c1-l031 note] Taizoku is presented as another name for the first month, shoogat(su). Pri- is wholly italic. The first o-mark is damaged and its baseline circumflex is retained provisionally; the later carons are clearer and are not imposed on that occurrence.
[c1-l032 >] *meira lua, ou mes.*
[c1-l032 note] Meira completes primeira: the first lunar month or month of the year. The physical division is retained instead of joining the word across rows.

[c1-l038] Tajit. Betno fi. *Outro dia.*
