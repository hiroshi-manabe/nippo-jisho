[c2-l015 note] Guru completes aguru, the alternative to sasaguru. The Portuguese explicitly identifies the action as presenting this petition.
[c2-l016] Meyǒ. *Aceno do olho.*
[c2-l016 note] Meyoo denotes a signal or gesture made with the eye. The caron over o remains distinct from an ordinary unmarked vowel.
[c2-l017] Mezamaxij. *Couſa de eſpanto.*

[c2-l019 >] Mezamaxǔ.
[c2-l019 note] Mezamashuu is the adverbial form of mezamashii. The scan's marked final u corrects the OCR i and the stale kana hint derived from it.

[c2-l021 >] *netra as couſas.*
[c2-l021 note] Netra completes penetra, describing insight into matters. It belongs with the discerning person above rather than with the following eye-movement entry.
[c2-l022] Mezzucai. *Meneo dos olhos. ¶* Mezzu-
[c2-l022 note] Mezukai is movement or use of the eyes. Mezzu- begins an example using the same noun, divided at the physical line boundary.

[c2-l024 note] The eye movement does not belong to just any ordinary person. Mento completes movimento and the negative comparison supplies the force of tada mono dewa nai.

[c2-l027] Mezzuracani. *Adu.* i, Mezzuraxǔ.
[c2-l027 note] Mezurakani is explicitly an adverb and is equated with mezurashuu. The abbreviation Adu. is Portuguese apparatus; the Japanese synonym after i is not an embedded term to flag for whole-word font toggling.
[c2-l028] Mezzuraxij. *Couſa noua, & agradauel.*
[c2-l028 note] Mezurashii is glossed as new and pleasing. The scan supports noua, not OCR nona, while preserving the historical u spelling.
[c2-l029 >] Mezzuraxiſa.

[c2-l030 note] Mezurashuu is the adverbial derivative of mezurashii. Its final marked u is visible and parallels the earlier synonym and the mezamashuu form.
