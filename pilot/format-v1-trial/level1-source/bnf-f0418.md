[c2-l001 >] *& atolando os pès dos caualos nella de manei-*

[c2-l009] Ricuriô. *Andas, ou carro em que caualga o*

[c2-l010 >] *Rey.*

[c2-l011] Ricut. *Razão, ou palauras com que hum ar-*

[c2-l014] Ricutǒ. *Liuro de arte militar.*
[c2-l014 note] Rikutō names a book of military art. The caron differentiates this title from the preceding unmarked Ricut.
[c2-l015] Ridon. *Agudeza, & tardeza de engenho.*
[c2-l015 note] The gloss pairs mental sharpness with slowness of understanding. The contrast is intentional, not a contradictory word to be corrected.
[c2-l016] Rifat. i, Saican. *Viueza, & ſagacidade,*
[c2-l016 note] Rihatsu is equated with Saican and defined as liveliness and sagacity. Another equivalent follows on the next row.
[c2-l017 >] *ou diſcriçaõ.*

[c2-l018] Rifatjin. *Homem eſperto, & ſagaz.*
[c2-l018 note] Names an alert, sagacious person. The jin ending distinguishes the person noun from the quality itself.
[c2-l019] Rifatna. *Idem.*
[c2-l019 note] Gives the adjectival form with the same alertness and sagacity sense. Idem refers to the description immediately above.
[c2-l020 >] Rifatni. *Adu.*
[c2-l020 note] Gives the corresponding adverb. Its indentation and the italic grammatical label are preserved.
[c2-l021 >] Rifatſa.
[c2-l021 note] Gives the nominal derivative for the quality or degree of sagacity. No separate gloss is printed; the final stop is visible.
[c2-l022] Rifi. *Razaõ, & ſem razaõ. Vt* Rifiuo
[c2-l022 note] Rihi contrasts right and wrong, or reason and its absence. The example has the object particle uo in Rifiuo, not the OCR's no; the whole Japanese word is roman.
[c2-l023 >] vaquru. *Dicernir a rezaõ, & ſem rezaõ,*
[c2-l023 note] Wakuru completes the instruction to distinguish right from wrong. Dicernir is retained without adding the s of a more familiar spelling.
[c2-l024 >] *ou juſtiça, & injuſtiça.*

[c2-l025] Rifujin. Cotouariuo tçucuſazu. *O naõ exa-*
[c2-l025 note] Kotowari wo tsukusazu is not fully examining or exhausting the reasons. The damaged vowel in naõ is a, not u; exa- continues the Portuguese verb below.
[c2-l026 >] *minar bem a razaõ. Vt,* Rifujin no vôxe.
[c2-l026 note] Completes the failure to examine the reasons properly and introduces an unreasonable command. The repeated headword contains f, not OCR t.
[c2-l027 >] *Mandado ſem razaõ, & juſtiça.*
[c2-l027 note] Translates the command as lacking reason and justice. The gloss is wholly Portuguese and remains italic.
[c2-l028] Rifujinna. *Couſa deſarrezoada.*

[c2-l029] Rifujinni. *Adu.*
[c2-l029 note] Gives the adverb 'unreasonably'. Both R and f are supported by the adjacent forms, replacing the OCR's Birujinni.
[c2-l030] Riguai. *Fora da razão.*

[c2-l031] Riguiǔ. Madara vxi. *Vaca malhada.*
[c2-l031 note] Madara ushi identifies a mottled or piebald bovine. The Portuguese specifically says a spotted cow; the marked headword is retained.
[c2-l032] Rijeme. *O apertar, ou conuencer com a força*

[c2-l034] Rijun. i, Ritocu. *Ganho, ou proueito.*

[c2-l035] Rimot. *Ganho, ou proueito, ou fruito.*

[c2-l038] Rin. *Hũa maneira de campainha, ou batega*

[c2-l040 >] naraſu. *Tanger eſta batega. Item, Em algũas*

[c2-l042 >] *painha.*

[c2-l047 >] *Iapão algũa tira doutro pano, peça, &c.*
[c2-l047 note] Specifies a strip or piece of another cloth. The scan has singular peça and the normal final &c., not the OCR's peças and mixed-font &oc.
