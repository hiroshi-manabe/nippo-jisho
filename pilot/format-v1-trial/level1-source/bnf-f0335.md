[c1-l001] Mǒxicauaxi, ſu, aita. *Trauar pratica, ou*
[c1-l001 note] Mǒxicauaxi means exchanging speech or entering into conversation. The caron is visible; its continuation gives the interlocutors.
[c1-l002 >] *falar com outros.*
[c1-l002 note] Falar com outros completes the definition as speaking with others. This is a short continuation of the conversational verb.
[c1-l003] Mǒxicaye, uru, eta. *Desdizerſe, ou em-*
[c1-l003 note] Mǒxicaye means retracting or changing what one has said. The faint caron is visible, and Desdizerſe corrects OCR Dendizerſe.
[c1-l004 >] *mendar o que està dito.*
[c1-l004 note] The second gloss is amending what has been said. The scan supports final à in està, not OCR i, while the physical em-mendar division is preserved.
[c1-l005] Mǒxicayouaxi, ſu, aita. *Fazer ir, & vir*
[c1-l005 note] Mǒxicayouaxi means making messages go back and forth. The causative communication sense continues over the next two rows.
[c1-l006 >] *com recados, ou communicarſe com algum por*
[c1-l006 note] The gloss concerns exchanging messages or communicating with someone. Communicarſe retains its doubled m and long ſ.
[c1-l007 >] *carta, ou recado.*
[c1-l007 note] Communication is by letter or message. This closes the definition without adding a modern distinction between written and oral media.
[c1-l008] Mǒxicoxi, ſu, oita. *Mandar dizer.*
[c1-l008 note] Mǒxicoxi means sending word or sending someone to say something. The printed oita principal form is retained.
[c1-l009] Moxicuua. i, Xijen. *Poruentura, l, ou.* S.
[c1-l009 note] Moxicuua is equated with Xijen and glossed as 'perhaps' or the conjunction 'or'. The o remains unmarked. The final written-register label S. is upright in the scan, outside the italic Portuguese gloss.
[c1-l010] Mǒxicuuaye, uru, eta. *Acrecentar ao nu-*
[c1-l010 note] Mǒxicuuaye means adding someone to the number of others through speech or intercession. The word numero is divided after nu-.
[c1-l011 >] *mero de outros falando, ou intercedendo, &c.*
[c1-l011 note] This completes the explanation of adding by speaking or interceding. The scan has falando without the OCR's extra u.
[c1-l012] Mǒxidaxi, ſu, aita. l, Mǒxi idaxi, ſu. *Co-*
[c1-l012 note] Mǒxidaxi and Mǒxi idaxi are explicitly alternative forms. The Portuguese Co- is italic with its line-end hyphen and is completed below.
[c1-l013 >] *meçar a dizer, ou mouer pratica.*
[c1-l013 note] The gloss is beginning to speak or opening a conversation. It completes Co-meçar while preserving the physical line break.
[c1-l014] Mǒxifanaxi, ſu, aita. *Lançar de ſi com pa-*
[c1-l014 note] Mǒxifanaxi means dismissing something by words or speaking decisively. The divided pa-lauras is retained across the next row.
[c1-l015 >] *lauras, ou dizer reſolutamente.*
[c1-l015 note] The second gloss specifies speaking resolutely. This completes the same entry, not a separate synonym headword.
[c1-l016] Mǒxifayaxi, ſu, aita. *Louuar, ou encare-*
[c1-l016 note] Mǒxifayaxi means praising or extolling something in conversation. The second Portuguese infinitive is divided after encare-.
[c1-l017 >] *cer algũa couſa em pratica.*
[c1-l017 note] Cer completes encarecer, extolling something in conversation. The abbreviation tilde in algũa is retained.
[c1-l018] Mǒxifiraqi, u, aita. *Dar rezãa como ẽ de-*
[c1-l018 note] Mǒxifiraqi means explaining or giving reasons in defence. The scan appears to print final a in rezãa, unlike rezão two rows below; that anomaly is retained. The space after ẽ and the divided de-fenſa preserve the physical setting.
[c1-l019 >] *fenſa, &c. ¶* Dǒrino muneuo mǒxi
[c1-l019 note] The defence gloss ends and a Japanese example begins, stating the grounds or substance of one's reasoning. Dǒrino and mǒxi both visibly have carons.
[c1-l020 >] firaite, &c. Xid. *Dando rezão, ou ar-*
[c1-l020 note] Firaite completes the Japanese example of explaining the grounds. Xid. is a wholly roman citation label, not the OCR's mixed Xi/d styling; here rezão ends with o.
[c1-l021 >] *rezoando.*
[c1-l021 note] Rezoando completes arrezoando, giving reasons or making an argument. The word remains divided at the physical line break.
[c1-l022] Mǒxifuraxi, ſu, aita. *Lançar fama, ou pu-*
[c1-l022 note] Mǒxifuraxi means spreading a report or making something public. The scan supports f in both furaxi and fama, correcting the OCR's r and long ſ respectively.
[c1-l023 >] *blicar.*
[c1-l023 note] Blicar completes publicar, to make public. This is the end of the spreading-a-report definition.
[c1-l024] Mǒxifure, uru, eta. *Apregoar, ou fazer*
[c1-l024 note] Mǒxifure means announcing publicly or making known. The worn f has a surviving upper trace; the proclamation gloss supports fure rather than the OCR's rure.
[c1-l025 >] *a ſaber.*
[c1-l025 note] A ſaber completes fazer a ſaber, making something known. The short continuation belongs to the announcement entry.
[c1-l026] Mǒxifuzzucuri, uru, utta. *Negocear de*
[c1-l026 note] Mǒxifuzzucuri means seeking something through negotiation or verbal contrivance. A faint caron survives over the damaged first o; the doubled zz and printed uru, utta remain unchanged.
[c1-l027 >] *palaura, ou com inuenção de palauras procu-*
[c1-l027 note] The gloss specifies negotiation by speech or by devising words. The worn but recoverable u in palauras corrects OCR palaaras; procu- continues below.
[c1-l028 >] *rar algũa couſa.*
[c1-l028 note] Rar completes procurar, seeking to obtain something. This closes the verbal-negotiation definition.
[c1-l029] Mǒxigoto. *Dito, ou fala.*
[c1-l029 note] Mǒxigoto is a saying or utterance. The noun is distinguished from the surrounding inflected verbs.
[c1-l030] Mǒxi daxi, ſu, aita. *Vide,* Mǒxidaxi, ſu.
[c1-l030 note] The separately printed Mǒxi daxi points back to Mǒxidaxi. The visible spacing and absence of an additional i before daxi are retained rather than normalized from the earlier alternative form.
[c1-l031] Mǒxi ire, uru, eta. *Dizer a alguem.*
[c1-l031 note] Mǒxi ire means telling someone. The o has a caron, correcting the OCR's circumflex.
[c1-l032 >] *¶* Annaiuo mǒxi iruru. *Fazer aſaber.*
[c1-l032 note] The example concerns conveying notice or information. The scan supplies the omitted paragraph mark and the x in mǒxi, replacing the malformed mǒ ci; the Portuguese keeps the printed joined aſaber.
[c1-l033] Mǒxijo. *Carta, ou eſcrito em que ſe darazão*
[c1-l033 note] Mǒxijo is a letter or document giving an account of something. The visible c in eſcrito corrects OCR eſerito; the printed joined darazão is retained. The headword's final o has no established accent, so the lexical index's Mǒxijǒ is not substituted for this copy's form.
[c1-l034 >] *dalgũa couſa.*
[c1-l034 note] Dalgũa couſa completes the matter explained in the document. Its abbreviation tilde remains unexpanded.
[c1-l035] Mǒximauaxi, ſu, aita. *Dar recado, ou pu-*
[c1-l035 note] Mǒximauaxi means sending notice around or publishing it in different places. The mark over o is read as a caron, not the OCR's circumflex.
[c1-l036 >] *blicar por diuerſas partes. ¶ Item, per met.*
[c1-l036 note] This completes publicar por diuerſas partes and introduces a metaphorical extension. The scan indents the continuation, unlike the unindented OCR line marker.
[c1-l037 >] *Falar, ou dizer diuerſas couſas.*
[c1-l037 note] The extended sense is speaking or saying various things. Per met. in the preceding row explicitly signals this figurative use.
[c1-l038] Mǒxi midaxi, ſu, aita. *Perturbar, ou embu*
[c1-l038 note] Mǒxi midaxi means confusing or disturbing through words. Perturbar ends in r, not OCR s; embu continues below without an invented hyphen.
[c1-l039 >] *rulhar com palauras.*
[c1-l039 note] Rulhar completes emburulhar, here tangling or confusing through speech. The division and historical form are retained.
[c1-l040] Mǒxi muſubi, u, unda. *Tratar, ou nego-*
[c1-l040 note] Mǒxi muſubi means negotiating or arranging something verbally with another person. The printed u, unda principal forms remain unchanged.
[c1-l041 >] *cearſe com alguem de palaura. ¶* Yenpen
[c1-l041 note] This completes negotiating verbally with someone and begins Yenpen, the marriage-arrangement example continued on the next row. The paragraph mark is part of the printed line.
[c1-l042 >] uo mǒximuſubu. *Negocear algũs deſpo-*
[c1-l042 note] Yenpen uo mǒximuſubu means arranging a marriage or betrothal by negotiation. The Portuguese ends at deſpo-, whose continuation belongs to the next physical row.
[c1-l043 >] *ſorios, ou caſamento de palaura.*
[c1-l043 note] The continuation completes deſpoſorios, followed by marriage arranged verbally. The first vowel is o, correcting OCR ſarios; the historical spelling is not modernized.
[c1-l044] Mǒxin. Midarina cocoro. *Coração deſorde-*
[c1-l044 note] Mǒxin, explained by Midarina cocoro, is a disordered or troubled heart. The damaged middle of Midarina is consistent with that reading; the Portuguese adjective continues below.
[c1-l045 >] *nado, ou perturbado com ruins penſamintos.*
[c1-l045 note] The heart is described as disturbed by bad thoughts. The scan reads perturbado without the OCR's extra syllable and internal gap; the anomalous penſamintos is retained rather than changed to pensamentos.
[c1-l046] Mǒxinadame, uru, eta. *Aplacar cõ palauras.*
[c1-l046 note] Mǒxinadame means soothing or appeasing through words. The abbreviation cõ is preserved, and the Portuguese paraphrase distinguishes this from merely reporting speech.
[c1-l047] Moxinarauaxi, ſu, aita. *Correr em pratica.*
[c1-l047 note] This is Moxinarauaxi, an expression being current in speech, illustrated at the top of column 2.
