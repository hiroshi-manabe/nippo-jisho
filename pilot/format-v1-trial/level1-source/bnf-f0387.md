[c1-l009 note] Completes the definition of qexxo no mono: goods taken by the treasury or confiscated. Fiſco is the fiscal authority, not another Japanese expression.
[c1-l010] Qexxut. Sugurebito. *Homem afamado,*
[c1-l010 note] Kesshutsu is explained by sugurebito, an outstanding person. The bare final t is the dictionary’s checked-syllable notation; the Portuguese first calls the person renowned.
[c1-l011 >] *ou eſtremado. ¶* Qexxutno gacuxǒ.

[c1-l012 >] *Grande letrado.*

[c1-l013] Qeyaburi, u, utta. *Romper, ou quebrar cõ*

[c1-l014 >] *os pees.*

[c1-l015] Qeyaqi. *O paßar pollo fogo, ou chamuſcar a*

[c1-l018] Qeyaqi. *Aruore aßi chamada.*

[c1-l019] Qezzume. *Eſporão de galo.*

[c1-l020] Qezzuri, u, utta. *Aparar com faca, laurar*
[c1-l020 note] Kezuru means shaving or working a surface. The scan supplies two t types in utta and an initial l in laurar, the historical spelling of working or hewing wood.
[c1-l021 >] *madeira com enxò, acepilhar, &c. ¶* Ca-

[c1-l024 >] *liuro, rol, &c.*

[c1-l025 note] Kezuritatetsuru is glossed as planing thoroughly or completely. The principal forms are preserved rather than converted into a modern conjugation.

[c1-l026 note] Completes “plane entirely,” then begins an example about a freshly planed pillar. The final fa- belongs to faxira on the next physical row.

[c1-l030 >] *composto dos buracos da madeira como de le-*

[c1-l031 >] *tras que deixou o bicho comendoa.*
[c1-l031 note] Completes letras and attributes the marks to a creature eating the wood. This closes the example immediately before the new alphabetical section.

[c1-l032 initial=2] QI. i, Go. *Iogo de muitas pedrinhas que*
[c1-l032 note] Ki, equated with go, names the board game played with many small stones. The scan has an enlarged Q followed by capital I, not OCR M; the initial spans two physical rows.
[c1-l033 >] *mudão em hum taboleiro riſcado.* S.
[c1-l033 note] The description continues with moving stones on a ruled board. It is transcribed literally rather than revised to match modern descriptions of go; the terminal S. is a roman written-register label.
[c1-l034 >] Qi. Vma. *Caualo. ¶* Fai qini tçu
[c1-l034 note] A different ki is explained as uma, a horse. The example says that flies attach to the horse; tçu continues as tçuqu without a printed line-end hyphen.
[c1-l035 >] qu. *As moſcas peganſe no caualo.* S.
[c1-l035 note] Completes tçuqu and translates it as flies fastening themselves to the horse. The final S. again marks written usage and is roman.
[c1-l036] Qi. *Coração, eſpiritos vitaes, ou vigor do co-*
[c1-l036 note] Ki now denotes the heart, vital spirits, or inner vigor. The headword is Qi, not the OCR’s Qu; co- begins Portuguese coração.
[c1-l037 >] *ração. ¶* Qiga ſanzuru, l, qiuo ſanzu-
[c1-l037 note] The alternatives ki ga sanzuru and ki wo sanzuru describe relieving or dispersing pent-up feelings. Latin l introduces the alternative; the second verb continues below.
[c1-l038 >] ru. *Deſabafar o coração. ¶* Qiga tçuca-
[c1-l038 note] Completes sanzuru and glosses it as unburdening the heart. Deſabafar is one printed word, not OCR Deſaba far; the next example begins ki ga tsukaruru.
[c1-l039 >] ruru. *Eſtar muito canſado, & abafado do*
[c1-l039 note] Completes tsukaruru, becoming exhausted; the Portuguese speaks of severe tiredness and oppression of the heart. Its final do leads into the next row.
[c1-l040 >] *coração. ¶* Qiga tçumaru. *Idem.*
[c1-l040 note] Completes the preceding gloss and gives ki ga tsumaru with the same sense of being inwardly oppressed or constricted.
[c1-l041 >] *¶* Qiga tçuqu. *Tornar em ſi o que eſmo-*
[c1-l041 note] Ki ga tsuku is recovering one’s senses after fainting. The translation’s eſmo- continues into eſmoreceo on the following row.
[c1-l042 >] *receo. ¶* Qiga tçuquru. *Eſtar muito de*
[c1-l042 note] Completes the fainting example, then gives ki ga tsukuru for having one’s inner strength exhausted. The printed de continues into debilitado without a visible hyphen.
[c1-l043 >] *bilitado, & exhausto das forças interiores.*
[c1-l043 note] Completes debilitado and explains depletion of the inner powers. Exhausto is preserved in its historical spelling and provisional st classification.
[c1-l044 >] *¶* Qiniataru. *Dar no coração. ¶* Qi-
[c1-l044 note] Ki ni ataru is glossed as striking or affecting the heart. The final Qi- begins the separate expression ki ni kakaru below. The narrow gap within Qiniataru is not decisive enough to overturn the aligned compact OCR spacing; that chosen spacing remains a minor caveat.
[c1-l045 >] ni cacaru. *Fazer eſcrupulo, ou tocar no co-*
[c1-l045 note] Ki ni kakaru means causing concern or weighing on the mind. The Portuguese coração is divided across the column boundary and finishes at the top of column 2.
