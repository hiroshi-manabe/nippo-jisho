[c2-l004] Ganpi. *Hũa flor vermelha. ¶ Item, A*

[c2-l008] Ganqe. Meno vazzurai. *Doença dos olhos.*
[c2-l008 note] Ganqe denotes an eye disease, paraphrased by me no wazurai. The Portuguese reads olhos, not OCR obos; the l and h are present despite their close contact.
[c2-l009] Ganqen. Manaco miru. *O ver dos olhos.*
[c2-l009 note] Ganqen is the act of seeing with the eyes. The Japanese explanatory phrase is retained in its printed order rather than recast as modern syntax.

[c2-l013] Ganquǒ. Manacono ficari. *Claridade, ou*

[c2-l015] Ganri. Manacono vchi. *Dentro no olho.*
