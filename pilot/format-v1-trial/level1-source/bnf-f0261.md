[c1-l001] Ichiracu. Fitotçuno tanoximi. *Hum goſto,*
[c1-l001 note] Ichiracu is explained as one pleasure or enjoyment, matching Fitotçuno tanoximi. The Portuguese begins a pair of near-synonyms completed below.
[c1-l002 >] *ou prazer.*
[c1-l002 note] Ou prazer completes the gloss as pleasure or delight; this short continuation is a separate physical line.
[c1-l003] Ichiracu ſacu. *Determinação, ou ſentença ſo-*
[c1-l003 note] Ichiracu sacu denotes settling or determining a dispute. The Portuguese divides sobre at the visible line-end hyphen.
[c1-l004 >] *bre algũa demanda. ¶* Ichiracu ſacuno
[c1-l004 note] The determination concerns a lawsuit or claim; Ichiracu sacuno begins a temporal expression continued with aida below.
[c1-l005 >] aida. *Em quanto ſe conclue, & auerigua*
[c1-l005 note] Aida completes 'while the matter is being settled'. The scan supports conclue and an ampersand, rather than OCR condue; the Portuguese also mentions investigating or ascertaining the claim.
[c1-l006 >] *a demanda.*
[c1-l006 note] A demanda supplies the object of conclue and auerigua above: the dispute being resolved, not a new entry.
[c1-l007] Ichiran. *Hũa perturbação, ou reuolta.*
[c1-l007 note] This Ichiran is a disturbance or revolt, distinct from the homophonous viewing term in the next entry.
[c1-l008] Ichiran. *Hũa viſta. Vt,* Ichiran tçuca-
[c1-l008 note] The second Ichiran means one look or inspection. Its example begins the deferential verb tçucamatçuru across the physical line break.
[c1-l009 >] matçuru. *Ver de hũa vez, ou leuemẽte.*
[c1-l009 note] Matçuru completes the verb in Ichiran tçucamatçuru; the Portuguese explains taking one look or looking cursorily. The abbreviation tilde in leuemẽte is retained.
[c1-l010] Ichirei. Fitotçuno rei. *Hũa corteſia, ou*
[c1-l010 note] Ichirei, explained by Fitotçuno rei, is one act of courtesy or reverence; the gloss continues below.
[c1-l011 >] *reuerencia. Vt,* Ichireiuo mǒſu. *Fazer*
[c1-l011 note] Ichireiuo mǒſu illustrates making a respectful salutation. The Japanese mǒſu is upright, while Vt and Fazer belong to the italic apparatus.
[c1-l012 >] *hũa reuerencia, ou corteſia.*
[c1-l012 note] Completes Fazer with one reverence or courtesy, confirming the salutation sense rather than a numerical rule.
[c1-l013] Ichiri. Fitotçuno cotouari. *Hũa rezão, ou*
[c1-l013 note] Ichiri is one reason or argument. The Japanese explanatory cotouari has r, not the OCR's t, consistent with the Portuguese rezão.
[c1-l014 >] *arrezoamento.*
[c1-l014 note] Arrezoamento completes the alternative gloss as reasoning or an argument. Its historical spelling is preserved.
[c1-l015] Ichirin. Fitotçuno va. *Hũa roda. ¶ Itẽ,*
[c1-l015 note] Ichirin is first defined as one wheel or circle; the following Item introduces a transferred use of that circular image.
[c1-l016 >] *Per met,* L*ũa, ou ſol. Vt,* Ichirin tacacu
[c1-l016 note] Per met introduces the moon or sun as a disc. Ichirin tacacu begins the example about that disc being high in the sky.
[c1-l017 >] cacareba, bancocu vonajicu miru. *Eſtã-*
[c1-l017 note] Cacareba completes the condition 'when it hangs high'; bancocu vonajicu miru says that all countries see it alike. Eſtã- begins the Portuguese rendering on this row.
[c1-l018 >] *do o ſol, ou lũa alta, de todos os Reinos o*
[c1-l018 note] Do completes Eſtãdo, describing the sun or moon high above and visible from all kingdoms; the final o anticipates the object of vem below.
[c1-l019 >] *vem da meſma maneira. S.*
[c1-l019 note] Completes the comparison: people see it in the same way. Final S is the upright written-register label, not part of the italic sentence.
[c1-l020] Ichiriǒxo. *Hum, ou dous lugares.*
[c1-l020 note] Ichiriǒxo means one or two places. The internal r corrects OCR t; the marked o belongs to the ryo element, not a separate malformed syllable.
[c1-l021] Ichiriqi. Fitotçuno chicara. *Hũa força, ou*
[c1-l021 note] Ichiriqi is one person's strength or resources, explicitly equated with Fitotçuno chicara. The headword begins with a capital I in the scan.
[c1-l022 >] *força de hum ſoo. ¶* Ichiriqide xita co-
[c1-l022 note] The gloss specifies the strength of one alone; Ichiriqide xita co- begins 'a thing done by one's own resources'. Two o types are visible in ſoo.
[c1-l023 >] togia. *He couſa que eu fiz com minhas forças,*
[c1-l023 note] Togia completes cotogia, 'it is a thing that ...'. The Portuguese makes the subject first person: something I did with my own strength.
[c1-l024 >] *ou com meu poder, & de meus criados.*
[c1-l024 note] The explanatory alternatives include the person's power and that of his servants. This qualifies 'alone' as one's own household resources, not necessarily unassisted physical effort.
[c1-l025] Ichiriǔ manbai. *Por hum dez mil. Vt,*
[c1-l025 note] Ichiriǔ manbai expresses ten thousandfold return from a single grain. The Portuguese hum has the same italic h shape seen elsewhere, not OCR bum.
[c1-l026 >] Ichiriǔ manbaino miuo toru. *Receber*
[c1-l026 note] Ichiriǔ manbaino miuo toru illustrates receiving the fruit of that multiplication. The Japanese example repeats the headword's marked u.
[c1-l027 >] *fruito, ou proueito de mil, ou dez mil por hũ*
[c1-l027 note] The Portuguese states a yield or benefit of a thousand or ten thousand for one; the compressed hũ belongs with grão on the next row.
[c1-l028 >] *grão de ſemente.*
[c1-l028 note] Grão de ſemente completes the image of one seed producing a great return. The source's singular grain remains explicit.
[c1-l029] Ichiro. Fitotçuno michi. *Hum caminho. S.*
[c1-l029 note] Ichiro is one road or way, as the Japanese michi and Portuguese caminho agree. Preserve the capital I and the upright written-register S.
[c1-l030] Ichirô. Fitotçuno yagura. *Hum baileu, ou*
[c1-l030 note] Ichirô is a single elevated structure, explained by yagura and Portuguese baileu; its circumflex distinguishes this from the unmarked road entry above.
[c1-l031 >] *ſobrado alto como torreſinha, &c.*
[c1-l031 note] The continuation describes a high storey or platform like a small tower. The scan retains the n in torreſinha, absent from the OCR draft.
[c1-l032] Ichirui. Fitotçuno taguy. *Hũa eſpecia,*
[c1-l032 note] Ichirui means one kind or class, with taguy as its Japanese explanation. The comma after eſpecia connects to the alternative on the next line.
[c1-l033 >] *ou laya.*
[c1-l033 note] Ou laya completes the classifying gloss as a kind or sort. It belongs to Ichirui above, not the house entry below.
[c1-l034] Ichiv. Fitotçuno iye. *Hũa caſa.*
[c1-l034 note] Ichiv here means one house, with iye making the sense explicit. The worn initial is I, not OCR A; the final v is retained as printed.
[c1-l035] Ichiv. Fitoame. *Hũa chuua.*
[c1-l035 note] The next Ichiv is homophonous but means one rainfall or shower, explained by Fitoame and chuua.
[c1-l036] Ichiun. Fitotçuno cumo. *Hũa nuuem.*
[c1-l036 note] Ichiun means one cloud, with cumo confirming the noun. The capital initial and nuuem's historical spelling are preserved.
[c1-l037] Ichiuǒ. Fitotçu yucu. *Hũa vez. Vt,* Ichi
[c1-l037 note] Ichiuǒ is glossed as once, with Fitotçu yucu supplying its Japanese explanation. The example begins Ichi at the right edge without an added hyphen.
[c1-l038 >] uǒ mǒsǒ. *Direi, ou falarei hũa vez.*
[c1-l038 note] Uǒ completes the divided example headword; mǒsǒ means 'I shall say'. Enlargement shows a worn round s between the marked vowels, not OCR e and not a tall long-s type.
[c1-l039] Ichiyacu. Fitotçuno cuſuri. *Hũa mezinha.*
[c1-l039 note] This Ichiyacu is one medicine or remedy, explicitly equated with cuſuri. The medical example continues below.
[c1-l040 >] *Vt,* Ichiyacuuo xubiǒni atǒru. *Dar hũa*
[c1-l040 note] Ichiyacuuo xubiǒni atǒru illustrates applying one remedy to many illnesses. The marked vowels in the Japanese are retained rather than normalized from modern pronunciation.
[c1-l041 >] *mezinha pera todas as infirmidades.*
[c1-l041 note] Completes Dar hũa mezinha as giving one remedy for all illnesses; this is the dictionary's example, not an endorsement of a medical claim.
[c1-l042] Ichiyacu. Fitotçuno yacu. *Hum officio.*
[c1-l042 note] A different Ichiyacu means one office or duty, as yacu and officio indicate. It is independent of the medicine entry.
[c1-l043] Ichiyacu. Fitotçuno yacuſocu. *Hũa pro-*
[c1-l043 note] The third Ichiyacu denotes one promise or agreement, made explicit by yacuſocu. The Portuguese pro- continues on the next physical row.
[c1-l044 >] *meßa, ou concerto.*
[c1-l044 note] Meßa completes promeßa, followed by concerto in the sense of an agreement. The printed beta-like doubled-s form is retained from the aligned draft.
[c1-l045] Ichiye. Fitotçuno coromo. *Hum veſtido.*
[c1-l045 note] Ichiye is one garment, corresponding to coromo. The Portuguese veſtido describes clothing, not a household.
[c1-l046] Ichiyei. Fitotçuno ſacaye. *Hũa proſperi-*
[c1-l046 note] Ichiyei denotes prosperity or flourishing, with ſacaye as its Japanese explanation. The entry has a capital I and divides proſperidade across the row boundary.
[c1-l047 >] *dade. Vt,* Ichiyei ichiracu mano atari.
[c1-l047 note] Dade completes proſperidade. The example juxtaposes flourishing and falling away as something plainly before one's eyes; its Portuguese explanation begins at the top of the second column.
