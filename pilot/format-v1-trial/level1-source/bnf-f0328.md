[c1-l023] Mǒgan. *Cego.*

[c1-l045] Mogiri, u, itta. *Idem.*

[c1-l047 note] Mogui introduces taking or wrenching something by force. The final i in the stem is visible, correcting OCR Mogu; for- continues as força at the top of column 2.
