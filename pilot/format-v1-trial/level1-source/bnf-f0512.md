[c1-l001] Tomodachi. *Matalotes, ou companheiros.*
[c1-l001 note] Tomodachi denotes companions or comrades; the Portuguese pairs travelling or shipboard companions with the broader companheiros.
[c1-l002] Tomodochi. P. i, Tomodachi. *Idem.*
[c1-l002 note] Tomodochi is the poetic form referred to Tomodachi, with Idem retaining the same meaning. The scan has a comma after the explanatory i. The isolated P. keeps the baseline roman setting; its slight slant is not decisive against neighbouring roman types.
[c1-l003] Tomodori. l, Tomotori. *Remeiro que re-*
[c1-l003 note] Tomodori and the alternative Tomotori name a rower stationed near the rudder; re- begins rema on the following line.
[c1-l004 >] *ma junto ao leme.*
[c1-l004 note] Completes rema: the rower rows beside the rudder. This short continuation belongs to the nautical entry, before the homonymous bird entry.
[c1-l005] Tomodori. *Paßaros de hũa meſma eſpecie que*
[c1-l005 note] Tomodori here denotes birds of the same species; the description continues with their flying together. The homonymous entry is distinct from the preceding rower.
[c1-l006 >] *vão juntamente voando.*
[c1-l006 note] The birds fly together, completing the explanation of Tomodori. The printed vão and voando retain the source’s spelling and tilde.
[c1-l007] Tomogara. *Os, ou aquelles, peßoa, ou gente.*
[c1-l007 note] Tomogara refers to those people or that company, matching aquelles, peßoa, and gente. The Portuguese list is retained without imposing a single modern equivalent.
[c1-l008] Tomomo. *Coxas das pernas da bãda de fora.*
[c1-l008 note] Tomomo is glossed as the outer sides of the thighs, in contrast to Vchimomo below. The printed short headword is retained rather than expanded to a modern expected compound.
[c1-l009 >] *¶* Vchimomo. *As da parte de dentro.*
[c1-l009 note] Vchimomo gives the contrasting inner thighs. As refers back to coxas, so this is an interior-versus-exterior contrast within the anatomical explanation.
[c1-l010] Tômon. Figaxino mon. *Porta da rua pera*

[c1-l011 >] *o Oriente.*
[c1-l011 note] Oriente completes the eastward direction of the gate. The initial is the round capital O, not the OCR Griente.
[c1-l012] Tomonai, ǒ, ǒta. *A companhar a alguem.*
[c1-l012 note] Tomonai, with its supplied principal parts, means accompanying someone. The visibly separated A companhar is preserved as printed instead of joined to modern acompanhar.

[c1-l014 note] Monauǒ completes the divided verb in the recommendation to associate with one’s equals. Fab. supplies the citation label; Os homens ande begins the Portuguese explanation, retaining the printed contraction.
[c1-l015 >] *andar, ou tratar com os de ſua laya.*
[c1-l015 note] Completes the advice: people should go about or associate with those of their own sort. Laya expresses kind or class in this explanation.
[c1-l016] Tomoni. *Com, ou de companhia, juntamente,*

[c1-l018] Tomoni motte. *Idem.*

[c1-l020 >] *chegado ao leme.*

[c1-l037 >] *ou commendação por algum de funto.*
[c1-l037 note] Completes the noun definition with commendation for a deceased person. The physical continuation and historical de funto spacing are retained.
[c1-l038] Tomurai, ǒ, ǒta. *Viſitar a alguem, ou per-*

[c1-l039 >] *guntar por elle, &c.*

[c1-l040] Tômuxiro. *Esteira de rota, ou vimes.*

[c1-l041] Tonaca. *Meo do golfão.*
[c1-l041 note] Tonaca is the middle of the gulf or open sea, glossed by Meo do golfão. The concise geographical definition stands as its own entry.
[c1-l042] Ton-ai: *poſto que ſe pronuncia,* Tonnai. *O*
[c1-l042 note] Ton-ai denotes absorbed attachment; the Portuguese explicitly says it is pronounced Tonnai. The colon and the letter sequence pronuncia correct the OCR punctuation and pronuteia; the embedded pronunciation is roman.

[c1-l044] Tônan. Figaxi, minami. *Oriente, & Sul.*
[c1-l044 note] Tônan combines Figaxi and minami, east and south. The two Japanese directions explain the compound; they are ordinary synonyms, not Portuguese-embedded terms for typeface tagging.
[c1-l045] Tǒnan. Catanano nangui. *O ſer cortado à*
[c1-l045 note] Tǒnan concerns suffering by the sword, explained as Catanano nangui. [c1-l047 note] Completes the misfortune as being killed by the sword. This is body text ending column 1, independent of the right-column catchword below.
