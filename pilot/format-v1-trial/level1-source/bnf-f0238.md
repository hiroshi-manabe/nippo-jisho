[c1-l001] Guianet. *Sezoens de febres ſẽ frios.*
[c1-l001 note] Gyanetsu is glossed as recurring fever without chills. The weakened n is read from its surviving strokes and the headword sequence, corroborated by the supplied NINJAL index; it is not Cuiaret.

[c1-l003] Guibôxi. *Hũa erua, ou fula que nace della.*
[c1-l003 note] Gibooshi names an herb or the flower arising from it. Fula is retained as printed, not replaced with flor or folha merely to resemble modern Portuguese.
[c1-l004] Guibôxi. *Maçanetas de pao, ou latão que es-*
[c1-l004 note] A separate gibooshi denotes wooden or brass finials. The circumflex matches the preceding headword, while es- begins the verb estão on the next row.
[c1-l005 >] *tão por remate dos balauſtes, daſpontes, &c.*
[c1-l005 note] The finials cap balusters on bridges and similar structures. The tight daſpontes and historical balauſtes are retained rather than normalized.
[c1-l006] Guichǒ. *Cajado de jugar a chòca ao modo de*
[c1-l006 note] Gichoo is a stick for a Japanese ball game, compared with Portuguese choca. The grave in chòca and caron on the headword are separate graphic details.
[c1-l007 >] *Iapão. ¶* Guichǒde tamauo vtçu. *Dar*

[c1-l009] Guidarin. i, Chauſu. *Mo de moer chà.*
[c1-l009 note] Gidarin is equated with chausu, a millstone for grinding tea. The scan supports the long s in Chauſu; the Portuguese Mo and chà are retained as printed.
[c1-l010] Guidatte. *Modo deſe agaſtar, ou enfadar*
[c1-l010 note] Gidatte describes taking offence or becoming irritated. The damaged double t is supported by the repeated example below and the supplied lexical index, rather than by modernizing the word.
[c1-l011 >] *em couſas que não ha pera que ſe agastar. Vt,*
[c1-l011 note] The irritation concerns things that give no proper reason for anger. Preserve the printed short s in agastar despite the long-s form in the preceding line.
[c1-l012 >] Guidatte monouoyǔ fitogia. *Homem*
[c1-l012 note] The example describes a person who speaks in an unnecessarily affronted manner. Guidatte and the joined monouoyǔ correct OCR Gaidatte while retaining the impression's word division.
[c1-l013 >] *que ſe a gasta, ou perturba por qualquer couſa,*

[c1-l014 note] Alternatively the person takes unnecessary offence or insists on misplaced dignity. Deſneceßario retains the ligature and explains the excessive nature of the reaction.
[c1-l015] Guifei. *Soldados, ou gente de guerra. ¶* Gui-
[c1-l015 note] Gihei denotes soldiers or a body of fighting men. Gui- begins the repeated headword in the example on the next row.

[c1-l042 note] The sheet may be silver or gold and supports fragrant substances such as aguila. The source describes its use; no specific modern mineral identification is imposed on gin.

[c1-l046] Guinan. Xirocaneno cura. *Sella de caualo*

[c1-l047 >] *prateada.*
[c1-l047 note] Prateada completes the description of the horse's saddle as silvered. It is body text, not a catchword or page signature.
