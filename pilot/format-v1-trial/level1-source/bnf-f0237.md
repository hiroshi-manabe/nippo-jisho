[c1-l001] Gueſui. *Hum vaſo que ſerue no Chanoyu pera*
[c1-l001 note] Gesui denotes a tea-ceremony vessel for discarded water; the next lines extend the name to its contents. The final ui is visible, correcting Gueſu; Chanoyu occurs inside the Portuguese explanation.
[c1-l002 >] *vazar nelle agoa: mas mais propriamente ſe*
[c1-l002 note] The vessel receives poured-out water. Nelle and the colon after agoa are visible; the following contrast introduces the more specific sense of the headword.
[c1-l003 >] *toma pella agoa que esta neſte vaſo.*
[c1-l003 note] The word is more properly applied to the water in this vessel. Esta is retained without supplying a modern accent or changing the historical spelling pella.
[c1-l004] Gueta. *Hũs chiripos feitos ao modo de Iapão*
[c1-l004 note] Geta are described by comparison with Japanese-style low footwear. The weak nasal marks and damaged country-name are read as Hũs and Iapão; the gloss does not warrant a modernized name for the footwear.
[c1-l005 >] *muito baixos.*
[c1-l005 note] Muito baixos completes the description of the geta as very low. The marks after the period are stray ink rather than an additional lexical item.
[c1-l006] Guetẽ. i, Tenyori cudaru. *Decer do ceo. Bur.*
[c1-l006 note] Geten is explained by ten yori kudaru, descending from heaven. The visible final label is retained as Bur., without silently regularizing it to the more familiar Buddhist abbreviation Bup.
[c1-l007] Guetjen. Tçuqino maye. *Diante da lũa.*
[c1-l007 note] Guetjen is paraphrased as tsuki no mae, before the moon. The printed t before j is retained as part of the historical romanization rather than expanded within the transcription.
[c1-l008 >] Guetquǒ. i, Tçuqino ficari. *Claridade*

[c1-l009 >] *da lũa.*
[c1-l009 note] Da lũa completes claridade, the moon's brightness. It belongs to Guetquǒ and remains a separate physical row.
[c1-l010] Guexacubara. i, Sobabara. *Filho bastardo.*
[c1-l010 note] Geshakubara is equated with sobabara and glossed as an illegitimate child. The initial is G, not OCR C; the following example explains the family relationship.
[c1-l011 >] *Vt,* Guexacubarano co. *Filho ilegitimo,*

[c1-l012 note] The child is not the son of the man's own wife. The impression appears to omit l in fiho; that printed anomaly is retained rather than silently repairing it to filho.
[c1-l013] Guexen. *Baixeza, ou homem baixo.*
[c1-l013 note] Gesen means baseness or a person of low standing. Baixo belongs entirely to the italic Portuguese gloss, not to an isolated roman a as in the OCR styling.
[c1-l014] Guexenna. *Couſa baixa, ou homem baixo.*
[c1-l014 note] Gesenna gives the corresponding adjectival expression for something or someone low. The damaged internal letter in the final baixo is read with the matching form immediately above.

[c1-l015 note] This gesetsu entry concerns something low or vile, or baseness itself. Keep it distinct from the separate homographic entry about popular speech below.
[c1-l016] Guexet. *O falar, ou contar de gente baixa.*
[c1-l016 note] The second gesetsu denotes the speech or accounts of common people. Its separate headword is printed and should not be merged with the preceding entry.

[c1-l042] Guexocu. *Officio baixo. ¶* Guexocu ſu-
