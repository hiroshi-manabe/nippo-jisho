[c1-l001 >] *ſe deixa em aberto por reuerencia da peßoa a q̃*
[c1-l001 note] Continues f412's account of a blank portion of a letter, left out of respect for the addressee. A fold obscures part of por; the visible residue and continuous phrase support that reading rather than OCR za. The crop is enlarged to include the sloping right end.
[c1-l002 >] *ſe eſcreue. ¶* Raixiuo voqu. *Deixar*
[c1-l002 note] Completes the reference to the person being addressed. Raixiuo voqu begins the example about leaving respectful blank space; its spelling contrasts with printed Ranxi on f412.
[c1-l003 >] *este papel em aberto, & limpo no principio*
[c1-l003 note] Translates leaving the paper blank and clean at the beginning. The phrase continues to specify the beginning of the letter; no missing material is invented across the damaged fold.
[c1-l004 >] *da carta.*
[c1-l004 note] Completes the explanation of blank space at the beginning of a letter. This finishes the entry carried over from the previous page.
[c1-l005] Raixi, ſuru, ita. *Venerar, ou reuerenciar.*
[c1-l005 note] Raixi gives principal forms for venerating or showing reverence. The abbreviated ita is preserved rather than expanded to a full inflected form in the transcription.
[c1-l006] Raixǒ. *Vida futura, ou a outra vida.* S.
[c1-l006 note] Raixǒ means the future or other life. The real space in outra vida is restored, and the final S. is an upright usage label.
[c1-l007] Raixǔ. Qitaru aqi. *Outono que vem.* S.
[c1-l007 note] Raixǔ denotes the coming autumn. The final vowel has a caron and the explanatory aqi supplies the season; S. is upright.
[c1-l008] Raixun. Qitaru faru. *A primauera que*
[c1-l008 note] Raixun denotes the coming spring. The Japanese verb reads Qitaru, not Qitaiu; primauera is continuous rather than the OCR's internally divided word.
[c1-l009 >] *vem.* S.
[c1-l009 note] Completes the coming-spring definition. The final S. is a register label following the short Portuguese continuation.
[c1-l010] Raiyu. i, Yurai. *Cauſa, origem, &c.* S.
[c1-l010 note] Raiyu is equated with Yurai and glossed as cause or origin. The scan clearly supplies initial R and capital Y, correcting OCR Faiyu and rurai.
[c1-l011] Ramǒ. i, Ami. *Rede.*
[c1-l011 note] Ramǒ is glossed by Japanese ami and Portuguese rede, a net. The caron in the headword remains distinct from the plain vowels of the synonym.
[c1-l012] Ran. Midare. *Perturbação.* Ranuo vo-
[c1-l012 note] Ran denotes disorder or disturbance. The example begins Ranuo vocoſu, causing a disturbance, with its verb divided across the physical rows.
[c1-l013 >] coſu. *Cauſar algũa perturbação. ¶* Ran-
[c1-l013 note] Completes vocoſu and its translation, then begins the next example with Ran-. Both physical divisions are retained rather than merged into continuous lines.
[c1-l014 >] ga deqita. *Aconteceo, ou aleuantouſe per*
[c1-l014 note] Completes Ranga deqita, a disturbance arising. The Portuguese begins perturbação at the edge without a secure hyphen, so none is supplied.
[c1-l015 >] *turbação de guerra. ¶* Ranga ideqitaru.
[c1-l015 note] Completes warlike disturbance and gives a closely related Japanese example with ideqitaru. The latter form remains distinct from deqita above.
[c1-l016 >] *Idem. ¶* Ranga iqu. *Auer perturba-*
[c1-l016 note] Idem refers back to the previous example; Ranga iqu is another expression for the occurrence of disturbance. The initial is R rather than OCR B, and the Portuguese definition continues below.
[c1-l017 >] *ção deguerra.*
[c1-l017 note] Completes disturbance of war. The printed joining deguerra is preserved rather than silently separated.
[c1-l018] Ranbiǒxi. *Certo modo de tanger o tabaquinho.*
[c1-l018 note] Ranbiǒxi denotes a particular way of playing the small drum. The Portuguese noun tabaquinho is one word; the apparent OCR space was introduced by damage.
[c1-l019] Ranbǒ. Midare ſamataguru. *O ſaquear, ou*
[c1-l019 note] Ranbǒ concerns disorderly interference, glossed specifically as pillaging or robbery. The next row completes the second Portuguese infinitive and gives an example.
[c1-l020 >] *roubar. Vt,* Ranbǒ ſuru.
[c1-l020 note] Completes robbing and gives Ranbǒ suru. The repeated headword has n, not the OCR's u; the initial damaged Portuguese letters remain recoverable as roubar.
[c1-l021] Ranbu. Midare mǒ. *Folguedo de ballar, &*
[c1-l021 note] Ranbu is a performance involving dance and song, with a Japanese paraphrase about dancing. The headword is Ranbu, not OCR Babu; the dance verb's caron is retained.
[c1-l022 >] *cantar. ¶* Ranbu ſuru. *Fazer eſte fol-*
[c1-l022 note] Completes dance and song, then illustrates performing this entertainment. The visible Portuguese verb is Fazer, not OCR Farzer, and folguedo is divided across the rows.
[c1-l023 >] *guedo, &c.*
[c1-l023 note] Completes folguedo and its open-ended qualification. This short row belongs to the Ranbu example.
[c1-l024] Rancan. Vobaxima. *Balauſtes de ponte, ou*
[c1-l024 note] Rancan and Vobaxima refer to a balustrade or railing, here on a bridge. The historical Balauſtes is retained without adding an expected r.
[c1-l025 >] *de varandas, &c.*
[c1-l025 note] Adds verandas as another location for the balustrades. It completes the examples of where Rancan occurs.
[c1-l026] Randa. Vmi vocotaru. *Deſcuido, ou negli-*
[c1-l026 note] Randa means neglect or lack of care. The Japanese explanatory phrase is read with that sense; Portuguese negligencia is divided at the edge.
[c1-l027 >] *gencia.* S.
[c1-l027 note] Completes negligencia. The upright S. marks written usage and is not italicized with the final word.
[c1-l028] Randat. *Perturbação, ou deſordem de vida,*
[c1-l028 note] Randat describes disorder in one's life or conduct. The final t is part of the headword and remains visible in its derived example below.
[c1-l029 >] *cuſtumes, &c. ¶* Randatna fito. *Ho-*
[c1-l029 note] Adds conduct or customs and describes a disorderly person. The scan includes t in Randatna, omitted by OCR Randana; Ho- begins homem.
[c1-l030 >] *mem deſordenado na vida.*
[c1-l030 note] Completes a person whose life is disorderly. This is the translation of Randatna fito, not a new evaluative headword.
[c1-l031] Rango. Midareno nochi. *Depois da pertur-*
[c1-l031 note] Rango means after the disturbance, as Midareno nochi explicitly states. The initial of Midareno is M, not OCR X; perturbação is divided across the rows.
[c1-l032 >] *bação, ou reuolta da guerra.*
[c1-l032 note] Completes disturbance or upheaval of war. It specifies the event after which Rango places something.
[c1-l033] Rangocu. Midaruru cuni. *Reino perturbado.*
[c1-l033 note] Rangocu describes a kingdom in disorder, matching Midaruru cuni. The headword has n rather than the OCR's u.
[c1-l034] Ranguequi. Midare. *Reuolta, ou pertur-*
[c1-l034 note] Ranguequi is defined as revolt or disturbance, with Midare as its Japanese explanation. The Portuguese word continues on the next physical row.
[c1-l035 >] *bação.*
[c1-l035 note] Completes perturbação, the second gloss of Ranguequi. This short continuation is not an independent entry.
[c1-l036] Ranguicu. Araragui qicu. *Erua cheiroſa aßi*
[c1-l036 note] Ranguicu names a fragrant herb, with Araragui qicu as the Japanese explanation. The scan reads Ranguicu, not OCR Fanguieu, and cheiroſa rather than cheinoſa.
[c1-l037 >] *chamada. Item, A flor desta erua.*
[c1-l037 note] Completes the herb definition and adds its flower as a second sense. The short s in desta is retained as printed.
[c1-l038] Ranguiǒ. Midarini voconǒ. *Obras maas,*
[c1-l038 note] Ranguiǒ concerns improper conduct, explained by Midarini voconǒ. The scan supplies initial M and Portuguese Obras, correcting OCR Xidarini and Gbras.
[c1-l039 >] *torpes, & deſordenadas, ou peccados de tor-*
[c1-l039 note] Qualifies the actions as shameful and disorderly and begins the alternative expression peccados de torpeza. The division tor- continues below.
[c1-l040 >] *peza.*
[c1-l040 note] Completes torpeza in the description of improper or shameful acts. This row belongs to the Ranguiǒ definition.
[c1-l041] Raiguiocu. l, ranqiocu. *Certo modo de tã-*
[c1-l041 note] Raiguiocu has ranqiocu as an alternative form; lowercase l introduces an alternative rather than being a numeral. The gloss begins a manner of playing or singing. The first o of modo is damaged and c-like; modo is the chosen contextual reading, not a silent claim that the glyph is clear.
[c1-l042 >] *ger, ou cantar picado, ou apreßado de quando*
[c1-l042 note] Completes tanger and describes a pointed or hurried manner of singing at intervals. The printed ending is ger, not get, and the beta-like double-s sort in apreßado is retained.
[c1-l043 >] *em quando.*
[c1-l043 note] Completes de quando em quando, specifying intermittent changes in the musical performance. It belongs to Raiguiocu rather than the next entry.
[c1-l044] Ranguiǒjin. *Peßoa deſhonesta.*
[c1-l044 note] Ranguiǒjin denotes a person of improper conduct. The scan has the beta-like double-s sort in Peßoa and reads deſhonesta, not the garbled OCR deſhorefta; the local st ligature is rendered st.
[c1-l045] Ranguiǒna. *Idem. Vt,* Ranguiǒna fito.
[c1-l045 note] Ranguiǒna expresses the same description adjectivally, illustrated by Ranguiǒna fito. Both occurrences retain the caron over o.
[c1-l046] Ranguy. *Estacada que ſe faz nalgum caminho,*
[c1-l046 note] Ranguy denotes a stockade or line of stakes across a route. The initial is R rather than OCR B, and the definition continues through the column break.
[c1-l047 >] *ou paſsagem muito ao comprido pẽra não deixar*
[c1-l047 note] Describes the long barrier across a passage, intended to prevent entry. The words are muito, não, and deixar, not the OCR's maido, nã, and deinar; the locally visible mixed paſsagem is retained.

[c2-l011 >] *roſas, ou flores quando eſtão muitas hũas ſobre*

[c2-l013] Ranmǒ. Midarinaru nozomi. *Maos deſejos,*

[c2-l014 >] *ou deſordenados.* S.

[c2-l016 >] *ſe fazem eſpalhadas, & ſem ordem.*

[c2-l017] Rannhǔ. Midare iru. *Entrar de romania, ou*

Its medial letter looks like n, so romania is retained instead of silently substituting expected romaria; that consonant remains a nonblocking reading caveat.
[c2-l018 >] *de tropel muita gente.* S.

[c2-l019] Rannicu. Tadareta xiximura. *Carne que*

[c2-l022] Ranpat. Midareta cami. *Cabelos ſoltos, &*
