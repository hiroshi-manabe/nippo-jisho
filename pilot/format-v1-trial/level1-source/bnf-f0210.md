[c1-l001 >>] *cipal, & cabeça em algum feito, ou obra;*
[c1-l001 note] Completes principal from f209: the principal person or leader in an affair or deed. The repeated cipal at f209's foot is therefore a catchword. Here the scan ends obra; rather than obras.
[c1-l002 >] *principalmente ruim*
[c1-l002 note] Especially a bad deed: ruim qualifies the preceding feito or obra. This belongs to the honnin definition, not the next heading.
[c1-l003] Fonno. *Couſa propria, ou verdadeira.*
[c1-l003 note] Hon no, 本の, something genuine or true, agrees with propria and verdadeira. Preserve the joined Fonno rather than imposing modern word division.
[c1-l004] Fônô. Voſame tatematçuru. *Pagar algum*
[c1-l004 note] Hōnō, 奉納, respectfully presenting or paying something, glossed osame tatematsuru. Both circumflexes and the historical honorific expression are retained.
[c1-l005 >] *rendimento às Teras, ou varelas. ¶* Fô-
[c1-l005 note] Payment goes to temples or varelas. Teras is embedded Japanese in italic; the example hōnō itasu starts with Fô- at the right.
[c1-l006 >] nô itaſu. *Idem.*
[c1-l006 note] Hōnō itasu, making the offering, completes split Fô-/nô. Idem refers to the preceding temple payment.
[c1-l007] Fonobono. *Adu. P. Modo de amanhecer, ou*
[c1-l007 note] Honobono describes dawn's dim arrival. Adu. and P. are italic usage labels; the reduplication is grammatical, not duplicate OCR.
[c1-l008 >] *abrir amenhaã. ¶* Yono fonobono aqe-
[c1-l008 note] Yo no honobono ake ni, at the first light of dawn. Preserve Portuguese amenhaã with its joined article; the Japanese aqe-/ni crosses rows.
[c1-l009 >] ni mairǒ. *Irei logo em amanhecendo.*
[c1-l009 note] Mairō, I shall go, completes the dawn example. Irei logo em amanhecendo confirms a prospective statement, not an imperative.
[c1-l010] Fonocani. *Eſcaſamente. Vt,* Fonocani
[c1-l010 note] Honoka ni means faintly or indistinctly. Escasamente concerns limited sensory perception here, not merely scarcity of quantity.
[c1-l011 >] miru, l, qiqu. *Ver, ou ouuir eſcaſamen-*
[c1-l011 note] Honoka ni miru or kiku: seeing or hearing faintly. The l marks alternative verbs; qi supplies ki without needing an extra u.
[c1-l012 >] *te, ou mal.*
[c1-l012 note] Completes escasamente, or poorly. The row is entirely Portuguese and needs no Japanese reading hypothesis.
[c1-l013] Fonogurai. *Entre luſco, & fuſco antes de*
[c1-l013 note] Honogurai, dim, matches entre lusco e fusco. Preserve the OCR s allographs: their length does not affect this interpretation.
[c1-l014 >] *bem amanhecer, ou debem anoitecer. ¶* Fo-
[c1-l014 note] Before full daylight or full nightfall. Keep debem joined as printed; the next example starts Fo-/nogurai.
[c1-l015 >] nogurai vchini mairu. *Ir, ou vir neſte*
[c1-l015 note] Honogurai uchi ni mairu means going or coming while it is dim. The gloss allows both directions of mairu here.
[c1-l016 >] *tempo, que nem he eſcuro, nem claro de todo.*
[c1-l016 note] Neither fully dark nor fully light. This qualifies the journey's time and completes its example.
[c1-l017] Fonomeqi, u, eita. *P. Dar a lũa, eſtrela,*
[c1-l017 note] Honomeki, honomeku, honomeita: shining faintly, of moon or stars. Retain the historical inflection list and this literal light sense, rather than only modern 'hint'.
[c1-l018 >] *&c. claridade eſcaſa & hum pouco eſcura.*
[c1-l018 note] Sparse, somewhat dark light. The final a in escura belongs to the italic adjective; OCR's roman a had generated a spurious kana hint.
[c1-l019 >] *Vt,* Cuſano farayori izzuru tçuqiua an-
[c1-l019 note] Kusa no hara yori izzuru tsuki wa: the moon rising from a grassy plain. An-/ba plausibly gives anba, 鞍馬, saddles and horses, as the Portuguese confirms.
[c1-l020 >] bano iyeni fonomeqite, *&c.* Taif. *Li.*
[c1-l020 note] Faint moonlight over saddles, cited from Taiheiki by Taif. Iyeni is problematic: uyeni would give 'above', matching sobre, but the first letter is not sufficiently clear to force that correction. Retain Li. followed by to. as an explanatory label, not Japanese Di.
[c1-l021 >] *to. Alũa que ſahia por entre as eruas eſcaſa-*
[c1-l021 note] The moon was emerging among grasses. Read sahia rather than sabia: the scan and context support sair, not saber. Alũa stays joined as printed.
[c1-l022 >] *mente ſe via, ou reluzia ſobre as ſellas dos*
[c1-l022 note] The light was scarcely visible or shone upon saddles. Sellas has two visible l stems, repairing the shortened OCR selas.
[c1-l023 >] *caualos.*
[c1-l023 note] Dos cavalos completes 'the horses' saddles'. This supports anba in the quotation without independently settling iyeni/uyeni.
[c1-l024] Fônoqi. *Aruore aſsi chamada de que fazem*
[c1-l024 note] Hōnoki, the magnolia used for scabbards. Aruore replaces Aruere; enlarged aſsi has separate long and short s, not the OCR's impossible aßsi sequence.
[c1-l025 >] *as bainhas das Catanas.*
[c1-l025 note] Wood for catanas' scabbards. The borrowed Japanese sword word is italic here and is not made roman merely from earlier pages' practice.
[c1-l026] Fonouo. *Labareda de fogo. ¶* Fono-
[c1-l026 note] Honowo, 炎, a flame. The repeated Fono- continues with woga; uo is part of the historical spelling.
[c1-l027 >] uoga tobichiru. *Saltar, & eſpalharſe a-*
[c1-l027 note] Honowo ga tobichiru, flames leap and scatter. Preserve the printed a- before labareda even though it divides article from noun.
[c1-l028 >] *labareda. ¶* Fonouo moye izzuru. *Sair,*
[c1-l028 note] Honowo moe izzuru, flames burn forth. Initial labareda completes the previous gloss, not this new example.
[c1-l029 >] *ou acenderſe algũa labareda.*
[c1-l029 note] A flame emerges or catches fire. Algũa's tilde position is retained rather than mechanically shifted to the other vowel.
[c1-l030] Fonpô. *Regimento, ou regra que deixa-*
[c1-l030 note] Honpō is an established medical rule or prescription. The circumflex is visible; the verb crosses deixa-/rão.
[c1-l031 >] *rão alguns medicos inſignes eſcrita ſobre algũa*
[c1-l031 note] A regimen recorded by distinguished physicians for an illness. Feminine escrita agrees with regra and is not an error.
[c1-l032 >] *doença. ¶* Cuſurino fonpô. *Regra,*
[c1-l032 note] Kusuri no honpō, an established medicinal rule. This is a recipe sense, distinct from the Buddhist homograph below.
[c1-l033 >] *ou receita dalgũa mezinha eſcrita em liuro*
[c1-l033 note] A remedy's recipe written in a book. Mezinha is historical Portuguese for medicine and is retained.
[c1-l034 >] *antigo.*
[c1-l034 note] Antigo qualifies the book preserving the prescription. This short continuation is body text, not furniture.
[c1-l035] Fonpô. Fonno nori. *Propria lei, ou doc-*
[c1-l035 note] Honpō, 本法, one's proper law or doctrine, hon no nori. Magnification confirms a circumflex rather than OCR caron; NINJAL independently lists Fonpô.
[c1-l036 >] *trina. Bup.*
[c1-l036 note] Completes doctrina and adds Buddhist label Bup. Keep the label italic here rather than importing roman style from another page.
[c1-l037] Fonpu. Motono votto. *Proprio, & legiti-*
[c1-l037 note] Honpu, 本夫, one's proper husband, glossed moto no otto. Votto represents Japanese husband, not a foreign token.
[c1-l038 >] *mo marido. Vt,* Fon ſai fonpu. *Propria*
[c1-l038 note] Honsai honpu pairs wife and husband. Retain the visible gap in Fon ſai rather than normalizing it from the later heading.
[c1-l039 >] *molher, & proprio marido.*
[c1-l039 note] Completes the paired spouses' definition. Historical molher is retained rather than replaced by modern mulher.
[c1-l040] Fonpu. *Saber, & ſer de homem ordinario.*
[c1-l040 note] A different honpu means ordinary human understanding or disposition. This is not the preceding husband sense.
[c1-l041 >] *¶* Fonpuna fitogia. *He homem que não*
[c1-l041 note] Honpu na hito ja, an ordinary sensible person. Restore the clearly printed pilcrow before this example.
[c1-l042 >] *he paruo, nem deſaſiſado.*
[c1-l042 note] Neither foolish nor out of one's senses. Preserve desasisado and its s sequence rather than modernizing the vocabulary.
[c1-l043] Fonpucu. Fonno fara. *Verdadeira, & pro*
[c1-l043 note] Honpuku, 本腹, the principal wife, analyzed as hon no hara. Fonno replaces OCR Bonno, with both visual and Japanese support.
[c1-l044 >] *pria molher. ¶* Fonpucuno cogia. *He*
[c1-l044 note] Honpuku no ko ja identifies a child of the principal wife. Initial pria completes propria without inventing an unseen hyphen above.
[c1-l045 >] *filho legitimo.*
[c1-l045 note] Filho legitimo confirms legitimate birth. This belongs to the preceding child example, not the recovery entry below.
[c1-l046] Fonpucu. Motoni cayeru. *Vide* Fonbucu.
[c1-l046 note] Honpuku meaning recovery is glossed moto ni kaeru and cross-referenced to Fonbucu. Preserve the explicitly documented p/b variation.
[c1-l047] Fonqe. *Linha judiciaria, ou ponto dos que cõ-*
[c1-l047 note] Honke here is an astrological natal line or point, not a main household. Read Linha judiciaria rather than Linhajudiuaria; the following column completes the astrology explanation.

[c2-l001 >>] *ſiderão os aſtrologos em que hum naceo pera*
[c2-l001 note] Astrologers consider the natal point. This continues con-/siderão across columns and is not a new headword.
[c2-l002 >] *por ahi julgarem o ſucceſo, fortuna, inclina-*
[c2-l002 note] Por ahi, by that means, judging events, fortune and inclination. The second letter in ahi is h, not OCR b.
[c2-l003 >] *ção, &c. dalguem. ¶* Fonqeuo toru.
[c2-l003 note] Honke wo toru, assessing the natal figure. Dalguem is one printed word rather than the OCR's dal guem.
[c2-l004 >] *Vendo eſtas linhas, ou pontos conjeiturar ſo-*
[c2-l004 note] Inspecting these lines or points to conjecture about a person. Preserve historical conjeiturar rather than modernizing it.
[c2-l005 >] *bre a vida, cuſtumes, &c dalguem.*
[c2-l005 note] The judgement concerns life and customs. Retain custumes and the printed punctuation rather than regularizing them by expectation.
[c2-l006] Fonqen. *Primeira carta de venda que tinha*
[c2-l006 note] Honken, 本券, an original sale deed held by the purchaser. Carta here means a title document, not an ordinary letter.
[c2-l007 >] *oque comprou as caſas, & a dà alem do.* V-
[c2-l007 note] The buyer passes it on with the uriken when reselling the houses. Upright V- joins riqen on the next row.
[c2-l008 >] riqen, *a aquelle que as compra delle: & ſe he*
[c2-l008 note] Uriken, 売券, is the sale deed handed to the next buyer. Aquelle and delle keep their historical double consonants.
[c2-l009 >] *outra carta mais antigua ſe diz* Coqen.
[c2-l009 note] An older deed is koken, 古券. The Japanese document name is upright within the explanation.
[c2-l010] Fonqi. Motouo xiruſu. *Eſcritura, ou his-*
[c2-l010 note] Honki, 本紀, a record of origins, moto wo shirusu. Preserve the short s in his-/toria rather than altering an allograph from meaning alone.
[c2-l011 >] *toria antigua.*
[c2-l011 note] An ancient history. Antigua remains as printed, despite antigo elsewhere on the page.
[c2-l012] Fonrai. *Principio, ou deſdo principio ate*
[c2-l012 note] Honrai, 本来, from the beginning. Desdo is historical Portuguese, not an OCR mistake requiring expansion.
[c2-l013 >] *agora.*
[c2-l013 note] Até agora, up to now, completes the temporal definition. No Japanese token occurs in this continuation.
[c2-l014] Fonriǒ. Fonno chiguiǒ. *Propria renda,*
[c2-l014 note] Honryō, 本領, one's holding, glossed hon no chigyō. Preserve both long-vowel marks and the guio-type palatalized spelling.
[c2-l015 >] *ou terras, & herdade.*
[c2-l015 note] The holding comprises lands or an estate. Herdade clarifies the property sense rather than an abstract receipt.
[c2-l016] Fonſacu. Fonno ſacu. *Obra do proprio,*
[c2-l016 note] Honsaku, 本作, a genuine work by its maker. Keep upright long s, not NINJAL's normalized short-s headword spelling.
[c2-l017 >] *& affamado official, que não he contrafeita,*
[c2-l017 note] By a renowned craftsman, not counterfeit. Read official with two f stems and joined contrafeita with f, correcting meaning-bearing OCR errors.
[c2-l018 >] *nem arremedada.*
[c2-l018 note] Nor imitated: arremedada completes the authenticity gloss. It is not an independent entry.
[c2-l019] Fonſai. Fonno tçuma. *Propria eſpoſa, ou mo*
[c2-l019 note] Honsai, 本妻, principal spouse, hon no tsuma. The Portuguese mo continues below as molher without an invented hyphen.
[c2-l020 >] *lher legitima, & principal.*
[c2-l020 note] The legitimate and principal wife. Preserve the historical household distinction rather than flattening it to a generic spouse.
[c2-l021] Fonſô. *Agaſalhado. Vt,* Tôzaini fon-
[c2-l021 note] Honsō, 奔走, attentive hospitality. Tōzai ni honsō suru evokes bustling east and west to attend to guests.
[c2-l022 >] ſô ſuru. *Fazer grande agaſalhado andã-*
[c2-l022 note] Completes honsō suru and starts andando. Retain the circumflex in agreement with the printed heading.
[c2-l023 >] *do ſolicito, & mouendoſe compreſſa: no meo*
[c2-l023 note] The host moves solicitously and hurriedly. Compressa represents closely printed com pressa, not a new compound requiring literal interpretation.
[c2-l024 >] *do conuite ſe diz às vezes louuando.* Coto
[c2-l024 note] A banquet guest may praise the host with the following formula. Coto joins nai below, not the OCR's mai.
[c2-l025 >] nai gofonſô degozaru. *Grande agaſalha-*
[c2-l025 note] Koto nai gohonsō de gozaru praises exceptional hospitality. Visible nai repairs mai and makes the Japanese formula intelligible.
[c2-l026 >] *do nos faz o ſenhor, &c.*
[c2-l026 note] The host gives us great hospitality. Senhor is the person praised, not an additional Japanese token.
[c2-l027] Fonſugi. *Vea propria, ou direita, ou cami-*
[c2-l027 note] Honsuji, 本筋, the main or direct channel. Its watercourse sense leads into lineage and doctrinal metaphors.
[c2-l028 >] *nho por onde corre agoa. ¶ Item per met.*
[c2-l028 note] Water runs through the channel; per met. introduces metaphor. Agoa remains in its historical spelling.
[c2-l029 >] *filho legitimo, & morgado que vem por*
[c2-l029 note] A legitimate son and principal heir. Morgado supports the main-line descent sense, not merely any child.
[c2-l030 >] *linha direita. ¶ Item, Arte, ou doctri-*
[c2-l030 note] Linha direita makes direct descent explicit. The next item extends the idea to an art or doctrine.
[c2-l031 >] *na que vem da fonte, ou de ſeu princi-*
[c2-l031 note] A teaching comes from its source. Vem is one printed word, repairing the meaningless OCR gap in ve m.
[c2-l032 >] *pio por dedução ſem miſtura de outras.*
[c2-l032 note] Transmission without mixture of other teachings. Preserve miſtura's allograph rather than changing s solely from a language model's preference.
[c2-l033] Fontacu. Fonno iye. *Propria caſa em com-*
[c2-l033 note] Hontaku, 本宅, one's main house, hon no ie. The comparison with subsidiary houses continues below.
[c2-l034 >] *paração de outras que hum tem menos prin-*
[c2-l034 note] Other houses are less principal. The scan and syntax support outras, not oatras.
[c2-l035 >] *cipaes.*
[c2-l035 note] Completes principaes. This short row is body text: the next heading follows within the column.
[c2-l036] Fontai. Fonnotai. *Verdadeira, ou propria*
[c2-l036 note] Hontai, 本体, true substance or essence, hon no tai. Keep Fonnotai joined as printed rather than respacing it from the analysis.
[c2-l037 >] *ſuſtancia.*
[c2-l037 note] Sustancia completes the essence definition. Its missing b is historical, not grounds for modernization to substância.
[c2-l038] Fon v. Motoyori v, nari. *Couſa que ouue,*
[c2-l038 note] Hon u, 本有, original existence, moto yori u nari. Independent v represents u and is not a numeral.
[c2-l039 >] *ou tem ſer deſdo principio. Bup.*
[c2-l039 note] Existing from the beginning, marked Buddhist by Bup. The label remains italic as printed here.
[c2-l040] Fonxen. *Caxas, ou moedas de cobre que ſe*
[c2-l040 note] Honsen, 本銭, principal capital in copper cash. Caxas means coins here, not modern caixas as boxes.
[c2-l041 >] *dão, ou empreſtão ao ganho. i, Cabedal de*
[c2-l041 note] Coins given or lent at interest. Initial d in dão has lost much of its ascender; the surviving mark and emprestão context support repairing OCR cão.
[c2-l042 >] *caxas tirando os ganhos.*
[c2-l042 note] The principal excludes profits or interest. This confirms the financial sense of honsen.
[c2-l043] Fonxeqi. *Pedra principal, & mais alta que*
[c2-l043 note] Honseki, 本石, the principal and tallest garden stone. Xe represents the period's se sound rather than an exotic new reading.
[c2-l044 >] *ſe poem nas Niuas.*
[c2-l044 note] The stone is placed in niwas, gardens. The borrowed term is italic here and should remain easy to toggle for human review.
[c2-l045] Fonxet. Fonnoxet. *Vide* Fonjet. *Nouas cer-*
[c2-l045 note] Honset, 本説, authentic news or teaching, refers back to Fonjet. Preserve the final t and the documented voiced/unvoiced variant.
[c2-l046 >] *tas. ¶ Item, Opinião certa, ou doctri-*
[c2-l046 note] True news or an established opinion or doctrine. This parallels f209's Fonjet rather than calling for a new lexical interpretation.
[c2-l047 >] *na, &c.*
[c2-l047 note] Completes doctrina. Fonxi lower at the right is the following page's catchword, not part of this sentence.
