[c1-l001] Fôbucu. *Vestido de* Bõzos. *ou religioſos, ou*
[c1-l001 note] Fôbucu is hōbuku, religious or ceremonial clothing; the Portuguese explicitly identifies the wearers as Buddhist religious. The damaged b was read as t by OCR. Bõzos is an embedded upright word, with a full stop following it in this impression.
[c1-l002 >] *veſtidos de que vſão em ſeus officios, & ſo-*
[c1-l002 note] This continues the definition, specifying clothes worn in religious offices and solemnities. Officios refers to observances here, not simply occupations; the last so- belongs to solennidades on the next line.
[c1-l003 >] *lennidades.*
[c1-l003 note] Lennidades completes solennidades, solemn celebrations. The narrow letter after nn is i, not the OCR u; this is a continuation, not a separate entry.
[c1-l004] Fobucuro. *Bolſo da vela quãdo vai cõ vẽto.*
[c1-l004 note] Fobucuro is hobukuro, the belly or pocket formed by a wind-filled sail. Bolso da vela and quãdo vai cõ vẽto give that nautical context; the abbreviated quãdo has a worn nasal mark, not the OCR quido.
[c1-l005] Fǒca. *Trejeitador. ¶* Fǒcauo ſuru. *Fa-*
[c1-l005 note] Fǒca is hōka, a conjurer or performer. Fǒcauo suru means performing such tricks; the Portuguese action begins with Fa- and continues on the next line.
[c1-l006 >] *zer officio, ou obras de trejeitador enganando*
[c1-l006 note] Fazer officio, ou obras de trejeitador describes acting as a conjurer and deceiving the viewer. The scan supports officio, including its faint i, rather than offcio.
[c1-l007 >] *a vista artificioſamente.*
[c1-l007 note] A vista artificiosamente completes the thought: deceiving sight by artifice. The damaged middle letters still support artificioſamente, not artificinſamente; vista's short s is left as printed/OCR-read.
[c1-l008] Foca. *Fora. ¶* Cono foca. *Alem di-*
[c1-l008 note] Foca is hoka, outside or other; cono foca is kono hoka, beyond this or in addition. Portuguese Fora and Alem di- separate the spatial and additive uses.
[c1-l009 >] *ſto. ¶* Iyeno foca. *Fora da caſa.*
[c1-l009 note] Isto finishes the preceding Portuguese phrase. Iyeno foca is ie no hoka, outside the house, exactly matching Fora da casa; the old romanization is not modernized.
[c1-l010 >] Sono focano guiuo nochi catari mara-
[c1-l010 note] Sono focano giu means the remaining matter; nochi catari introduces telling it later. Mara- belongs to the historical polite ending continued below, not to an independent word.
[c1-l011 >] xǒzu. *O reſtante praticarei depois. ¶* Cu
[c1-l011 note] Xǒzu completes the historical polite/future expression, rendered in Portuguese as discussing the rest later. Cu at the end starts Cugue across the line break; it is not a one-syllable standalone example.
[c1-l012 >] gue buquãno fitobito yori focaua qiǒgiù
[c1-l012 note] Cugue buqua names court nobles and the warrior class; yori foca ua means apart from them. Qiogiù is the printed form for residence in the capital in this quotation; its faint one-sided accent is retained rather than regularized from pronunciation.
[c1-l013 >] ni fito aritomo miyezariqeri. *T*aif. *Li-*
[c1-l013 note] Ni fito aritomo miyezariqeri completes the negative account of people being present. The citation is provisionally Taif., the usual Taiheiki label, with its final f severely worn; the surviving area after i and the book citation support it, but this is not a cleanly preserved letter.
[c1-l014 >] *bro 30*. *Tirando os nobres aßi Cũgues co-*
[c1-l014 note] Livro 30 identifies the cited book. Tirando os nobres means excepting the nobles; Cũgues is embedded Japanese court terminology but looks italic in this line, so it is not automatically made upright.
[c1-l015 >] *mo caualeiros não auia dentro do Miyaco gente.*
[c1-l015 note] The Portuguese finishes by saying there were no people within Miyaco except the named noble groups. Miyaco is printed upright within the italic explanation; nao auia retains the historical spelling of modern não havia.
[c1-l016] Focafoca. *Adu. Impetuoſamente, ou com*
[c1-l016 note] Focafoca is an adverb explained here as impetuously or forcefully, not limited to the modern warm/fluffy sense of hokahoka. Impetuoſamente is supported by the scan and the following ou com força.
[c1-l017 >] *força. Vt,* Focafocato tçuqi touoſu.
[c1-l017 note] Focafocato tçuqi touoſu is hokahoka to tsuki tōsu, piercing forcefully through something. The Portuguese com força belongs to the preceding definition; Vt introduces this example.
[c1-l018 >] *Modo de traſpaßar com adaga, ou lança furio*
[c1-l018 note] The example is explained as driving a dagger or spear through furiously. Furio continues into samente below without an added editorial hyphen; traſpaßar retains the OCR ligature distinction.
[c1-l019 >] *ſamente. ¶ Item, inconſideradamente.*
[c1-l019 note] Samente completes furiosamente. Item then introduces another sense, inconsiderately, rather than another Japanese headword.
[c1-l020 >] *Vt,* Focafocato monouo yù. *Falar algũa*
[c1-l020 note] Focafocato monouo yû means speaking heedlessly. The Portuguese Falar algũa begins the corresponding definition; the source's nasal mark is kept on u.
[c1-l021 >] *couſa deſatentadamente.*
[c1-l021 note] Cousa desatentadamente completes speaking something heedlessly. The first consonant is a worn d, and the apparent gap inside desatentadamente is mostly a damaged a, not good evidence for two words.
[c1-l022] Focagamaye. *Forte, cerca, ou tranqueira q̃ ſe*
[c1-l022 note] Focagamaye is hokagamae, outer works or an outer enclosure. Forte, cerca, tranqueira are alternative descriptions of a defensive perimeter, consistent with hoka plus kamae.
[c1-l023 >] *faz fora da fortaleza, ou da cidade, pouoa-*
[c1-l023 note] The enclosure is built outside a fortress, city, or settlement. Pouoa- is continued below; the line does not introduce another example.
[c1-l024 >] *ção, &c.*
[c1-l024 note] Ção completes pouoação, settlement. The short physical line is a real continuation and is kept with its own stable ID and readable crop.
[c1-l025] Focague. *Vela que aparece ao longe. ¶ Itẽ,*
[c1-l025 note] Focague is hokage in the sail sense, a sail visible at a distance. The following Item adds a related meaning; it should not be interpreted as the modern firelight homophone just from kana.
[c1-l026 >] *Sombra da vela.*
[c1-l026 note] Sombra da vela gives the second meaning, the shadow or silhouette of a sail. This confirms the nautical reading of the preceding headword.
[c1-l027] Fôcai. i, Xecai. *Mundo.*
[c1-l027 note] Fôcai is hōkai, equated with xecai, sekai, the world. The printed circumflex is distinct from the unaccented Focai in the next entry.
[c1-l028] Focai. *Hũa maneira de caxa como* Bentò *que ſe*
[c1-l028 note] Focai is hokai, a portable food container. Caxa is the historical spelling of caixa, not cara; Bentò is an upright embedded Japanese comparator, retaining the printed grave accent.
[c1-l029 >] *leua por caminho, ou na embarcação com co-*
[c1-l029 note] The container is carried on a journey or aboard a vessel. Com co- begins com comer dentro, with food inside, continued below.
[c1-l030 >] *mer dentro.*
[c1-l030 note] Mer dentro completes the explanation of the container's contents. Comer functions nominally as food here, not an instruction to eat indoors.
[c1-l031] Fǒcan. Cobaxij fude. i, fumi. *Carta falan-*
[c1-l031 note] Fǒcan is an honorific term for a letter, glossed with fragrant writing and fumi. Cobaxij fude is severely worn at its beginning; although kōbashii is the expected sense, no dependable accent survives over o, so the unaccented spelling is retained.
[c1-l032 >] *do com reuerencia da peßoa.*
[c1-l032 note] Carta falando com reverencia da pessoa explains the honorific use of the preceding Japanese expression. It is about deferential reference to someone's letter, not a separate sense of fude as a physical brush.
[c1-l033] Focani. *Adu. Fora, ou pera fora.*
[c1-l033 note] Focani is hoka ni, outside or outward, marked Adu. The Portuguese distinction Fora / pera fora expresses location versus direction without requiring a change to the Japanese spelling.
[c1-l034] Focaqebune. *Embarcação que vai à vela.*
[c1-l034 note] Focaqebune is hokakebune, a sailing vessel. The scan and Embarcação que vai à vela agree; qe belongs to ke, while bune is the voiced form of fune.
[c1-l035] Foccai. Qitano vmi. *Mar do Norte.*
[c1-l035 note] Foccai is hokkai, the northern sea, explicitly unpacked as qita no umi. The doubled c represents the consonant sequence, not a missing vowel.
[c1-l036] Fôcǒ. *Rebuço. Vt,* Fôcǒ ſuru. *Por*

[c1-l037 note] Por rebuço no rosto completes putting a covering over the face. The spelling roſto is left to the OCR's typographic reading, rather than inferred from a general s-before-t rule.
[c1-l038] Foccocu. Qitano cuni. *Reinos da parte*
[c1-l038 note] Foccocu is hokkoku, northern provinces or countries, glossed qita no cuni. The explanation continues below and supports the doubled consonant.
[c1-l039 >] *do Norte.*
[c1-l039 note] Do Norte completes the geographical definition. This short line has its own body rectangle; it is neither a heading nor page furniture.
[c1-l040] Foccu. i, Iydaſu fajimeno cu. *Primeira ſẽ*
[c1-l040 note] Foccu is hokku, the opening verse; iydaſu fajimeno cu explicitly describes the first verse brought forth. The nasal mark over the final e in ſẽ is visible and belongs to sentença across the break.
[c1-l041 >] *tença das Vtas*, *ou verſos que ſe enfião, & cõ*
[c1-l041 note] Vtas refers to Japanese poems and is upright in the Portuguese explanation. Enfião means the verses are strung together; the worn nasal ending is not the OCR enfido, and cõ starts continuão below.
[c1-l042 >] *tinuão tè cento.*
[c1-l042 note] Tinuão tè cento completes continuing the linked verses up to one hundred. The damaged u is supported by the remaining strokes and cõ above; the printed historical tè is preserved.
[c1-l043] Fochifochito. *Adu. Modo de cairem cou-*
[c1-l043 note] Fochifochito is an expressive adverb, first explained through small objects falling. The Portuguese cou- continues into cousas, not a Japanese fragment.
[c1-l044 >] *ſas miudas, ou modo de ſoar algũa couſa que ſe*
[c1-l044 note] The definition also covers the sound of something breaking. Algũa is retained with its tilde over u; nothing in the sense licenses relocating that mark.
[c1-l045 >] *quebra. Vt,* Fochifochito mameuo tçu-
[c1-l045 note] Fochifochito mameuo tçumu illustrates eating beans with a crunching sound. The final tçu- continues to mu; the faint terminal stroke is retained without introducing a new hyphen elsewhere.
[c1-l046 >] mu. *Comer grãos que ſoão entre os dentes*
[c1-l046 note] Mu completes the Japanese verb. The Portuguese explains grains sounding between the teeth, supporting the expressive reading rather than a silent picking action inferred from modern tsumu alone.

[c1-l047 note] A further use is reading slowly, as if spelling out letters. So- continues with letrando at the top of column 2; this is body text, not a catchword.
