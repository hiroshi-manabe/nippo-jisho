[c1-l001] Fendo. i, Catainaca. *Aldea, ou arrabal-*
[c1-l001 note] Hendo, katainaka, means a village or outlying district. Arrabaldes is divided across the next row.
[c1-l002 >] *des. ¶* Fendo fennai. *Aldea, ou arra-*
[c1-l002 note] Hendo hennai is another expression for these outlying settlements; the comparison point follows below.
[c1-l003 >] *baldes em reſpeito do Miyaco.*
[c1-l003 note] The settlements are peripheral relative to Miyako, the capital. Miyaco is Japanese embedded in the Portuguese definition.
[c1-l004] Fendô. Fenji vgoqu. *Mudança, & varieda*
[c1-l004 note] Hendou, henji ugoku, is change or variation. Mudanca restores the Portuguese noun lost in OCR Malanca.
[c1-l005 >] *de. ¶ Item, Alteração, ou perturbação.*
[c1-l005 note] Alteracao and perturbacao add disturbance or agitation to the sense of change. The missing letters were checked in the scan.
[c1-l006] Fengai. *O faltar na promeſſa. Vt,* Yacuſo-
[c1-l006 note] Hengai is failure to keep a promise. The repeated fengai in the example supports F rather than OCR B.
[c1-l007 >] cuuo fengai ſuru. *Arrependerſe da promeſ-*
[c1-l007 note] Yakusoku wo hengai suru is retracting or failing a promise. Promessa continues across the row boundary.
[c1-l008 >] *ſa, ou não a comprir.*
[c1-l008 note] The gloss says not fulfilling the promise. Comprir is retained as the historical printed form, rather than modern cumprir.
[c1-l009] Fengaye. *Idem. Meliùs,* Fengai.
[c1-l009 note] Hengaye is an alternative form; Melius explicitly recommends hengai. That preference is the dictionary's, not an editorial modernization.
[c1-l010] Fengi. Vt, Sucuſan fengi. *Terra como ilha*
[c1-l010 note] Henji in shukusan henji concerns a peripheral island or land. This example should not be confused with henji meaning a reply.
[c1-l011 >] *comarcãa a algũa terra firme, & larga como*
[c1-l011 note] The island lies near a large mainland. Terra firme, restored from OCR frme, is essential to the geographical comparison.
[c1-l012 >] *Iapão em comparação da China. S.*
[c1-l012 note] Japan relative to China is the stated example. S identifies the written register of this geographical expression.
[c1-l013] Fengue. *O transformarſe em outra figura.*
[c1-l013 note] Henge is transformation into another appearance or shape. Figura corrects a vowel error in the Portuguese gloss.
[c1-l014 >] *¶* Fengueno mono. *Couſa transſormada*
[c1-l014 note] Henge no mono is a transformed being or thing; the visible no mono sequence is not OCR mo mono.
[c1-l015 >] *como o Diabo em figura de rapoſa, &c.*
[c1-l015 note] The missionary illustrates transformation with a devil in fox form. This expresses the source's framing, not a modern identification of all such beings.
[c1-l016 >] *¶* Tengu qitçuneni fengue ſuru. *Tomar*
[c1-l016 note] Tengu kitsune ni henge suru means a tengu assuming fox form. Both kitsune and henge are supported by the gloss and scan.
[c1-l017 >] *o demonio a figura de rapoſa.*
[c1-l017 note] The Portuguese repeats that the demon takes the appearance of a fox, completing the transformation example.
[c1-l018] Fenguen. i, Catacotoba. *Palaura imperfei-*
[c1-l018 note] Hengen, katakotoba, is an incomplete or brief utterance. Kotoba and Portuguese palavra jointly expose two OCR word corruptions.
[c1-l019 >] *ta, ou qualquer palaurinha que pode dar no co-*
[c1-l019 note] Even a small word can affect someone's heart. Palaurinha is the diminutive of palavra, not the separated OCR fragment.
[c1-l020 >] *ração dalguem.*
[c1-l020 note] Coracao de alguem completes that effect on another person's heart; it belongs to the same hengen definition.
[c1-l021] Fen-i. Fenji, cauaru. *Mudança, ou va-*
[c1-l021 note] Hen-i is change, glossed henji kawaru. The printed internal hyphen distinguishes the vowel after n and is not a line-end mark.
[c1-l022 >] *riedade.*
[c1-l022 note] Completes variedade. The vowel sequence rie is restored from OCR ric without changing the physical line division.
[c1-l023] Fenji. Cayeri coto. *Reposta.*
[c1-l023 note] Henji, kaeri koto, is a reply. Keep this reply entry distinct from the preceding change entry.
[c1-l024] Fẽji, uru, ita. *Mudarſe, ou trocar a figura*
[c1-l024 note] Fẽji / fenzuru means changing one's appearance or form. An enlarged view shows the contraction mark over e; the following example concerns changing into a mountain ascetic's costume.
[c1-l025 >] *trajo, &c. Vt,* Fôguan yamabuxino
[c1-l025 note] The example concerns Hougan wearing a yamabushi's appearance. Vt introduces the example; it is not Portuguese Et.
[c1-l026 >] ſugatani fenji Foccocuuo ſaite cudaraxe-
[c1-l026 note] Yamabushi no sugata ni henji means changing into an ascetic's guise before traveling through the northern provinces. Sugata corrects the OCR vowel.
[c1-l027 >] raruru. *Tog. Fonguandono veſtindoſe em*
[c1-l027 note] Completes the honorific verb. The following source label remains Tog. pending identification; the Portuguese begins the account of Honguandono changing dress.
[c1-l028 >] *trajos de Yamabuxi deceo pera as partes do*
[c1-l028 note] Yamabuxi names the mountain ascetic whose dress is assumed; it is an embedded Japanese term, not the OCR split Yam ibuxi.
[c1-l029 >] *Norte. ¶* Yacuſocuuo fenzuru. *Que-*
[c1-l029 note] Norte completes the northern destination. Yakusoku wo fenzuru then introduces breaking a promise.
[c1-l030 >] *brar a promeſſa. ¶* Cocoroga fenzuru.
[c1-l030 note] Quebrar a promessa completes that translation. Kokoro ga fenzuru introduces a change of mind or intention.
[c1-l031 >] *Mudar a vontade, ou propoſito. ¶* Irouo
[c1-l031 note] The Portuguese names will or purpose as what changes; iro wo begins the next example concerning facial color.
[c1-l032 >] fenzuru. *Mudar a cor do roſto*
[c1-l032 note] Iro wo fenzuru means changing the color of the face. Rosto belongs to Portuguese and its s-type is retained.
[c1-l033] Fenjǒ. Cayexi tatematçuru. *Reſponder a*
[c1-l033 note] Henjou, kaeshi tatematsuru, is replying respectfully. The restored headword matches the honorific verb and the noble addressee below.
[c1-l034 >] *peſſoa nobre.*
[c1-l034 note] Pessoa nobre specifies the high-status addressee. The OCR double-l reading is incompatible with the printed s-types and noun.
[c1-l035] Fenjo. *Repoſta por carta.*
[c1-l035 note] Henjo is a written reply by letter, a separate noun from the preceding respectful action.
[c1-l036] Fenjô. Vt, Quǒ miô fenjǒ. *O alumiaro*
[c1-l036 note] Fenjô is the illuminating sense of henjou, illustrated by Quǒ miô fenjǒ. The headword and example have visibly different o marks; they are retained rather than normalized to a single spelling of the Buddhist expression.
[c1-l037 >] *Fotoque todas as couſas.*
[c1-l037 note] Fotoque is the illuminator in the Portuguese explanation: the Buddha illuminates all things. The embedded term is printed italic here.
[c1-l038] Fenman. Amanecu, michitari. *O eſtarmui-*
[c1-l038 note] Henman means filling everywhere, explained by amaneku michitari. Initial F, not B, is supported by both the scan and the example.
[c1-l039 >] *to cheo, & abũdãte. Vt,* Cono xecaini acu-
[c1-l039 note] Kono sekai ni aku... begins 'in this world, evil...'. The ni particle was misread as mi; the Portuguese describes fullness or abundance.
[c1-l040 >] ga fenman xite aru. *Eſtà eſte mundo cheo*
[c1-l040 note] Aku ga henman shite aru says evil pervades the world. Fenman repeats the headword rather than OCR tenman.
[c1-l041 >] *de maldade. S.*
[c1-l041 note] De maldade completes the world's being full of evil, with S marking written use.
[c1-l042] Fenmet. Cauari forobu. *Mudança, &*
[c1-l042 note] Henmet is change and destruction, glossed kawari horobu. Final t represents a checked ending rather than a missing full syllable.
[c1-l043 >] *deſtruição. S.*
[c1-l043 note] Destruicao completes the two-part definition of change and destruction; the written-register label follows.
[c1-l044] Fenmocu. *Demanda, ou negocio de emba-*
[c1-l044 note] Fenmocu concerns a dispute or troublesome affair. The crowded m was split into ni by OCR; NINJAL's headword identification supports returning to the visible m rather than inventing fennioku.
[c1-l045 >] *raço, briga, &c. que ſe leuanta entre al-*
[c1-l045 note] The entry includes quarrels arising between people; alguns continues across the row boundary.
[c1-l046 >] *gums. ¶ Item taboada de liuro.*
[c1-l046 note] Completes alguns and adds a second meaning, a book's table or index. Taboada restores the final vowel in OCR taboadu.
[c1-l047] Fenmonai. *Couſa de pouco momento, &*
[c1-l047 note] Fenmonai describes something of little consequence or profit. The enlarged scan and the NINJAL headword agree on one n before m; the first-pass double-n guess has been removed.
[c1-l048 >] *proueito.*
[c1-l048 note] Proveito completes 'of little importance and benefit'. This short continuation belongs to the preceding adjective.

[c2-l001] Fenni. Catamimi. *Hũa das orelhas, ou cu-*
[c2-l001 note] Henni, katamimi, is one ear. The Portuguese ouvidos is divided across the next row.
[c2-l002 >] *uidos. Vt,* Fenninuxite rifiuo tadaſu co-
[c2-l002 note] Henni ni shite rihi wo tadasu begins a maxim about judging right and wrong from one-sided hearing. Ri-hi is not OCR ri-fi.
[c2-l003 >] to canauazu. *Ouuindo com hũa ſo*
[c2-l003 note] Koto kanawazu makes that judgment impossible. The Portuguese begins hearing with only one ear.
[c2-l004 >] *orelha não ſe pode julgar, & diſcernir o bem*
[c2-l004 note] One cannot judge or distinguish good by hearing only one side; the paired evil follows below.
[c2-l005 >] *& o mal. S.*
[c2-l005 note] E o mal completes the good/evil contrast. S identifies the maxim as written usage.
[c2-l006] Fennǒ. Cayexi voſamuru. *Tornar, ou pagar*
[c2-l006 note] Hennou, kaeshi osamuru, means returning or paying what is owed. The final payment recipient follows in the gloss.
[c2-l007 >] *a diuida, &c. principalimente ao ſenhor.*
[c2-l007 note] The debt is repaid particularly to one's lord. Preserve the historical printed adverb rather than silently regularizing it.
[c2-l008] Fenoco. *Bolſo dos teſticulos.*
[c2-l008 note] Henoko is anatomically glossed as the sac of the testicles, the scrotum; the definition is narrower than some later uses of the word.
[c2-l009] Fenpô. Cayexi mucù. *Retribuição de bẽs.*
[c2-l009 note] Henpou is recompense, kaeshi mukuu, covering repayment of good actions as well as the retaliation below.
[c2-l010 >] *& vingança de males, poſto que mais propri-*
[c2-l010 note] The gloss adds vengeance for wrongs, then narrows the usual sense in the following row.
[c2-l011 >] *amente ſe toma por vingança. ¶* Fenpô-
[c2-l011 note] The dictionary says the term more properly means revenge. Henpou wo suru starts across the row break.
[c2-l012 >] uo ſuru. *Vingarſe. ¶* Von aruji De-
[c2-l012 note] Henpou wo suru is taking revenge. A Christian example then begins on aruji Deus, with Deus split between rows.
[c2-l013 >] us jen acuno gofenpôuo naſaxeraruru.
[c2-l013 note] God is said to recompense good and evil, using the honorific nasaseraruru. Deus and Christao elsewhere are borrowed Christian vocabulary inside Japanese examples.
[c2-l014 >] *Deus N. ſenhor da o pago dos bẽs, & males.*
[c2-l014 note] The Portuguese translates God our Lord giving recompense for good and evil; retain the source's theological claim as a gloss.
[c2-l015] Fenpoccai. *Todo eſte mundo.*
[c2-l015 note] Henpokkai denotes the entire world. Do not infer an unprinted space or repair its gemination solely from a modern compound analysis.
[c2-l016] Fenpucu. Cǒmuri. *Morcego.*
[c2-l016 note] Henpuku is bat, explained by koumuri. The printed form lacks the t of modern koumori-related alternatives and is retained.
[c2-l017] Fenpucu. Cayexi cutçugayeru. *Virarſe cain-*
[c2-l017 note] The homophone henpuku means overturning, kaeshi kutsugaeru. Caindo is physically divided in the Portuguese gloss.
[c2-l018 >] *do, ou ſoſobrarſe.*
[c2-l018 note] Completes falling or capsizing. This is the overturning entry, not a continuation of the bat definition.
[c2-l019] Fenqi. *Contradizer a algũa couſaa que hum*
[c2-l019 note] Henki is opposition to something one dislikes. The nasal vowel in algua was corrupted by OCR; the broader phrase continues below.
[c2-l020 >] *tẽ aſco, ou auerſão, por obras ou palauras. Vt,*
[c2-l020 note] Aversion may be expressed by acts or words. The abbreviated tem is tẽ, not grave-accented te.
[c2-l021 >] Varui gẽtioxù Chriſtaono voxiyeuo fẽqi
[c2-l021 note] Varui gẽtioxù describes hostile gentios, with a Japanese shuu ending, rejecting Christian teaching. The t is badly damaged and is supplied contextually in this working reading; the visible loan-word sequence and the next line's Portuguese gentios support it, but this remains a useful human-check point.
[c2-l022 >] ſuru. *Os gentios maos des danhão da doc-*
[c2-l022 note] The Japanese suru completes the example; the Portuguese begins the account of hostile gentiles scorning the doctrine.
[c2-l023 >] *trina dos chriſtãos, & moſtranlhe pouca von-*
[c2-l023 note] Christaos identifies the Christians whose doctrine is rejected. The gloss continues with showing it little goodwill.
[c2-l024 >] *tade, &c.*
[c2-l024 note] Completes vontade and the conventional etcetera abbreviation; OCR e with an accent was not a lexical word.
[c2-l025] Fenrei. Reigayexi. *Paga da viſita, ou*
[c2-l025 note] Henrei, reigaeshi, is returning a visit or gift. The Portuguese paga here concerns reciprocity rather than necessarily money.
[c2-l026 >] *retorno dalgum preſente.*
[c2-l026 note] Retorno dalgum presente completes the reciprocal-gift sense of henrei.
[c2-l027] Fenſai. Fotori, qiua. *Borda, termo, &c.*
[c2-l027 note] Hensai, hotori/kiwa, is an edge or limit. The headword begins with F, matching its repetition below.
[c2-l028 >] *Vt,* Fenſaimo nai, l, caguirimo nai. *Não*
[c2-l028 note] Hensai mo nai and kagiri mo nai both express having no limit. Nao starts the Portuguese negative construction.
[c2-l029 >] *ter termo, nem fim.*
[c2-l029 note] Ter termo nem fim completes 'have neither limit nor end', matching both Japanese examples.
[c2-l030] Fenſan. *O peregrinar por diuerſas partes, ou*
[c2-l030 note] Hensan is pilgrimage through different places, not simply casual travel in this religiously framed definition.
[c2-l031 >] *andar de terra ẽ terra fazẽdo certos exercicios*
[c2-l031 note] The traveler goes from place to place performing particular exercises or observances, whose practitioners are named next.
[c2-l032 >] *como fazem os Bõzos chamados Yẽgueſos.*
[c2-l032 note] Yenguesos is the source's name for those practitioners; the Portuguese plural ending is retained. It is an embedded Japanese-derived term.
[c2-l033] Fenſanzǒ. *Bõzos que andão desta maneira pe-*
[c2-l033 note] Hensanzou denotes monks who travel in this way. Peregrinando continues across the physical row boundary.
[c2-l034 >] *regrinando.*
[c2-l034 note] Completes peregrinando, closing the description of the traveling monks.
[c2-l035] Fenſat. *Repoſta da carta. Vt,* Gofenſatni
[c2-l035 note] Hensat is a reply letter, with a checked final t. Gohensat ni begins the respectful example acknowledging such a reply.
[c2-l036 >] azzucari ſoro. *Recebi a carta de voſſa mer-*
[c2-l036 note] Azukari soro completes the receipt formula. The Portuguese says receiving the addressee's letter.
[c2-l037 >] *ce em reposta da minha. S.*
[c2-l037 note] The received letter answers the speaker's own letter; S marks the epistolary written register.
[c2-l038] Fentǒ. i, Fenji. *Repoſta. ¶* Fentǒ.
[c2-l038 note] Hentou is a reply, equated with henji. The following example repeats hentou before the verb on the next row.
[c2-l039 >] yǔ. *Reſponder.*
[c2-l039 note] Yuu completes hentou yuu, to answer. The vowel mark is a length mark rather than OCR's nasal tilde.
[c2-l040] Fenxi. Catatoqi. i, Sucoxino aida. *Hum*
[c2-l040 note] Henshi, katatoki, is a short interval, su koshi no aida. Initial P was an OCR error for F.
[c2-l041 >] *pequeno eſpaço. ¶* Fenximo nǒ. *Lo-*
[c2-l041 note] Fenximo nǒ means without even a moment's delay. The enlarged image supports the separate negative nǒ after mo, not the OCR's extra a; the Portuguese logo / sem tardança confirms the immediate sense.
[c2-l042 >] *go, ou ſem tardança.*
[c2-l042 note] Logo ou sem tardanca explicitly means immediately, without delay, supporting the preceding example's negative-time interpretation.
[c2-l043] Fenxin. Cayexi mairaſuru. *Pagar o que*
[c2-l043 note] Henshin, kaeshi mairasuru, is respectfully returning a borrowed object. The Portuguese says paying back what was taken.
[c2-l044 >] *ſe tomou ẽprestado particularmente ao ſenhor,*
[c2-l044 note] The borrowed object is returned particularly to a lord; the nasal abbreviation in emprestado is retained rather than expanded.
[c2-l045 >] *ou a outra peſſoa honrada, ou do meſmo teor.*
[c2-l045 note] The recipient may instead be another honored person or someone of equivalent standing; this clause ends before the next qualification.
[c2-l046 >] *Não ſe dirà de arroz, dinheiro, &c. mas de*
[c2-l046 note] The word is not used for rice or money. Nao se dira supplies that restriction, correcting garbled OCR dtrade.
[c2-l047 >] *couſas que ſe tornão as meſmas numero como de*
[c2-l047 note] It concerns returning the very same objects, not equivalent quantities. The sentence continues onto the next page with an example.
