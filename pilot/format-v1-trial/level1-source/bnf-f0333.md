[c1-l001 >] *¶* Ruſuuo moru. *Ficar guardando a caſa.*
[c1-l001 note] Rusu wo moru means staying to guard the house. The scan supplies the omitted paragraph mark and caſa, not eaſa. The first vowel of Ruſuuo is badly abraded; the surviving stroke is compatible with u, and the house-watching context supports this chosen reading rather than the misleading kana hint for Riſu.

[c1-l019 >] *deſta maneira dous o caualo. ¶* Morocuchini

[c1-l021] Moroi. *Couſa fragil, & caduca. ¶* Mo-

[c1-l022 >] roi inochi. *Vida breue, ou q̃ logo ſe acaba.*

[c1-l023] Moromi. *Arroz que està feito em vinho antes*

[c1-l025] Moromizaqe. *Vinho que eſtà ainda por eſpre-*
[c1-l025 note] Moromizake names the still-unpressed rice wine. The scan supports eſtà, correcting OCR eſts; the divided infinitive continues below.
[c1-l026 >] *mer juntamente com o arroz cõuertido ja na ſu-*
[c1-l026 note] The wine remains together with the rice already converted into its substance. The abbreviation cõuertido is retained, and ſu- is a physical division of ſuſtancia.
[c1-l027 >] *ſtancia do vinho.*
[c1-l027 note] This completes ſuſtancia do vinho, the substance of the wine. It is the end of the Moromizaqe definition, not a new entry.
[c1-l028] Moromoro. *Todos. ¶* Moromorono fi-
[c1-l028 note] Moromoro means all or the various members of a group. The example Moromorono fi- continues into fito on the next physical row.
[c1-l029 >] to. *Todos os homẽs.*
[c1-l029 note] To completes fito in the example meaning all people. The Portuguese repeats Todos os homẽs with the printed abbreviation.
[c1-l030] Moroqe, uru, eta. *Desfazerſe, ou delirſe,*
[c1-l030 note] Moroqe gives a verb meaning to disintegrate or dissolve. Its principal forms are retained rather than modernized to a present-day conjugation.
[c1-l031 >] *como peixe muito cozido, &c. ¶* Xôguo
[c1-l031 note] The gloss compares disintegration to overcooked fish. The example begins Xôguo, small fish: the capital X and guo are visible, without the OCR's extra r.
[c1-l032 >] niruniua farauatauo ſaqezu, ſono moroqẽ
[c1-l032 note] The example says that when cooking small fish one does not slit the belly, fearing that the fish will fall apart. The long ſ in ſaqezu, supported by the unsplit-belly sense, corrects OCR faqezu; farauatauo retains f. Moroqẽ carries an abbreviation tilde, not a long-vowel accent.
[c1-l033 >] cotouo voſoru. *O peixe miudo aßi ſemlhe ti*
[c1-l033 note] Kotouo voſoru completes the fear that the fish will fall apart. The Portuguese begins the recommendation to cook small fish without removing the guts; its final ti continues as tirar on the following row without a supplied hyphen.
[c1-l034 >] *rar as tripas he bõ cozelo pera q̃ ſe não desfaça.*
[c1-l034 note] This completes tirar and explains the purpose: leaving the guts in helps prevent the fish from disintegrating. The scan separates as tripas; the historical bõ and cozelo are retained.
[c1-l035] Morote. *Ambas as mãos* || {mark} *(* || {word}*juntos.*
[c1-l035 note] Morote means both hands. The separate right-edge (juntos. is visibly printed on this row but semantically completes Todos in the following Morotomoni entry; it is preserved in its physical position rather than moved into that sentence. [c1-l036] Morotomoni. *Iuntamente ambas. ¶ Itẽ, Todos*

[c1-l037] Morovruxi. *Certo verniz fino que ſe poem por*
[c1-l037 note] Morourushi is a fine lacquer, described here as a varnish put over another material. The visible v after Moro corrects OCR y and reflects the urushi element; the supplied NINJAL index agrees on the lexical identification (167a17).
[c1-l038 >] *cima do couro das bainhas.*
[c1-l038 note] The lacquer is applied over the leather of scabbards. This completes the preceding relative clause and does not name another material independently.
[c1-l039] Moſacu. Saguri, motomuru. *Buſcar, ou in-*
[c1-l039 note] Mosaku is paraphrased saguri, motomuru, searching or seeking. The Portuguese verb investigar is divided after in-; its continuation and Buddhist label are below.
[c1-l040 >] *uestigar.* Bup.
[c1-l040 note] This completes investigar, to investigate or search. The weak final vowel is read as a rather than OCR e. The second pass retains the aligned round s in the connected st type; Bup. is a roman Buddhist-domain label, not a Japanese word or a partly italic headword.
[c1-l041] Mǒſô. Midarini vttayuru. *Acuſar falſa-*
[c1-l041 note] The Japanese paraphrase means bringing an accusation rashly or without basis. Enlargement supports Midarini, not Xidarini, and a worn long ſ in Mǒſô rather than t. The printed final circumflex is retained even though the lexical index flags the headword as an apparent misprint for Mǒso (NINJAL 167a19).
[c1-l042 >] *mente, ou ſem fundamento, & leuemente.*

[c1-l043 note] Mǒſochicu names a Chinese bamboo. The first o bears a caron; the following o is unmarked in this impression, so the expected long vowel in the lexical name is not supplied.
[c1-l044] Mǒſot. *Gente feroz, como de guerra, &c.*
[c1-l044 note] Mǒſot denotes fierce people, such as warriors. The faint initial caron is retained, and the final checked t is not expanded to tçu; the supplied NINJAL index corroborates this headword (167a21).
[c1-l045] Mǒſsǒ iy. *Certa maneira darroz cozido que*
[c1-l045 note] This names a particular preparation or serving of cooked rice, with iy representing rice. The worn ſ followed by round s corrects OCR is; both o vowels show carons. The printed initial long-vowel marking is retained despite the lexical index's warning about an apparent anomaly (NINJAL 167a22).
[c1-l046 >] *dão os Ienxùs.*
[c1-l046 note] The rice is given by the Ienxùs, the Zen religious group named in the Portuguese gloss. This is an embedded Japanese term, not a standalone synonym or a new headword.
[c1-l047] Môſu. *Certa laya de barretes de que vſão os*
[c1-l047 note] Môſu names a type of cap. The bottom row is recoverable in the scan despite the severely garbled OCR; its definition continues at the top of column 2 with the wearers and occasions of use.
