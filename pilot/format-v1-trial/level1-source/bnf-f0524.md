[c1-l001] Vabibito. *Homem triste, pobre, & miſerauel.*

[c1-l003 note] Completes alguem and specifies that the petition is made with self-humbling. This is an indented continuation of wabikoto, not a separate entry.

[c1-l004 note] Wabigoe names the voice of a person pleading or interceding; hu- begins the adverb humildemente across the physical line break.
[c1-l005 >] *milmente, ou ſe mezquinha, & entriſtece.*
[c1-l005 note] Completes humildemente, then adds lamenting or becoming sad. The printed mezquinha is one word, rather than the OCR split mez quinha.
[c1-l006] Vabixij. *Estar, ou ſer pobre, & miſerauel,*

[c1-l015 >] *mal. ¶ Item, Repartir, ou fazer differẽ-*

[c1-l019 >] *pobres.*

[c1-l020] Vacacuſa. *Eruas nouas.*
