[c1-l004 >] faruru. *Aclarar o tempo.*

[c1-l005 note] This ハレ is swelling, 腫れ, not clearing. テ アシガ starts an example involving hands and feet.

[c1-l007] Farebareto. *Modo deſe aſerenar o tempo. Vt,*

[c1-l011 note] With preceding Fare, the example is ハレ ホゥチャク スル, undergoing the substantial swelling just defined.

[c1-l013 >] *ſo, &c.*

[c1-l016] Farema. i, Faruru aida. *Eſpaço em que eſ-*
[c1-l016 note] ハレマ is a clear interval, expanded as ハルル アイダ. The Portuguese begins the same temporal definition.

[c1-l021] Farena. *Couſa publica, ou festiual.*

[c1-l022 note] ハレッ is 破裂, with final t retained. ヤブレ サクル explains breaking or splitting; the scan's Y and damaged b support Yabure, not unconvertible Talure. The b is partly supplied from context.

[c1-l029 >] *deſabafada.*
[c1-l029 note] Deſabafada completes the description of something open and unconfined; it is Portuguese continuation, not a separate headword.
[c1-l030] Fareyuqi, u, uita. *Ir aclarando o tempo, &c.*
[c1-l030 note] ハレユキ is progressively clearing. The listed uita is a historical past ending, not an independent word suggested by its isolated mechanical kana.

[c1-l031 note] アサギリノ ハレ ユク ママニ means as the morning mist gradually clears. The Portuguese comparison begins with Aſſi and continues below.

[c1-l032 note] “As the morning mist was dispersing” translates the preceding Japanese; pollamenhaã supplies the morning time reference in a historical spelling.
