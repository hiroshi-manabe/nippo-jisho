[c1-l001] Feicǒ. Cuchiuo tozzuru. *Fechara boca.*
[c1-l001 note] Heikou is closing the mouth, kuchi wo tozuru. The particle is uo without a long-o mark; the neighboring tall type had confused OCR.
[c1-l002 >] *¶ Item. Per met. Ficar vencido, ou con-*
[c1-l002 note] Per met. introduces the figurative sense: being defeated or silenced. Item is the editorial word, not lowercase ltem.
[c1-l003 >] *cluido na diſputa.*
[c1-l003 note] Concluido na disputa completes the figurative explanation: being overcome in a dispute rather than merely shutting the physical mouth.
[c1-l004] Feifacu. i, Fei. *Hũs papeis retalhados q̃ſer-*
[c1-l004 note] Heihaku, also hei, refers to cut-paper ritual offerings. The Portuguese papeis retalhados connects it with the preceding page's ritual-wand entry.
[c1-l005 >] *uem em hũa certa ceremonia diante do Cami.*
[c1-l005 note] The paper serves in a ceremony before the kami. Cami is Japanese embedded within the Portuguese sentence, not a Portuguese noun.
[c1-l006] Feifeito. *Modo de eſtar a couſa praina. ¶* Fei-
[c1-l006 note] Heiheito describes flatness or calmness. The sea example begins with a divided repetition of that expression.
[c1-l007 >] feito xita vmi. *Mar praino, & quieto. Vt,*
[c1-l007 note] Heiheito shita umi is a smooth, quiet sea, directly matched by mar praino e quieto.
[c1-l008 >] Feifeito xitaruno nacani ginuo fari, &c.
[c1-l008 note] Heifeito xitaruno nacani ginuo fari describes pitching a camp on level ground. The second scan pass restores the printed xitaruno, rather than shortening it to xita no, and reads ginuo with gi for ji; both are legitimate Japanese spellings here.
[c1-l009 >] Taif*. Lib. 30. Aſſentando o arrayal ẽ*
[c1-l009 note] Taif. is the Japanese source label, followed by Portuguese Lib. 30. Assentando o arrayal begins the translation of pitching camp.
[c1-l010 >] *hum campo largo, & raſo.*
[c1-l010 note] Hum campo largo e raso supplies the broad, level field in which the camp is established.
[c1-l011] Feifǒ. *Arte de eſgrima. ¶* Feifǒuo tçu-
[c1-l011 note] Heihou means fencing or martial technique here. The example repeats Feifou, not OCR Feitou; the scan supports f in both places.
[c1-l012 >] cǒ. *Vſar da arte de eſgrima.*
[c1-l012 note] Completes tsukou, 'use', in heihou wo tsukou. The Portuguese explicitly says practicing the art of fencing.
[c1-l013] Feifǒja. *Eſgrimidor.*
[c1-l013 note] Heihouja is a fencer; the following two entries give equivalent practitioner nouns. Fcirouja was an OCR corruption of the same heihou stem.
[c1-l014] Feifǒjin. *Idem.*
[c1-l014 note] Heihoujin is another practitioner noun; Idem refers to the preceding fencer definition, not to the art itself.
[c1-l015] Feifǒxa. *Idem.*
[c1-l015 note] Heihousha is likewise a fencer, with -sha marking the person. Preserve the printed sequence rather than modernizing all three synonyms.
[c1-l016] Feifucu. *Vestido roto, & velho.*
[c1-l016 note] Heifuku here denotes worn, torn clothing, as roto e velho specifies; a modern gloss of ordinary dress would miss this entry's sense.
[c1-l017] Feigi. Tairacana tçuchi. *Terra praina.*
[c1-l017 note] Heiji, tairakana tsuchi, is level ground. Bare gi gives the historical voiced palatal reading rather than hard gui.
[c1-l018 >] *¶* Feigini naſu. *A prainar, ou igualar*
[c1-l018 note] Heiji ni nasu means leveling or making the ground even. The Portuguese verb is visibly divided as A prainar.
[c1-l019 >] *a terra.*
[c1-l019 note] A terra completes the leveling example: the thing made even is the ground.
[c1-l020] Feigua. Tairacani fuſu. *Estar deitado.*
[c1-l020 note] Heigwa is lying down flat, tairakani fusu. The printed fu- belongs to the verb, not to long-s su-.
[c1-l021 >] *¶* Qinô yori vazzurǒ xǔ gozatte feigua
[c1-l021 note] Kinou yori wazurou shuu gozatte introduces being ill since yesterday. The weak letter after x is a caron-bearing u, giving xǔ rather than the OCR's xùi; the health and lying-down context helps resolve it.
[c1-l022 >] no teide gozaru. *Eſtando mal deſposto deſ*
[c1-l022 note] No tei de gozaru completes the description of being in this condition. The Portuguese starts 'being indisposed since...'.
[c1-l023 >] *dontem eſtou desta feição deitado.*
[c1-l023 note] Dontem supplies yesterday and completes the statement that the speaker is lying down in this manner.
[c1-l024] Feiguai. *Sem comedimento, & corteſia.*
[c1-l024 note] Heigwai means lacking restraint and courtesy. The same headword stem recurs in both examples below; cortesia was OCR corteſta.
[c1-l025 >] *¶* Feiguainamono. *Homem ſem primor,*
[c1-l025 note] Heigwai na mono is an uncouth or discourteous person, matched by homem sem primor.
[c1-l026 >] *& ſem corteſia. ¶* Feiguaina nari. *Mo-*
[c1-l026 note] Sem cortesia completes that description. Heigwai na nari introduces the person's manner or appearance.
[c1-l027 >] *do, ou feição de homem altiuo, & pouco*
[c1-l027 note] The Portuguese describes the manner of a haughty person with little courtesy, continuing the nari example.
[c1-l028 >] *cortes.*
[c1-l028 note] Cortes completes pouco cortes. This is a short Portuguese continuation rather than an independent lexical item.
[c1-l029] Feiguin. Tairacani fitoxij. *Paz, & con-*
[c1-l029 note] Heigin is equality or peaceful concord, glossed tairakani hitoshii. The final Japanese spelling is fitoxij: the terminal ij sequence expresses the long vowel, not the OCR's transposed ji.
[c1-l030 >] *cordia. ¶* Cuniuo feiguinni voſamu-
[c1-l030 note] Cuniuo feiguinni voſamuru means governing the country peacefully. The long-s reading fits osamuru and the enlarged printed letter, unlike votamuru; ru continues on the next line.
[c1-l031 >] ru. *Gouernar o reino pacificamente.*
[c1-l031 note] The Portuguese explicitly says governing the kingdom peacefully, completing the preceding Japanese example.
[c1-l032] Feiguiua. *Borda da parede, ou cerca.*
[c1-l032 note] Heigiwa is the edge of a wall or enclosure. Gui preserves the hard g in the compound's kiwa/giwa element.
[c1-l033] Feijei. i, Tçuneno coto. *Sempre, ou ordi-*
[c1-l033 note] Heizei, tsune no koto, means ordinarily or always. Ordinariamente is divided after ordi-.
[c1-l034 >] *nariamente.* Feijeina. *Couſa ordinaria.*
[c1-l034 note] Completes ordinariamente, then adds heizei na for something ordinary or usual.
[c1-l035] Feiji. Tçuuamono, votto. *Soldado. S.*
[c1-l035 note] Heiji here means soldier, with tsuwamono and otto as explanatory components. S marks the written register.
[c1-l036] Feiji. Taira vgi. i, Feiqeno ichimon. *Hũa*
[c1-l036 note] The homophone Heiji is the Taira lineage, explicitly identified as the Heike family. This is not the soldier entry continued.
[c1-l037 >] *familia nobre antigua de Iapão.*
[c1-l037 note] The Portuguese calls the Taira an ancient noble Japanese family, completing the preceding proper-name definition.
[c1-l038] Feiji. *Certa laja de bules de vinho feitos de pao.*
[c1-l038 note] Another heiji denotes wooden wine vessels. The vessel sense must remain distinct from both soldier and Taira clan.
[c1-l039] Feijimon. *Certafeição de portal de caſas prin-*
[c1-l039 note] Heijimon is a particular gateway form belonging to important residences; principais is split at the row boundary.
[c1-l040 >] *cipaes.*
[c1-l040 note] Completes principais, qualifying the houses whose gateways the entry describes.
[c1-l041] Feijin. Tairacana fito. i, Yonotçuneno fi-
[c1-l041 note] Heijin is an ordinary person, explained by tairakana hito and yo no tsune no hito. The last hito is divided.
[c1-l042 >] to. *Homem ordinario, & comum.*
[c1-l042 note] Completes hito and gives the Portuguese 'ordinary, common person'. This confirms that no new entry begins with to.
[c1-l043] Feimon. Monuo tozzuru. *Fechar a porta, ou*
[c1-l043 note] Heimon, mon wo tozuru, is closing the gate. The gloss also allows shutting oneself in.
[c1-l044 >] *trancarſe. Vt,* Feimonuo ſuru. *¶* Fei-
[c1-l044 note] Heimon wo suru illustrates that closure; the following divided example uses a causative form.
[c1-l045 >] monuo ſaſuru. *Fechar a alguem como em*
[c1-l045 note] Heimon wo sasuru means confining someone, compared in the gloss to placing them in prison.
[c1-l046 >] *priſão de maneira que não ſaya fora.*
[c1-l046 note] The confinement prevents the person going outside. Preserve historical saya rather than modernizing its spelling.
[c1-l047] Feiqe. Taira vgino iye. *Hũa gèração no-*
[c1-l047 note] Heike is the house of the Taira lineage. The noble-family gloss continues at the top of column 2; initial B was an OCR error.

[c2-l027] Fen. Fotori. *Borda, parte, ou lugar.*

[c2-l031 note] The division belongs to a writing or book. This closes the literary sense rather than the following character-component entry.
