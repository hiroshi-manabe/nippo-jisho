[c1-l012] Ichijiqi. Fitotabi xocuſu. *Iejum no qual ſe*
[c1-l012 note] Ichijiki is eating once, paraphrased hitotabi shokusu. The scan has Fitotabi, not OCR Fitotebi; the Portuguese describes a fast allowing a single meal.

[c1-l014 >] *Iejuar comendo hũa ſoo vez no dia.*

[c1-l015] Ichijit. *Verdade ſolida.* Bup.
