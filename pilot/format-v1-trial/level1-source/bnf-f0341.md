[c1-l001] Mura. *Vila, ou pouoação.*

[c1-l002] Mura. *Couſa feita ſem igualdade, & perfeição*
[c1-l002 note] The homographic Mura denotes unevenness or lack of uniform finish, rather than a village. The Portuguese begins a description of something made without equality and perfection.
[c1-l003 >] *como couſa tinta, ou pintada bem em hũa par-*
[c1-l003 note] Uneven dyeing or painting illustrates this second sense of Mura. The source preserves hũa and a visible par- division before the next row's te.
[c1-l004 >] *te, & mal noutra, &c. ¶* Qino mura-
[c1-l004 note] This completes the contrast between a well-finished part and a poorly finished one. The scan separates mal noutra; Qino mura- begins an example about removing unevenness from wood.

[c1-l005 note] Qino mura- / uo nuqu is rendered as working and planing the wood well. The final pao is the historical Portuguese spelling, not an instruction to introduce a modern accent.
[c1-l006 >] *que não fiquem altos, & baixos. ¶* Mura-

[c1-l010 note] This completes eſ- / peßas and contrasts thick clouds with thin ones. The doubled-s ligature is retained rather than expanded or normalized to a different pair of s types.
[c1-l011] Muradachi, tçu, atta. *Ajuntarſe, ou acumu-*

[c1-l013 >] juga muradatçu. *Ajuntarſe gente em ma-*
