[c1-l001] Fuxinauame. *Certo feitio, & tecedura de*
[c1-l001 note] Fuxinauame names a manner of weaving or lacing armour. The scan reads tecedura, not tecedira; this meaning makes the rope-pattern compound plausible.
[c1-l002 >] *armas.*
[c1-l002 note] Armas completes the armour definition. This is a genuine short continuation row, with no Japanese headword omitted.
[c1-l003] Fuxinqiû. *Hum certo arco pequeno.*
[c1-l003 note] Fuxinqiû is a small bow; the final vowel is u with a circumflex, not ô. The enlarged-looking type and NINJAL agree on this uncommon printed long-u variant.
[c1-l004] Fuxiqi. Xirazu. *Não ſaber. Bup.*
[c1-l004 note] Fuxiqi, xirazu, is not knowing, with a Buddhist label. Xira is the regular dictionary shi-ra sequence, not a modern spelling imposed.
[c1-l005] Fuxiqi. *Aruore caida com tormenta, ou de*
[c1-l005 note] A different fuxiqi is a tree fallen in a storm. Its physical-tree sense is distinct from the preceding Buddhist ignorance.
[c1-l006 >] *podre, & velha. ¶ Item, No Cami,*
[c1-l006 note] The tree can also have fallen from rot and age. Cami introduces a regional usage; the name is wholly italic rather than only its first syllable.
[c1-l007 >] Fuxiqi. *Aruore, ou pao de muitos noos.*
[c1-l007 note] In Cami, fuxiqi is a tree or timber with many knots. This second sense fits fushi, knot, rather than the falling sense alone.
[c1-l008] Fuxitauore, ruru, eta. *Cair no chão.*
[c1-l008 note] Fuxitauore is collapsing or falling to the ground. Cair is visible in the Portuguese, correcting the OCR's Cuir.
[c1-l009] Fuxiuabi, uru, ita. *Estar deitado com tris-*
[c1-l009 note] Fuxiuabi combines lying down and grieving or pleading. The historical uru/ita principal parts are preserved instead of replacing them with modern forms.
[c1-l010 >] *teza, ou pedir, & ſuplicar humildemente.*
[c1-l010 note] The gloss includes humbly begging and supplicating. The scan reads humildemente; the OCR's humeldemente is not retained.
[c1-l011] Fuxiuogami, u, ôda. *Proſtrandoſe adorar.*
[c1-l011 note] Fuxiuogami is worshipping while prostrate. The entire Japanese headword is Roman; internal italic runs were spurious, and the printed ôda is retained.
[c1-l012] Fuxiuore. *Quebradura polo nò como de aruo-*
[c1-l012 note] Fuxiuore is breaking at a joint, such as in a tree. It is a nominal fracture definition, not the earlier verb of bowing down.
[c1-l013 >] *re erua alta, &c.*
[c1-l013 note] Tall plants are included in the joint-breaking gloss. Erua remains the historical form, not modern erva.
[c1-l014] Fuxiutçubuqi, u, uita. *Deitarſe de bruços.*
[c1-l014 note] Fuxiutçubuqi is lying prone, with uita as the historical past abbreviation. Bruços confirms the posture; there is no reason to regularize the Japanese.
[c1-l015] Fuxixizzumi, u, ûda. *Estar deitado com*
[c1-l015 note] Fuxixizzumi is lying overwhelmed by sorrow. The worn principal part is ûda, supported by the remaining strokes and NINJAL; its u is not a Portuguese italic insertion.
[c1-l016 >] *grandetristeza. Vt,* Qinu fiqicazzuite,
[c1-l016 note] The example begins qinu fiqicazzuite, covering oneself with silk. The ca is damaged; the enlarged remnants and covering gloss support it over fiqitazzuite. Joined grandetristeza is retained.
[c1-l017 >] namidano yucani fuxixizzumarete,
[c1-l017 note] Namida no yuca ni is a floor or bed of tears; fuxixizzumarete is the respectful/passive-looking form. The worn c is retained as c, without inventing a different poetic noun.
[c1-l018 >] *Estando cuberta com hum mãto de ſeda eſta-*
[c1-l018 note] The Portuguese describes her covered with a silk mantle. The nasal contraction is mãto, not mẽto; the feminine cuberta is retained.
[c1-l019 >] *ua com grande triſteza, & lagrimas deitada.*
[c1-l019 note] She was lying in great grief and tears. This completes the silk-and-tears example, with the translation confirming the emotional rather than underwater sense.
[c1-l020 >] *¶* Vomoini fuxi xizzumu. *Estar ſumer-*
[c1-l020 note] The pilcrow begins another example, vomoi ni fuxi xizzumu, sinking under sorrow or longing. Its omission from OCR is repaired from the scan.
[c1-l021 >] *ſo com tristeza, ou derrubado, & triſte de*
[c1-l021 note] The Portuguese says submerged in sadness or cast down. Tristeza and derrubado restore the damaged but identifiable words; the u of derrubado is especially weak.
[c1-l022 >] *amor.*
[c1-l022 note] Amor completes the phrase: sadness caused by love. It qualifies the emotional example, not a separate headword.
[c1-l023] Fuxizzuqe. *O meter no fundo a alguem com*
[c1-l023 note] Fuxizzuqe is sinking someone with a weight. This dictionary definition records an action, without implying it is an instruction to perform it.
[c1-l024 >] *pedra ao peſcoço. Vt,* Fuxizzuqeni ſu-
[c1-l024 note] The stone is at the neck; fuxizzuqeni ſu- begins the corresponding verbal phrase. The Portuguese details confirm the immersion sense.
[c1-l025 >] ru. *Idem. ¶ Item, Meter canas, ou*
[c1-l025 note] ru completes suru, then Item shifts to placing reeds or branches underwater. The common feature is submerging material, not necessarily the first sense's violence.
[c1-l026 >] *ramos de aruore amarradas, & juntas dentro*
[c1-l026 note] The branches or reeds are tied together. Amarradas and juntas are kept as printed despite the intervening masculine ramos.
[c1-l027 >] *dagoa onde ſe recolhem os pexes com frio, &*
[c1-l027 note] The submerged material shelters fish from cold. Pexes and dagoa retain historical spelling and joining.
[c1-l028 >] *ſe tomão.*
[c1-l028 note] Se tomão ends the fish-catching explanation. The very short row is part of that definition, not page furniture.
[c1-l029] Fuxǒ. Vmarezu. *Não nacer. Vt,* Fuxǒ
[c1-l029 note] Fuxǒ is non-birth, paraphrased Vmarezu. The r is worn but recoverable in the enlargement; Vma ezu was an OCR omission and false space.
[c1-l030 >] fumet. *Não nacer, nem morrer.*
[c1-l030 note] Fuxǒ fumet is neither arising nor perishing. Final t is retained as historical notation, not completed with an imagined vowel.
[c1-l031] Fuxǒ. Saiuaini arazu. *Couſa que não ſuccede*
[c1-l031 note] Another fuxǒ is an unsuccessful or unwanted result, paraphrased saiuaini arazu. The dictionary distinguishes this sense from non-birth.
[c1-l032 >] *bem, nem à vontade dalguem. ¶* Fuxǒ-
[c1-l032 note] The event does not go well or according to someone's will. Fuxǒ- begins an adjectival example on the next row.
[c1-l033 >] na meni vǒ. *Encontrar com ruim ſucceſ-*
[c1-l033 note] Fuxǒna me ni vǒ is meeting misfortune. The contracted verb vǒ and the Portuguese encontrar agree; the accent is preserved.
[c1-l034 >] *ſo, ou trabalho, & deſgosto. ¶* Fuxǒ
[c1-l034 note] The gloss includes trouble and displeasure, then begins a reluctant-action phrase. The two senses are explicitly related in the entry.
[c1-l035 >] buxoni ſuru. *Fazer de maa vontade, ou*
[c1-l035 note] buxoni ſuru completes the reluctant phrase. The o in this occurrence lacks a clear mark, so no accent is supplied solely from the later fully accented headword.
[c1-l036 >] *com repugnancia algũa couſa.*
[c1-l036 note] Com repugnancia explains doing something unwillingly. The source's contracted algũa remains diplomatic rather than expanded.
[c1-l037] Fuxô. Monono cazuni arazu. *Não entrar*
[c1-l037 note] Fuxô is not counting among capable people, paraphrased mono no cazu ni arazu. The circumflex distinguishes this printed form from the preceding caron forms.
[c1-l038 >] *em numero. i, Não ter partes de letras ſa-*
[c1-l038 note] The gloss concerns lacking literary and intellectual accomplishments. The explanatory i, is Portuguese metalanguage, not Japanese.
[c1-l039 >] *ber, &c. ¶* Fuxôno mi naredomo.
[c1-l039 note] Fuxô no mi naredomo means although I am unworthy. Mi is self/body here; the quotation is a self-deprecating formula.
[c1-l040 >] *Posto que eu não entre em numero de gente,*
[c1-l040 note] Posto que introduces the concessive translation: although I do not count among people of worth. It should not be read as a physical location.
[c1-l041 >] *nem tenha partes, &c.*
[c1-l041 note] The speaker also disclaims accomplishments. This completes the modest formula and preserves partes in its older sense of abilities.
[c1-l042] Fuxôbuxǒni. *De mà vontade, ou contra*
[c1-l042 note] Fuxôbuxǒni is reluctantly, with two different visible long-o signs. The first circumflex was absent from OCR and is restored from the scan.
[c1-l043 >] *o deſejo, & goſto.*
[c1-l043 note] Acting contrary to desire and pleasure completes the reluctant-adverb gloss. No separate sense is inferred from the physical line break.
[c1-l044] Fuxocu. Xocuxezu. *Faſtio. ¶* Fuxocu
[c1-l044 note] Fuxocu is lack of appetite, glossed Xocuxezu, not eating. The clear compound shoku-sezu corrects OCR's Xucinxezu; faſtio independently confirms it.
[c1-l045 >] ſuru. *Ter faſtio.*
[c1-l045 note] Fuxocu suru means having no appetite. The Japanese example and Portuguese ter faſtio agree directly.
[c1-l046] Fuxôna, mata fuxǒna. *Vide* Fuxô, *&*
[c1-l046 note] Fuxôna and fuxǒna refer back to the two differently accented headwords. Both distinctions and the Japanese mata are retained.
[c1-l047 >] Fuxǒ.
[c1-l047 note] The second cross-reference is Fuxǒ, with a weak but visible upper mark. It is not the short-vowel Fuxo produced by OCR.

[c2-l001] Fuxubi. Xubixezu. *Diſcrepancia. Vt,*
[c2-l001 note] Fuxubi, xubixezu, is inconsistency: beginning and end do not agree. The Portuguese discrepancia and the following promise example confirm the interpretation.
[c2-l002 >] Fuxubina cotouo mǒſu. *Dizer couſas*
[c2-l002 note] Fuxubina coto wo mǒsu is saying inconsistent things. Mǒsu's caron is visible and the verb remains respectful speech.
[c2-l003 >] *que não concordão, como quando hum prome-*
[c2-l003 note] The things said fail to agree; the example starts with someone promising. This is explanatory context, not a second Japanese quotation.
[c2-l004 >] *teo algũa couſa, & de pois moſtra arrepen-*
[c2-l004 note] After promising something, the person shows regret. De pois remains visibly separated; it is not modernized to depois.
[c2-l005 >] *derſe.*
[c2-l005 note] derſe completes arrependerſe, to regret. The line break does not terminate the example prematurely.
[c2-l006] Fuye. Yaburazu. *Não ſe deſtruir, ou des*
[c2-l006 note] Fuye, yaburazu, is not breaking or being destroyed. The printed des at the edge has no compulsory added hyphen.
[c2-l007 >] *fazer. Vt,* Congǒ fuye. *O ouro fino*
[c2-l007 note] Congǒ fuye is the unbreakable substance expression, glossed here as fine gold. This gloss is retained even though kongō has broader Buddhist associations.
[c2-l008 >] *não ſe desfaz nem destrue por mais que o ba-*
[c2-l008 note] The gold is not destroyed however much it is beaten. Destrue and desfaz are preserved as source forms.
[c2-l009 >] *tão, &c.*
[c2-l009 note] tão completes batão. The nasal verb ending belongs to the beating example, not a new word.
[c2-l010] Fuye. *Garganta.*
[c2-l010 note] Fuye is throat, a homophone of the flute sense below. Garganta is clear and does not warrant collapsing the entries.
[c2-l011] Fuye. *Frauta. Vt,* Fuyeuo fuqu. *Tanger*
[c2-l011 note] Fuye is flute; Frauta, not Frauia, is the visible Portuguese word. Fuye wo fuqu means blowing/playing it.
[c2-l012 >] *a frauta. ¶* Tçuzzumiuo naraxi, fu-
[c2-l012 note] The example combines sounding the tçuzzumi drum with the flute. Its second object continues fu-/ye across rows.
[c2-l013 >] yeuo fuqitatetareba, *&c.* Tai. *L. 28.*
[c2-l013 note] fuqitatetareba continues the sounding-up construction. The citation reads Tai. L. 28.; Tai. is Roman, L. 28. italic, and no absent f is added from the more usual Taif.
[c2-l014 >] *Tangendo o tabaquinho, & a frauta.*
[c2-l014 note] The drum-and-flute performance is translated directly. Tabaquinho is retained as the diminutive instrument term printed here.
[c2-l015] Fuyeqi. Cauarazu. *Não ſe mudar. ¶* Fu-
[c2-l015 note] Fuyeqi, cauarazu, is not changing. The ye represents the Japanese vowel sequence convention, not an extra modern syllable.
[c2-l016 >] yeqina coto. *Couſa immudauel, & per-*
[c2-l016 note] Fuyeqina coto is an immutable thing. The Portuguese immudauel spelling is historical and remains unchanged.
[c2-l017 >] *manente.*
[c2-l017 note] manente finishes permanente, reinforcing lasting immutability. This short continuation is not a separate Japanese form.
[c2-l018] Fuyet. Vono maſacari. *Machado.*
[c2-l018 note] Fuyet is an axe, paraphrased Vono maſacari. Vono gives ono, and the scan's o corrects the OCR's Vuno.
[c2-l019] Fuyô. *Flor aßi chamada.*
[c2-l019 note] Fuyô is the flower fuyō. The simple gloss does not justify adding a botanical species to the transcription.
[c2-l020] Fuyô. *Priguiça. ¶* Fuyôuo camayuru.
[c2-l020 note] Another Fuyô denotes laziness, with fuyô wo camayuru. The same long-o spelling need not imply the flower meaning.
[c2-l021 >] *Ter priguiça.*
[c2-l021 note] Ter priguiça confirms laziness. The source's initial pri- is retained rather than changed to modern pre-.
[c2-l022] Fuyo. Cubari, atayuru. *Repartir, & dar.*
[c2-l022 note] Fuyo, with no long-o mark, is bestowing or distributing: cubari, atayuru. This is a meaning-bearing contrast with the previous long-vowel forms.
[c2-l023] Fuyôna. *Priguiçoſo, ou couſa de priguiça.*
[c2-l023 note] Fuyôna is the lazy adjective. The derivation and Portuguese priguiçoſo agree, and the circumflex is clearly printed.
[c2-l024] Fuyù. *Hum certo bicho.*
[c2-l024 note] Fuyù is a certain insect, distinct from short-vowel winter below. The grave-like u sign is retained and also occurs in the reference headword.
[c2-l025] Fuyu. *Inuerno. ¶* Fuyuni naru, l, fu-
[c2-l025 note] Fuyu is winter. Fuyu ni naru begins one of two expressions for its arrival.
[c2-l026 >] yu qitaru. *Vir, ou chegarſe o inuerno.*
[c2-l026 note] fuyu qitaru supplies the alternative, winter comes. Qitaru corresponds to kitaru, and the seasonal meaning is explicit.
[c2-l027] Fuyudachi, u. *Vir oinuerno.*
[c2-l027 note] Fuyudachi is winter setting in. The Portuguese oinuerno is joined in the source, not automatically separated.
[c2-l028] Fuyugomori, u, otta. *Fazer aparelho, &*
[c2-l028 note] Fuyugomori means making winter preparations here. Its verbal paradigm is retained, rather than restricting the term to modern hibernation.
[c2-l029 >] *apercebimento pera o inuerno.*
[c2-l029 note] Apercebimento is preparing/provisioning for winter. The worn m is supported by the intact repetition below; the OCR's n and false internal space are corrected.
[c2-l030] Fuyugomori. *Vt,* Fuyugomoriuo ſuru.
[c2-l030 note] Fuyugomori is repeated as a noun with suru. The first u is clearly present, correcting Fayugomori.
[c2-l031 >] *Fazer este aparelho, ou apercebimento como*
[c2-l031 note] The preparations or supplies are explained further. The same apercebimento corroborates the damaged occurrence two rows above.
[c2-l032 >] *de lenha, vestido, &c. pera o inuerno.*
[c2-l032 note] Firewood and clothing are examples of winter supplies. Vestido is preserved rather than replaced with a modern plural.
[c2-l033] Fuzacaxi, ſu, aita. *Amolentar algũa couſa*
[c2-l033 note] Fuzacaxi means softening something by soaking, not the modern joking sense suggested by fuzakeru. The following water gloss governs the interpretation.
[c2-l034 >] *nagoa demaſiadamente.*
[c2-l034 note] Nagoa demaſiadamente specifies excessive immersion. The joined preposition is retained as printed.
[c2-l035] Fuzaqe, uru, eta. *Estar muito tempo na agoa,*
[c2-l035 note] Fuzaqe is becoming damaged after too long in water. Its historical uru/eta paradigm is not modernized.
[c2-l036 >] *& danarſe, ou apodrecer como bate, ma*
[c2-l036 note] Bate and ma-/mes are food examples of rotting or spoiling. Bate is retained as visibly printed; this is not enough evidence to replace it with a more familiar word.
[c2-l037 >] *mes, arroz cozido, &c. No Ximo ſe diz.*
[c2-l037 note] Beans and cooked rice continue the food list. Ximo introduces the lower-region variant, with the visible capital X restored.
[c2-l038 >] Fozaqe, uru, eta.
[c2-l038 note] Fozaqe is the stated regional form. Its o is meaningful dialect evidence and is not normalized to Fuzaqe.
[c2-l039] Fuyuzare. *P. i,* Fuyunonaca. *Meo do*
[c2-l039 note] Fuyuzare is marked poetic and glossed fuyu no naca, midwinter. P. and explanatory i, are italic labels, while the Japanese remains Roman.
[c2-l040 >] *inuerno.*
[c2-l040 note] Inuerno completes midwinter. The preceding poetic label does not change this into another season.
[c2-l041] Fuzô. Maſazu. *Não exceder. Vt,* Fuzô
[c2-l041 note] Fuzô, maſazu, means not increasing. Vt, is italic as a whole; a stray Roman V had generated a false kana hint.
[c2-l042 >] fuguen. *Não crecer nem minguar.*
[c2-l042 note] Fuzô fuguen is neither increasing nor diminishing, 不増不減. The final n of gen is present and belongs to the compound.
[c2-l043] Fuzocu. Tçuqe, tçugu. *O entregar a he-*
[c2-l043 note] Fuzocu is handing over an inheritance or household. The first Portuguese fragment is he-, not be-, as confirmed by herança below.
[c2-l044 >] *rança, ou caſa. Vt,* Voyano ixxeqiuo
[c2-l044 note] The example begins voya no ixxeqi wo, the parent's hereditary place or estate. The inherited-property sense supports this Japanese expression.
[c2-l045 >] coni fuzocuſuru. *Deixar o pay a herança*
[c2-l045 note] coni fuzocuſuru transfers it to the child. The Portuguese reads pay, not poy, and states the father's bequest.
[c2-l046 >] *ao filho. ¶ Item, O communicar ſaber, dou-*
[c2-l046 note] Item extends the meaning to communicating knowledge. The faint c in communicar is recovered from the scan and the unmistakable syntax.
[c2-l047 >] *trina, ou arte a outros.*
[c2-l047 note] trina completes doutrina, followed by art conveyed to others. The first letter is t, not OCR's s; signature and catchword below are separate furniture.
