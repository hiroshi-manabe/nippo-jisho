[c1-l011] Sucure, uru, eta. *Meliùs,* Sucumi, u. *Vt,*
[c1-l011 note] Sucure is the verb for becoming stiff or cramped; Meliùs recommends Sucumi as the preferable form. The abbreviated principal parts remain as printed.
[c1-l012 >] Vmano axiga ſucureta. *Eſtarem os pes do*

[c1-l013 >] *caualo como hirtos, ou tropegos de não andar,*
[c1-l013 note] Completes the subject, the horse, and describes its feet as stiff or halting from lack of movement. The visible letters support caualo, not OCR caialo; the row is a continuation.
[c1-l014 >] *ou eſtar muito tempo na eſtrebaria ſem ſajr.*
[c1-l014 note] The alternative cause is a long stay in the stable without going out. The historical eſtrebaria and ſajr are preserved rather than modernized.
[c1-l015] Sucuyacana. *Peßoa forte, ou eſforçada.*
[c1-l015 note] Sucuyacana describes a strong or vigorous person. The Portuguese word is Peßoa despite worn spacing around its last letters; the Japanese historical vowel sequence is retained.
[c1-l016 >] Sucuyacani. Sucuyacaſa.
[c1-l016 note] These supply the adverbial and nominal forms of the preceding adjective: vigorously and vigor or robustness. They are Japanese forms, not embedded Portuguese terms.
[c1-l017] Sudachi. tçu, da ta. *Sairem os paßaros do*

[c1-l018 >] *ninho a primeira vez.*

[c1-l019] Sudachi. *Vt,* Sudachiuo ſuru. *Idem.*

[c1-l021] Sudaqi, u. *Cantarem os bichinhos juntos co-*

[c1-l023] Sudare. *Eſteira rala que ſe dependura às portas*

[c1-l029 >] *Eſteue ja quaſi a ponto de morrer.*

[c1-l030] Sudeni faya. *Idem.*

[c1-l041 >] *bebedarſe.*
