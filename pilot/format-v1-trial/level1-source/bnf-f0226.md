[c1-l003] Fuqi, u, uita. *Vt,* Iye, l, yaneuo fuqu.
[c1-l003 note] Fuqi here is roofing: iye or yaneuo fuqu, to roof a house. The abbreviated u principal part is retained rather than editorially expanded to qu.
[c1-l004 >] *Telhar, ou cobrir a caſa.*
[c1-l004 note] Telhar, ou cobrir a caſa confirms the roofing sense, distinct from the blowing entry below. The entire short line fits its crop.
[c1-l005] Fuqi, u, uita. *Ventar, ou aſoprar. ¶* Vſo

[c1-l006 note] Vſouo fuqu is whistling, Aſuuiar. Caiuo fuqu then introduces blowing a shell; cai is historical kai.

[c1-l009 note] Fuqiacaxi is fuki-akashi, blowing or playing through the night. The generated kana's potential qi+a contraction must not obscure this compound boundary.
