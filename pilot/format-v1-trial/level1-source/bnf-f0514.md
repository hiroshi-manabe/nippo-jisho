[c2-l001] Toqifodoqi, qu, oita. *Deſempeçar, ou de*
[c2-l001 note] Tokihodoki means untangling or untying. [c2-l003 note] Completes the gloss as disentangling the hairs of the head. This specifies the bodily meaning of kami in the preceding example.
[c2-l004] Toqiguxi. *Pentẽ ralo pera pentear os cabelos.*
[c2-l004 note] Tokigushi is a comb with widely spaced teeth for combing hair. Pentẽ retains the printed abbreviation mark, without supplying an unprinted final consonant.
[c2-l005] Toqimeqi, u, eita. *Ser tempo, & conjun-*
[c2-l005 note] Tokimeki describes the season or opportunity in which something flourishes. The printed principal forms u and eita are retained; the Portuguese division is conjun- followed by ção.
[c2-l006 >] *ção em que florece algũa couſa, como tempo das*

[c2-l007 >] *flores em que todos ſaem a ver, & recrearſe.*

[c2-l010] Toqimeqi ayeri, u, etta. S. *Concorrer*
[c2-l010 note] Tokimeki ayeri describes people gathering in celebration during a period of a household’s prosperity. S. is the upright written-usage label, separate from the italic Portuguese gloss.

[c2-l012 >] *folgar, ou auiſitarſe por ſer tempo em que os*
[c2-l012 note] Continues with celebrating or visiting because the household is enjoying favorable times. The scan reads folgar, with a, rather than OCR folgor; auiſitarſe retains the printed joining.
[c2-l013 >] *daquella familia florecem em proſperidade,*

[c2-l014 >] *riquezas, priuança, &c.*
