[c1-l020] Nominarazu. *Não ſomente, mas ainda.*

[c1-l028 >] *que esta em exemplo das fabulas não ſe vſa*

[c1-l029 >] *neſte ſentido ordinariamente.*

[c1-l030] Nomitçucuxi, ſu, uita. *Bebendo eſgotar.*

[c1-l031] Nomite. *Bebedor, ou o que bebe muito vinho.*

[c1-l046 note] Compares the Japanese call to saying oula, oula. This is the dictionary's Portuguese interjectional comparison, not an instruction to normalize the Japanese vowels.

[c1-l047 note] Nonomeki denotes bustle, commotion, or noisy activity. The last fa- continues with zerſe at the top of column 2, establishing the column-to-column reading order.
