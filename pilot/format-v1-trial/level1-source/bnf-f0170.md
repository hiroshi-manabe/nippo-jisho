[c1-l046 note] The dictionary reports a belief attributed to people in China. Cuidauão frames it as what they thought they saw, not as the narrator's assertion that the phenomenon occurred.
[c1-l047 >] *as almas dos defuntos.*
[c1-l047 note] The supposed visions were of the souls of deceased people. This completes the historical explanation of Fangoncǒ; the line contains no new Japanese headword.
