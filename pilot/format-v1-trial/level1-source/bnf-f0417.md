[c1-l001] Reôſocu. i, Ieni. *Caixas, ou moedas de cobre.*
[c1-l001 note] Ryosoku denotes money, here copper cash or coins. The gloss reads moedas, not mordas. The damaged initial remains a nonblocking uncertainty.
[c1-l002] Reô. Sunadori. *O caçar, & peſcar.*
[c1-l002 note] The entry equates ryo with sunadori and covers both hunting and fishing. The circumflex on Reô is retained as a graphic distinction, not normalized from the modern word.
[c1-l003] Reoto. Tabino michi. *Caminho que hum*
[c1-l003 note] Tabino michi identifies the road or course of a journey. The final vowel of michi is i, not OCR michu; the Portuguese relative clause continues below.
[c1-l004 >] *faz andando por fora, ou peregrinando.*
[c1-l004 note] Completes the description of a route taken while away from home or travelling as a pilgrim. It belongs to Reoto, not to a new entry.

[c1-l007] Reôxi. *Caçador, ou peſcador.*

[c1-l008] Reôxi. *Papel.*
[c1-l008 note] This separate homographic entry means paper, not a hunter. Papel is clearly legible, correcting Paprl; the mark on the headword is read as a circumflex.
[c1-l009] Reǒxo. Fut tocoro. *Dous lugares. ¶ Itẽ,*
[c1-l009 note] The first sense is two places; the following row adds two people. The Japanese gloss corresponds to futa tokoro, but the internal letters and spacing of the short printed form require retention rather than automatic expansion.
[c1-l010 >] *Duas peßoas.*

[c1-l011] Reoxo. *Lugar, onde ſe hum acha fora de*

[c1-l012 >] *ſua caſa.*
[c1-l012 note] Completes fora de sua casa, specifying that the place is outside one's own home. This short continuation is a full physical row.
[c1-l013] Reôxo. *Terras, ou renda propria dalgum ſe-*
[c1-l013 note] This entry concerns a lord's own lands or income. The headword's circumflex is compared with the repeated goreôxo below; the Portuguese ſe- begins senhor across the line break.
[c1-l014 >] *nhor, que tem reſeruada pera ſeu vſo, &*

[c1-l015 >] *gaſto. ¶* Dairi goreôxo. *Renda, ou*

[c1-l018] Reǒxu. Futacuſa. *Duas layas de couſas.*

[c1-l021] Reoxucu. Tabino yadori. *Eſtalagem, ou*

[c1-l023] Reôyen. Farucani touoi. *Grande diſtancia.*

[c1-l026] Reôyǒ. *Curar doença, & ter cuidado de ſua*

[c1-l030 >] *te aſaude.*

[c1-l031] Reqi. Coyomi. *Calendario dos dias, meſes,*
