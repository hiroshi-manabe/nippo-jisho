## column-1 [column] Column 1

[c1-l001 >] *Nem entendia o que lhe falauão.*
[c1-l001 note] Translates the quotation at the end of the preceding page: the person did not understand what was said to him. Nem is running text here, not a second catchword.
[c1-l002] Qiquai. i, Fuxiguina coto. *Couſa que cau-*
[c1-l002 note] Fushigi na koto explains a strange or extraordinary matter. The Portuguese causar is divided at the line end.
[c1-l003 >] *ſa eſpanto, ou duuida. ¶ Item, Couſa que*
[c1-l003 note] Completes the sense of causing astonishment or doubt and begins a further explanation involving personal unease.
[c1-l004 >] *toca de que hum ſe toma, ou deſconfia, &c.*
[c1-l004 note] Continues the source's wording about something that affects a person or arouses distrust. Deſconfia has f, not the OCR's long s; the unusual toca wording is not rewritten.
[c1-l005] Qira. *Couſa limpa, & luſtroſa. ¶* Qira-
[c1-l005 note] Kira concerns brightness or splendor. The example begins Qira- in roman type; the draft's italic O was a misread Q.
[c1-l006 >] uo migaqu furumai. *Conuite marauilhoſo,*
[c1-l006 note] Completes kira wo migaku furumai, explained as an impressive feast or entertainment; the Portuguese qualification continues below.
[c1-l007 >] *& bem feito. ¶* Qirauo nigaita xitacu.
[c1-l007 note] Finishes the feast gloss and gives a phrase for splendid attire. The scan appears to read nigaita, although the preceding migaqu and the sense suggest migaita; the apparent printed n is retained provisionally.
[c1-l008 >] *Trajo lustroſo.*
[c1-l008 note] Translates the preceding phrase as splendid dress. The provisional st allograph is retained without a global typography substitution.
[c1-l009] Qira. *Hũa laya de peças finas.*
[c1-l009 note] A separate kira entry describes a kind of fine fabric or textile piece, not the preceding abstract splendor.
[c1-l010] Qirabiyacana. *Couſa limpa, & luſtroſa. ¶* Qi-
[c1-l010 note] Kirabiyakana means bright or splendid. The example repeats the adjective across the physical line break.
[c1-l011 >] rabiyacana ninju. *Gente luzida, & lu-*
[c1-l011 note] Describes a splendidly presented group of people, with the Portuguese adverb continuing on the following row.
[c1-l012 >] *stroſamente veſtida.*
[c1-l012 note] Completes lustrosamente and explains that the people are splendidly dressed.
[c1-l013] Qiracu. Miyacoye cayeru. *Tornada ao*
[c1-l013 note] Miyako e kaeru explains a return to the capital. The Portuguese place-name is deferred to the next physical row.
[c1-l014 >] *Miyaco.*
[c1-l014 note] Miyaco completes the Portuguese return-to-capital gloss. Although alone on this row, it belongs within that explanation and qualifies for whole-word typeface review.
[c1-l015] Qirai. Vye qitaru. *Tendo, ou auendo fome.*
[c1-l015 note] This kirai entry concerns hunger coming upon someone, distinct from the following dislike verb.
[c1-l016 >] *Vt,* Qirai, qippan. *Tendo fome comer.*
[c1-l016 note] The example pairs becoming hungry with eating, explicitly confirmed by the Portuguese translation.
[c1-l017] Qirai, ǒ, ǒta. *Ter aſco, ou recuſar.*
[c1-l017 note] Gives the principal forms of kirau, to dislike or reject. The two abbreviated endings carry carons.
[c1-l018] Qirai. *Aſco, ou o recuſar.*
[c1-l018 note] Gives the corresponding noun for aversion or refusal, separately from the inflected verb above.
[c1-l019] Qirameqi, qu, meita. *Reluzir, ou estar*
[c1-l019 note] Kirameki means shining or gleaming. The damaged opening character is Q rather than OCR G, consistent with the visible neighboring initials.
[c1-l020 >] *liſo, & nedeo como couſa bem vruxada.*
[c1-l020 note] Specifies a smooth, sleek surface like a well-burnished object. The unusual historical vruxada is preserved.
[c1-l021] Qiransǒ. *Erua aßi chamada.*
[c1-l021 note] Names an herb without further description. No modern species identification is inserted into the transcription or gloss.
[c1-l022] Qiraqeiqiǔ. *Veſtidos brandos de peças finas,*
[c1-l022 note] Describes soft clothing made of fine materials; the long compound is retained with its printed final-u caron.
[c1-l023 >] *& de pelles, ou lãa branda, & molle.*
[c1-l023 note] Adds skins or soft wool as materials, retaining the printed lãa and doubled l of molle.
[c1-l024] Qiraqinxǔ. *Veſtidos ricos de ſeda, & ou-*
[c1-l024 note] Describes rich silk clothing and other luxurious fabrics. The Portuguese outras begins across the line break.
[c1-l025 >] *tras peças como brocadilho, &c.*
[c1-l025 note] Completes outras and gives brocade-like fabric as an example of the rich materials.
[c1-l026] Qiraqirato. *Adu. Modo de reluzir, ou*
[c1-l026 note] Kirakirato is an adverb describing sparkling. The Portuguese reads Modo, not the OCR's Medo; Adu. is italic apparatus.
[c1-l027 >] *reſplandecer de longe.*
[c1-l027 note] Completes the description as shining or gleaming from a distance.
[c1-l028] Qirara. *Hũa laya de criſtal que ſe tira ẽ algũs*
[c1-l028 note] Kirara is described as a crystal-like substance obtained from mountains. The abbreviated ẽ and algũs are preserved without expansion.
[c1-l029 >] *montes, mas mais brando, & facil de diuidir q̃*
[c1-l029 note] Distinguishes the material as softer and easier to divide than ordinary crystal; the comparison continues below.
[c1-l030 >] *o criſtal.*
[c1-l030 note] Finishes the mineral comparison. The source's physical description is retained rather than replaced by a modern mineral name.
[c1-l031] Qiratame, uru, eta. *Iulgar, ou diſcernir.*
[c1-l031 note] Kiratame means judging or discerning. The scan supports Iulgar and diſcernir, correcting two letter losses or confusions in the draft.
[c1-l032 >] *¶* Cono guiuo yocu qiratamete cudaſa-
[c1-l032 note] Begins a polite request to examine or settle the matter thoroughly. The honorific request ending is divided after cudaſa-.
[c1-l033 >] rei. *Peço vos que tireis iſto alimpo, & o a-*
[c1-l033 note] Completes kudasarei; the Portuguese asks for clarification. Peço and tireis replace OCR Pego and tirets, while alimpo remains attached as printed.
[c1-l034 >] *clareis.*
[c1-l034 note] Completes aclareis, finishing the request to clarify the matter.
[c1-l035] Qire, uru. *Quebrarſe fio, corda, &c.*
[c1-l035 note] Kireru is defined through a thread or cord breaking. Corda corrects the OCR's conda.
[c1-l036 >] *¶ Per met.* Nacaga qiruru. *Quebrarſe*
[c1-l036 note] Introduces the metaphorical expression naka ga kiruru: a relationship breaks off. The Portuguese object follows below.
[c1-l037 >] *a amizade. ¶* Vmaga qiruru. *Deſui-*
[c1-l037 note] Completes the broken-friendship example, then introduces a horse veering away. The Portuguese desviar-se is physically divided.
[c1-l038 >] *arſe o caualo do caminho eſpantandoſe, quando*
[c1-l038 note] Explains that the horse leaves the path when startled, with the circumstances continued below.
[c1-l039 >] *vai correndo, ou andando. Vt,* Cono v-
[c1-l039 note] Specifies a running or walking horse and starts the Japanese example, dividing uma after its initial v.
[c1-l040 >] maua qireyaſui vmagia. *Eſte caualo he*
[c1-l040 note] Completes kono uma wa kireyasui uma ja, a horse prone to shying. Maua and qireyaſui correct OCR mana and qiteyaſui.
[c1-l041 >] *eſpantadiço, & deſuiaſe, ou ſalta facilmente*
[c1-l041 note] Describes the horse as skittish and liable to veer or leap aside easily.
[c1-l042 >] *fora do caminho.*
[c1-l042 note] Completes the example by specifying movement off the path.
[c1-l043] Qire, uru, eta. *Gretarem os pees, mãos,*
[c1-l043 note] A separate kireru entry concerns chapping or cracking of feet and hands. The source has pees with two e types.
[c1-l044 >] *&c, com frio. ¶ Item, Ser cortado com*
[c1-l044 note] Attributes the cracking to cold, then adds the sense of being cut by a weapon.
[c1-l045 >] *Catana, &c.*
[c1-l045 note] Catana completes the Portuguese explanation of being cut by a sword; it is not a new Japanese headword.
[c1-l046] Qire, uru, eta. *Cortar a faca, naualha, &c.*
[c1-l046 note] Defines the cutting effectiveness of a knife or razor. Cortar replaces OCR Certar, and the headword is restored to flush-left placement.
[c1-l047 >] *¶* Cono camiſoriua qirenu. *Eſtas naua-*
[c1-l047 note] Kono kamisori wa kirenu says these razors do not cut. The negative qirenu and the preceding word boundary replace the garbled OCR; the translation continues in column 2.

[c2-l001 >] *lhas não cortam, ou rapam mal.*
[c2-l001 note] Completes naualhas from column 1: the razors do not cut or shave poorly. This continuation remains at the top of column 2.
[c2-l002] Qire. *Pedacinho de qualquer couſa.*
[c2-l002 note] Kire is a small piece of anything, a noun distinct from the preceding cutting verbs.
[c2-l003] Qireguire. *Pedacinhos.*
[c2-l003 note] The reduplicated kiregire is glossed as small pieces in the plural.
[c2-l004] Qirei. i, Iſaguiyoi coto. *Limpeza.*
[c2-l004 note] Isagiyoi koto explains cleanness or purity. The opening Q corrects OCR D without changing the printed Japanese synonym.
[c2-l005] Qireina. *Couſa limpa.*
[c2-l005 note] Gives the adjectival form kireina, describing a clean thing.
[c2-l006 >] Qireini.
[c2-l006 note] Supplies the associated adverbial form kireini. The scan has no separate Portuguese gloss on this indented row, so none is invented.
[c2-l007 >] Qireiſa.
[c2-l007 note] Supplies the abstract-noun form kireisa, again without a printed gloss. The short indented row is intentional, not omitted text.
[c2-l008] Qiri. *Souela, ou furador, ou verruma de*
[c2-l008 note] Kiri is an awl or boring tool. The enlarged tool-name has two r types followed by u and m: verruma, rather than the OCR's veruna.
[c2-l009 >] *Iapão.*
[c2-l009 note] Completes the description of the Japanese boring tool. Iapão is Portuguese and is not marked as an embedded Japanese expression.
[c2-l010] Qiri. *Neuoa, ou chuua miuda.*
[c2-l010 note] A separate kiri means mist or fine rain. Chuua has two u types, correcting the draft's chua.
[c2-l011] Qiri, u, itta. *Cortar. ¶* Yomi qiru.
[c2-l011 note] Gives kiru, to cut, and the compound yomi kiru. The initial Q replaces OCR G; the example is explained on the following row.
[c2-l012 >] *Ler atè certo lugar, & não ir por diante.*
[c2-l012 note] Explains reading up to a particular point and not continuing beyond it, preserving this historical sense of the compound.
[c2-l013 >] *¶* Fudeuo caqiqiru. *Gastar a pena toda*
[c2-l013 note] Fude wo kakikiru is using up the writing implement through writing. The Portuguese object continues below.
[c2-l014 >] *com eſcreuer. ¶* Iyqiru. l, mǒxi qiru.
[c2-l014 note] Completes the writing example and introduces saying something decisively. Eſcreuer corrects the OCR's final a; Iyqiru retains its printed joined form.
[c2-l015 >] *Dizer reſolutamente. E geralmente eſte verbo*
[c2-l015 note] Translates the speech example as speaking resolutely, then begins an explicit general explanation of compound kiru.
[c2-l016 >] *junto com a raiz de outros ſignifica fazerſe de*
[c2-l016 note] States that attaching this verb to another verb's stem can indicate a complete action; the qualification continues below.
[c2-l017 >] *todo, ou acabarſe perfeitamente algũa couſa.*
[c2-l017 note] Completes the grammatical explanation as doing something wholly or finishing it perfectly.
[c2-l018] Qiriai, ǒ, ǒta. *Andar às cutiladas, ou pe-*
[c2-l018 note] Kiriai denotes exchanging sword blows. The Portuguese pelejando begins at the physical line edge.
[c2-l019 >] *lejando hũs com os outros na batalha, &c.*
[c2-l019 note] Completes the description of fighting one another in battle. Pelejando and batalha correct OCR vowel and consonant confusions.
[c2-l020] Qiriame. *Chuua miuda.*
[c2-l020 note] Kiriame denotes fine rain or drizzle; the short entry stands independently between cutting compounds.
[c2-l021] Qiriaqe, uru, eta. *Abrir cortando. ¶* Mi-
[c2-l021 note] Kiriake means opening by cutting. The example begins michi, road, divided across rows.
[c2-l022 >] chi. l, roxiuo qiriaquru. *Cortando o ma*
[c2-l022 note] Completes michi and gives an alternative road-word before kiriakuru. The Portuguese mato begins after the Japanese example, without a supplied hyphen.
[c2-l023 >] *to, ou eruas abrir caminho.*
[c2-l023 note] To completes Portuguese mato, brushwood, not a Japanese word. The entire row is italic: clearing brush or herbs opens a path.
[c2-l024] Qiriauaxe, ſuru, eta. *Cortando igualar, ou*
[c2-l024 note] Kiriawase concerns equalizing or fitting things together by cutting; the Portuguese alternative continues below.
[c2-l025 >] *ajuntar.*
[c2-l025 note] Completes the joining sense. The descender of j is visible in ajuntar, correcting OCR auntar.
[c2-l026] Qiriban. *Trincho, ou taboa ſobre que cortão o*
[c2-l026 note] Kiriban is a chopping board, described as the board on which food is cut.
[c2-l027 >] *comer fora da meſa.*
[c2-l027 note] Completes the description as preparing food away from the dining table. Fora has f rather than the draft's long s.
[c2-l028] Qiricacari, ru, atta. *Arremeter, ou ſaltar*
[c2-l028 note] Kirikakari means rushing at someone with a cutting attack; the Portuguese object follows below.
[c2-l029 >] *com alguem cortando. Vt,* Teqini qiri-
[c2-l029 note] Completes the attack gloss and begins teki ni kirikakaru, striking at an enemy.
[c2-l030 >] cacaru.
[c2-l030 note] Completes the divided verb kirikakaru. This short Japanese continuation has no independent Portuguese gloss.
[c2-l031] Qiricachi, tçu, atta. *Vencer por armas.*
[c2-l031 note] Kirikachi is prevailing by force of arms. The initial syllable is Qiri, not the OCR's Quri.
[c2-l032] Qiricami. *Eſcrito, ou carta breue.*
[c2-l032 note] Kirikami is a short written message or letter. The scan corrects the opening Q and the Portuguese words Eſcrito and carta.
[c2-l033] Qiricaqe, uru, eta. *Cortando botar encima,*
[c2-l033 note] Kirikake concerns cutting and placing something upon another surface. The initial Q replaces the OCR's G.
[c2-l034 >] *como terra, &c. ¶* Cubiuo qiricaquru.
[c2-l034 note] Gives earth as one material, then begins the separate example kubi wo kirikakuru, explained below.
[c2-l035 >] *Cortando a cabeça dalguem pola, ou depen-*
[c2-l035 note] Explains placing or hanging a severed head. The historical pola is retained without supplying a modern accent or hyphen.
[c2-l036 >] *durala nalgum pao, &c.*
[c2-l036 note] Completes dependurala and specifies suspension from a pole or stick.
[c2-l037] Qiricayexi, ſu, eita. *Tornar a cortar a quẽ*
[c2-l037 note] Kirikaeshi is striking back at someone who struck first. Tornar replaces the OCR's Ternar.
[c2-l038 >] *me cortou. ¶* Qirigayexiuo ſuru. *Idẽ.*
[c2-l038 note] Completes the retaliation gloss and supplies the nominal construction kirigaeshi wo suru. The printed c/g alternation between headword and example is preserved.
[c2-l039] Qirichiraxi, ſu, aita. *Cortando fazer eſpa-*
[c2-l039 note] Kirichirashi includes scattering or putting others to flight by cutting attacks. The Portuguese expression is divided after eſpa-.
[c2-l040 >] *lha fato, ou fazer fugir. ¶ Item, Cortan-*
[c2-l040 note] Completes the scattering expression and introduces the second sense of cutting material into scattered pieces.
[c2-l041 >] *do eſpalhar, & desfazer empedaços como*
[c2-l041 note] Describes dispersing and breaking something into pieces by cutting. The attached empedaços is preserved.
[c2-l042 >] *papel, &c.*
[c2-l042 note] Paper supplies an example of material cut into pieces; the short row completes the preceding clause.
[c2-l043] Qiricoguchi. *Põta cortada de qualquer couſa.*
[c2-l043 note] Kirikoguchi is the cut end or edge of an object. The scan supports abbreviated Põta and cortada, replacing the garbled OCR Tta certada.
[c2-l044] Qiricomi, u, côda. *Cortãdo ẽtrar pellos inimigos*
[c2-l044 note] Kirikomi describes entering among enemies while striking. The two Portuguese nasal abbreviations and the absence of a final punctuation mark are retained.
[c2-l045] Qiricoroxi, ſu, oita. *Matar à eſpada.*
[c2-l045 note] Kirikoroshi is killing with a sword. The descender in eſpada identifies p, correcting the OCR's t.
[c2-l046] Qiricuchi. *Ponta cortada: ou abertura q̃ fica*
[c2-l046 note] Kirikuchi denotes a cut edge or the opening left by cutting. The initial and medial letters restore Qiricuchi from OCR Ginicuchi.
[c2-l047 >] *em couſa, q̃ ſe começou acortar.*
[c2-l047 note] Completes the definition as an opening in something that has begun to be cut. The source's attached acortar is preserved. The lower-right Qiri is a catchword; its narrow final i is undotted or worn, so that furniture reading is recorded as provisional.
