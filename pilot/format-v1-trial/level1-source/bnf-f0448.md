## catchword [catchword] Printed catchword

[cw-l001 >>] Sǒsǒ
[cw-l001 note] The catchword is abraded, especially its medial s and vowel marks. Sǒsǒ is the usable chosen reading supported by the surviving shapes and the first heading on f449; no terminal period is supplied where none is secure.
