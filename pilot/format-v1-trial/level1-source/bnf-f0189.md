[c1-l001] Ficari, u, atta. *Reſplandecer, ou dar cla-*
[c1-l001 note] Hikari, -u, -atta is shining; the single u is the inflectional ending, not OCR us.
[c1-l002 >] *ridade.*
[c1-l002 note] Ridade completes claridade, the noun for brightness, and is body continuation.
[c1-l003] Ficaricacayaqi, u, aita. *Reſplandecer mui-*
[c1-l003 note] Hikarikakayaki intensifies shining; the linking hikari is supported by the repeated example below.
[c1-l004 >] *to. Vt,* Teqino meniua ficari cacayaqu
[c1-l004 note] Teki no me ni wa hikari kakayaku describes dazzling the enemies' eyes; its relative clause continues into the armor below.
[c1-l005 >] azayacanaru yoroiuo qi, &c. *1. Parte*
[c1-l005 note] Azayakanaru yoroi wo ki means wearing splendid armor; the initial a is visible and must not be lost from the adjective.
[c1-l006 >] Goſag. *Veſtindo hũas armas mui luzidas q̃*
[c1-l006 note] Goſag is the printed source label after first part; the gloss describes brilliant armor, with mui luzidas and abbreviated que.
[c1-l007 >] *reluzião nos olhos dos inimigos.*
[c1-l007 note] The armor gleams in the enemies' eyes, matching the Japanese relative clause.
[c1-l008] Ficarimono. *Exhalação aceſa que aparece de*
[c1-l008 note] Hikarimono is a luminous phenomenon, explained in the period's language as a burning exhalation.
[c1-l009 >] *noite, ou outra claridade como de ralampa-*
[c1-l009 note] Nighttime light or lightning is included; retain ralampa- rather than modernizing its vowel.
[c1-l010 >] *go, &c.*
[c1-l010 note] Go completes ralampago, finishing the luminous-phenomena definition.
[c1-l011] Ficariuatari, u, atta. *Reſplandecer, ou dei-*
[c1-l011 note] Hikariwatari means spread light widely; the gloss begins shining or giving off light.
[c1-l012 >] *tar luz por todas as partes.*
[c1-l012 note] Por todas as partes completes the all-around extent of the shining.
[c1-l013] Ficata. *Lugar que fica enxuto onde a marè eſ-*
[c1-l013 note] Hikata is exposed dry tidal ground; onde a marè supplies the local condition, not OCR onde ra.
[c1-l014 >] *praya muito.*
[c1-l014 note] Espraya muito completes the strongly receding tide description.
[c1-l015] Ficaye, uru, eta. *Deter, ou ter mão. ¶* V-
[c1-l015 note] Hikaye is restraining or holding back; V- begins uma and therefore must remain Japanese roman text.
[c1-l016 >] mauo ficayuru. *Fazer parar o caualo.*
[c1-l016 note] Uma wo hikayuru means stop or rein in a horse; the cross-line split completes that object.
[c1-l017 >] *¶* Cocorouo ficayuru. *Irſe à mão, &*
[c1-l017 note] Kokoro wo hikayuru extends restraint to the heart or intention, glossed as holding oneself back.
[c1-l018 >] *não fazer ſua vontade. ¶* Xocujiuo fica-
[c1-l018 note] Not doing one's own will closes that sense; shokuji wo begins restraint in eating.
[c1-l019 >] yuru. *Refrearſe no comer.*
[c1-l019 note] Hikayuru completes moderating food intake; Refrearse confirms restraint rather than physical cooling.
[c1-l020] Ficazu. *Numero de dias. ¶* Ficazuuo
[c1-l020 note] Hikazu is the number of days; the example repeats the noun with wo below.
[c1-l021 >] mitçuru. *Encherſe, ou comprirſe o numero*
[c1-l021 note] Mitsuru means the appointed number of days is fulfilled; the visible m corrects the false initial in OCR.
[c1-l022 >] *dos dias. ¶.* Ficazuga caſanaru. *Acre-*
[c1-l022 note] Hikazu ga kasanaru means days accumulate; Acre- begins the Portuguese increase verb.
[c1-l023 >] *centaremſe os dias. ¶* Ficazuga tçumo-
[c1-l023 note] Hikazu ga tsumo- introduces another accumulation expression after acrecentaremse os dias.
[c1-l024 >] ru. *Idem.*
[c1-l024 note] Ru completes tsumoru, with Idem explicitly giving the same sense as kasanaru.
[c1-l025] Ficca. i, Fudeuo motaſuru dǒgu. *Hũ in-*
[c1-l025 note] Hikka is a brush rest, explained as an implement supporting the fude; it is not the act of writing yet.
[c1-l026 >] *ſtrumento de metal à maneira de ſerpente, ou*
[c1-l026 note] The metal rest may be serpent-shaped; maneira is the Portuguese shape formula.
[c1-l027 >] *couſa ſemelhante ſobre que poem as penas de*
[c1-l027 note] Japanese writing brushes are placed on it; penas is the dictionary's Portuguese designation for those implements.
[c1-l028 >] *Iapão.*
[c1-l028 note] Iapão completes penas de Iapão and stays italic as Portuguese, not a Japanese word.
[c1-l029] Ficca. Fudeno xita. *Aquelle comenos, ou*
[c1-l029 note] A homophonous hikka, fude no shita, refers to the moment of writing or taking up the brush.
[c1-l030 >] *tempo em que ſe eſcreue, ou toma a pena.*
[c1-l030 note] The prose defines the writing occasion, distinguishing this temporal sense from the physical rest.
[c1-l031 >] *Vt,* Ficcani cacuuo môqetari. *Toman-*
[c1-l031 note] Hikka ni kaku wo mōketari describes obtaining a phrase on taking up the brush; preserve the printed kaku wording pending lexical interpretation.
[c1-l032 >] *do a pena pera eſcreuer, ocorreo me hũa boa*
[c1-l032 note] The Portuguese says a good sentence occurred to the writer upon taking the pen.
[c1-l033 >] *ſentença. S.*
[c1-l033 note] Sentença closes the thought or phrase; S is the written-usage label.
[c1-l034] Ficcacari, u, atta. *Embaraçarſe, ou prender*
[c1-l034 note] Hikkakari is getting caught or entangled, an intransitive sense followed by physical examples.

[c1-l036 >] *quando puxão por algum pao, &c. ¶* Qi-

[c1-l037 >] rumonoga cuguini ficcacatta. *Prendeoſe*

[c1-l038 >] *me, ou ficou o vestido pegado em hum prègo.*
[c1-l038 note] The garment remained attached to a nail; preserve the printed hum and prègo forms.
[c1-l039] Ficcagami. *Curua da perna de baixo dos jo-*
[c1-l039 note] Hikkagami is the hollow or bend behind the knee; the anatomical gloss does not refer to a mirror.
[c1-l040 >] *elhos.*
[c1-l040 note] Elhos completes joelhos, knees, and belongs to the preceding anatomical entry.
[c1-l041] Ficcanaguri, u, utta. *Aferrar com a mão*
[c1-l041 note] Hikkanaguri is grasping and yanking; initial hi is visible rather than OCR ha.
[c1-l042 >] *de eruas, cabellos, &c. & arrancalos.*

[c1-l043] Ficcaqe, uru, eta. *Puxar, ou enteſar como*

[c1-l044 >] *arco pera por a ſeta. Vt,* Machiuo cobu-
[c1-l044 note] The bow example starts machi wo kobu-; the following fist and arrow-tip gloss provide the archery context.
[c1-l045 >] xi ficcaqe. *Embebendo o arco de maneira*
