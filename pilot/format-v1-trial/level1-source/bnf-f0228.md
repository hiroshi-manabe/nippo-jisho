[c1-l001] Fuvn. Vqicumo. *Nuuem que eſtà no ar.*
[c1-l001 note] Fuvn is fuun, floating cloud, glossed by Vqicumo and a cloud suspended in the air. This is distinct from the preceding bad-fortune homophone.
[c1-l002] Fuunna. *Couſa desditoſa, ou deſafortunada.*
[c1-l002 note] Fuunna is the adjectival unlucky form, glossed desditoſa and deſafortunada. The doubled n belongs to the adjective boundary, not a nasal abbreviation.
[c1-l003] Fuunni. *Aduer.*
[c1-l003 note] Fuunni supplies the adverb of the preceding unlucky adjective. Aduer. is Portuguese grammatical metalanguage and remains italic.
[c1-l004] Fuxe. *Eſmola que ſe da aos Bõzos por algum*
[c1-l004 note] Fuxe is fuse, alms to Buddhist monks for a service. Bõzos is embedded in Portuguese; its italic type and nasal abbreviation are preserved.
[c1-l005 >] *officio, ceremonia, &c. ¶* Fuxeuo ſu-
[c1-l005 note] The payment is for an office or ceremony; Fuxeuo ſu- starts fuse wo suru. The line break divides the verb, not a new entry.
[c1-l006 >] ru. *Dar ſemelhante eſmola.*
[c1-l006 note] ru completes suru. Dar ſemelhante eſmola means giving that kind of alms, confirming the preceding noun-plus-verb construction.
[c1-l007] Fuxe. *Remendo. ¶* Fuxeuo ſuru. *Re-*
[c1-l007 note] A separate Fuxe means a patch; Fuxeuo ſuru means patching. Re- continues the Portuguese verb remendar below.
[c1-l008 >] *mendar. No Cami ſe diz.* Tçuguiuo ſuru.
[c1-l008 note] mendar completes the gloss. Cami marks the upper-region variant tçugui wo suru, to patch; the region name visibly shares the italic explanation.
[c1-l009] Fuxe. *Lugar na Catana onde ſe mete a Cõga-*
[c1-l009 note] Fuxe here is a recess in a sword for the small knife or another implement. The scan reads Lugar and onde, not Lagar/ende; Catana and Cõga- are italic.
[c1-l010 >] *tana, ou outro inſtrumento de ferro. Vt,*
[c1-l010 note] tana completes the embedded Cõgatana. The iron implement and the following examples confirm a storage recess, not a winepress despite the OCR's Lagar.
[c1-l011 >] Cogatana buxe, l, cǒgai buxe.
[c1-l011 note] Cogatana buxe and cǒgai buxe name the knife and kogai recesses. The latter has a visible space before buxe; lexical segmentation is kept as printed.
[c1-l012] Fuxe, uru, eta. *Fazer deitar. Vt,* V-
[c1-l012 note] Fuxe, uru, eta is the transitive to lay down. V- begins Vma, horse; the terminal hyphen belongs to that Roman Japanese fragment.
[c1-l013 >] mauo fuſuru. *Fazer cair, ou deitar o*
[c1-l013 note] Vma wo fusuru means making a horse fall or lie down. Fusuru is the historical inflected form, not modern fuseru substituted into the text.
[c1-l014 >] *caualo. ¶ Item, Permet. Condenar. Vt,*
[c1-l014 note] The horse gloss ends; Permet. introduces the transferred sense to condemn. It is a semantic extension rather than another Japanese headword.
[c1-l015 >] Fitouo tçumini fuſuru.
[c1-l015 note] Fito wo tçumi ni fusuru is to subject a person to punishment. The legal gloss accounts for the superficially physical verb.
[c1-l016] Fuxe, uru, eta. *Remendar. X. Vt,* Qi-
[c1-l016 note] The homographic Fuxe means to mend, with X. as a usage label. Qi- begins qirumono; the label is wholly italic in the scan.
[c1-l017 >] rumonouo fuſuru.
[c1-l017 note] rumonouo completes qirumono wo, clothing as object of fusuru. The Japanese and Portuguese both support mending rather than laying down here.
[c1-l018] Fuxego. *Hũa maneira de cubertoura com hũs*
[c1-l018 note] Fuxego is fusego, a perforated cover associated with a fumigator. Cubertoura is retained as the printed Portuguese form, not modernized.
[c1-l019 >] *buraquinhos que poẽ ſobre algum perfumador*
[c1-l019 note] The small holes and perfumador explain the construction of the cover. Poẽ is the visible contracted verb; no silent expansion to poem.
[c1-l020 >] *polla qual ſae o cheiro.*
[c1-l020 note] The scent exits through the cover. Polla and ſae are historical forms; the sentence completes the preceding material description.
[c1-l021] Fuxegui, u, eida. *Reſiſtir, ou defenderſe.*
[c1-l021 note] Fuxegui gives fusegu, resist or defend oneself. The separated-looking de fenderſe is read as defenderſe from visible letters; OCR's long-s reading changed its sense.
[c1-l022 >] *¶* Teqiuo fuxegu. *Defenderſe dos ini-*
[c1-l022 note] Teqi wo fusegu means defending against an enemy. The Portuguese dos ini- continues inimigos below and confirms teqi, not a different q/g reading.
[c1-l023 >] *migos. ¶* Tentaçamuo fuxegu. *Reſis-*
[c1-l023 note] Tentaçam wo fusegu applies the verb to temptation, a Portuguese loan embedded in Japanese. Reſis- begins resistir, not the OCR's Reſu-.
[c1-l024 >] *tir à tentação.*
[c1-l024 note] tir completes resistir à tentação. This explicit translation supports the loanword example; its historical spelling is not replaced by modern Portuguese.
[c1-l025] Fuxeguiai, ǒ, ǒta. *Reſiſtir de ambas as par-*
[c1-l025 note] Fuxeguiai means resisting each other, with reciprocal ai and historical contracted principal parts. The Portuguese has a word boundary in Reſiſtir de.
[c1-l026 >] *tes, ou pelejarem muitos entreſi.*
[c1-l026 note] Both sides resist, or many fight among themselves. Entreſi is left as printed, without imposing modern spacing.
[c1-l027] Fuxeguitatacai, ǒ, ǒta. *Reſiſtir pelejãdo.*
[c1-l027 note] Fuxeguitatacai combines resistance and fighting. Pelejãdo has its nasal mark over a; the OCR's pelejõdo is inconsistent with both scan and verb.
[c1-l028] Fǔxei. Cajeno coye. *Soido, ou eſtrepito do*
[c1-l028 note] Fǔxei is wind-sound, paraphrased caje no coye. The long-u mark and coye agree with the explicit wind-noise gloss.
[c1-l029 >] *vento. Vt,* Fǔxei ſuivon. *Soido do*
[c1-l029 note] Fǔxei ſuivon pairs wind-sound with water-sound. Enlarged letters read xei rather than xoi; the v in suivon is retained, not normalized to another spelling.
[c1-l030 >] *vento, & ruido dagoa.*
[c1-l030 note] The noise of wind and water finishes that example. Dagoa is the source's joined form, not silently expanded to de agua.
[c1-l031] Fuxei. Vqiyo. *Mundo miſerauel.*
[c1-l031 note] Fuxei here is the miserable floating world, vqiyo. Miſerauel is one Portuguese word; the internal OCR space is spurious.
[c1-l032] Fuxen. Nobezu. *i, Não eſcreuer mais, ou*
[c1-l032 note] Fuxen means not expounding further. The scan reads Nobezu. i, followed by Portuguese, not a Japanese Nobezuiai; this matters to the automatic kana.
[c1-l033 >] *não ſe dilatar mais na carta. S.*
[c1-l033 note] The letter-writing explanation says not to extend the letter further. S. remains italic and is not part of the Japanese expression.
[c1-l034] Fuxeri, u, etta. *Iazer, ou deitarſe. ¶ Itẽ,*
[c1-l034 note] Fuxeri is lying down, with principal part etta. The final group is worn: enlarged strokes plus the paradigm and NINJAL support etta, not xeua; the uncertain printing is noted rather than concealed.
[c1-l035 >] *Defumarſe. X.*
[c1-l035 note] Defumarſe adds the fumigation sense, consistent with fusego above. The X. label is italic as a whole, not mixed type within one abbreviation.
[c1-l036] Fǔxet, l, fǔjet. *Nouas incertas que corrẽ.*
[c1-l036 note] Fǔxet/fǔjet are variants for circulating uncertain news, fuusetsu/fuu zetsu in modern terms. Final t is historical syllable-final spelling, not an omitted vowel.
[c1-l037] Fuxet. Tocazu. *Não declarar. Vt,* Ichi-
[c1-l037 note] Fuxet, tocazu, is not declaring or explaining, distinct from wind-rumour above. Ichi- begins a Buddhist example about a single character.
[c1-l038 >] ji fuxet. *Não declarar nem hũa ſò letra.*
[c1-l038 note] ji completes ichiji; not even one character is declared. The gloss explicitly supplies letra and confirms ji, while retaining final t in fuxet.
[c1-l039 >] *Bup.*
[c1-l039 note] Bup. is a Buddhist usage label, not Japanese body text. Its printed letters are italic; the earlier mixed B/up styling was an OCR artefact.
[c1-l040] Fuxi. Chichi, co. *Pay, & filho. ¶* Go-
[c1-l040 note] Fuxi is father and child, chichi/co, i.e. fushi in a Sino-Japanese compound. Go- starts the respectful example rather than another definition.
[c1-l041 >] fuxino aidani. *Entre o pay, & o filho*
[c1-l041 note] Go-fuxi no aida ni means between father and child. The translation and following register explanation support the honorific go prefix.
[c1-l042 >] *falando com reſpeito.*
[c1-l042 note] Falando com reſpeito explicitly says the expression is respectful. It qualifies the preceding example rather than altering the kinship definition.
[c1-l043] Fuxi. Xixezu. *Não morrer. i, Immortal.*
[c1-l043 note] Fuxi, xixezu, is not dying, immortal. Xe is the dictionary's normal se spelling; it is not evidence for an anomalous modern pronunciation.
[c1-l044 >] *Vt,* Fuxifumet. *Idem. ¶* Fuxi fu-
[c1-l044 note] Fuxifumet is not dying or perishing. The second occurrence is physically split fu-/met; both represent the same component without normalizing the layout.
[c1-l045 >] metno tai. *Suſtancia immortal.*
[c1-l045 note] metno tai completes the immortal substance/body expression. Suſtancia is kept without adding the b expected in modern substância.
[c1-l046] Fuxi. *Toada de canto. ¶* Fuxiuo tçu-
[c1-l046 note] Fuxi now means a melodic phrase or tune. Fuxiuo tçu- begins the example about assigning a tune; identical spelling does not imply the previous immortal sense.
[c1-l047 >] qiru. *Dar toada, ou por em canto algũa*
[c1-l047 note] qiru completes tçuqiru. The enlarged scan reads Dar and algũa, with a small final a rather than a hyphen; the original OCR lost both.
[c1-l048 >] *couſa.*
[c1-l048 note] Couſa finishes setting some thing to song. It is a separate physical row of the same Portuguese sentence, not a headword or catchword.
