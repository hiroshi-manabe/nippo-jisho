[c1-l001] Qinxit. i, coto. *Hum instrumento muſico*
[c1-l001 note] Qinxit is equated with coto and described as a musical instrument. This is the full entry anticipated by the Qinxit catchword on f393; the printed lowercase coto is retained.
[c1-l002 >] *como crauo.*
[c1-l002 note] The instrument is compared to a harpsichord. This is the dictionary's comparison, not a claim that the Japanese instrument has the same construction.
[c1-l003] Qinxǒ. Nixiqino vye. *Em cima de ricas*
[c1-l003 note] Nixiqino vye means on top of brocade. The Portuguese begins 'on top of rich fabrics', leading to the example of adding flowers to an already splendid surface.
[c1-l004 >] *peças. Vt,* Qinxǒni fanauo ſô. Xix.
[c1-l004 note] Qinxǒni fanauo ſô describes adding flowers to brocade. The upright Xix. is the citation label; ſô has an upward circumflex, unlike the headword's caron.
[c1-l005 >] *Couſas riquißimas, ou mui galantes hũas ſobre*
[c1-l005 note] The translation explains an accumulation of very rich or elegant things, one on another. The tilde in hũas remains over u.
[c1-l006 >] *outras. i, Sobejas, & demaſiadas.*
[c1-l006 note] Completes the piling-up image and interprets it as superfluous or excessive adornment. The explanatory i is followed by a comma in the scan.
[c1-l007] Qinxǒ. *Carta de bom estilo, ou verſos eſcri-*
[c1-l007 note] This Qinxǒ concerns a well-written letter or verses. The word is estilo, not OCR estllo; the Portuguese escrito is divided across rows.
[c1-l008 >] *tos que hum louua, ou aleuanta com hõra. Vt,*
[c1-l008 note] The writing is praised or spoken of with honour. Vt introduces an example of such a letter or poem arriving.
[c1-l009 >] Qinxǒ tǒrai. *Vir, ou mandarſe algũa car*
[c1-l009 note] Qinxǒ tǒrai expresses the arrival of an elegant letter or composition. The row ends with car without a visible hyphen; carta continues below.
[c1-l010 >] *ta, ou verſos elegantes.* S.
[c1-l010 note] Ta completes carta, followed by the alternative of elegant verses. The terminal S. is an upright written-usage label.
[c1-l011] Qinxocu. Coganeno iro. *Cor de ouro.*
[c1-l011 note] Coganeno iro gives the literal sense, the colour of gold. A further figurative sense concerning someone's expression follows within the same entry.
[c1-l012 >] *¶ Item, Roſto alegre. Vt,* Vomoteni
[c1-l012 note] The added sense is a cheerful face. Vomoteni begins an example contrasting a person's outward manner with what is inwardly in the heart.
[c1-l013 >] qinxocuno majiuariuo muſubi, cocoroni
[c1-l013 note] The example describes forming a pleasant outward relationship, then turns to the heart with cocoroni. The paired outward/inward phrases must be read together across these lines.
[c1-l014 >] jefino fariuo tçucǒ. *No exterior he mui-*
[c1-l014 note] The Japanese completes the contrast with an inward 'needle' of judging right and wrong. The Portuguese begins by describing a very cheerful exterior; tçucǒ is retained in the printed historical form.
[c1-l015 >] *to alegre, & apraziuel, mas no coração não he*
[c1-l015 note] Completes muito and contrasts a pleasant exterior with a heart that is not the same. The sentence continues rather than ending after the apparent description of cheerfulness.
[c1-l016 >] *aßi, por q̃ conſidera as couſas doutra maneira,*
[c1-l016 note] The reason for the contrast is that the person considers matters differently inwardly. The abbreviated q̃ and historical doutra remain unexpanded.
[c1-l017 >] *& da q̃ ẽtẽder a quẽ fala, ou argumẽta cõ elle.*
[c1-l017 note] The explanation ends by saying that he gives those speaking or arguing with him something to think about. The scan separates q̃ from ẽtẽder; the nasal abbreviations are retained, not expanded or fused.
[c1-l018] Qinxocu. *Candieiro de ouro.*
[c1-l018 note] This homograph is a golden lamp or lampstand. The printed Candieiro is preserved rather than modernized to candeeiro.
[c1-l019] Qinxǔ. Nixiqito nuimono. *Certa laya de pe-*
[c1-l019 note] Nixiqito nuimono combines brocade and needlework. The Portuguese starts a description of very rich textiles, dividing peças after pe-.
[c1-l020 >] *ças muito ricas como brocadilho, & baſtidor,*
[c1-l020 note] The rich fabrics are compared with brocade and embroidery work. The printed baſtidor remains part of the description rather than being replaced by a modern textile classification.
[c1-l021 >] *ou broslado.*
[c1-l021 note] Broslado adds embroidered work as an alternative description. Its historical consonant sequence is retained exactly.
[c1-l022] Qinxu. *Prohibição, ou ley que hum ſe poem a*
[c1-l022 note] Qinxu denotes a prohibition or rule one imposes on oneself. The continuation specifies abstaining from wine, distinguishing this unmarked -u headword from Qinxǔ above.
[c1-l023 >] *ſi de não beber vinho. Vt,* Qinxuuo ſuru.
[c1-l023 note] The rule is not to drink wine; Qinxuuo ſuru gives the corresponding Japanese example. The scan repeats Qinxu with the object particle uo, not the OCR's Qinzuuo.
[c1-l024 >] *Pòrſe esta ley, &c.*
[c1-l024 note] The example is translated as imposing this rule on oneself. The grave accent in Pòrſe is preserved.
[c1-l025] Qinyacu. i, Tçuyoqi yacuſocu. *Forte, ou*
[c1-l025 note] Tçuyoqi yacuſocu explains a strong or firm promise. The Portuguese adjective phrase continues on the next physical line.
[c1-l026 >] *firme promeſſa.*
[c1-l026 note] This completes the definition as a firm promise. The two long-s forms in promeſſa are retained.
[c1-l027] Qinyet. Yorocobi, u. *Alegria.* S.
[c1-l027 note] Qinyet is joy, explained by the Japanese verb for rejoicing. The abbreviated u is kept as printed, and S. is the upright written-usage label.
[c1-l028] Qinzan. Nixiqino yama. *Monte, ou mato*
[c1-l028 note] Nixiqino yama presents a mountain as brocade. The Portuguese explains this through a mountain or woodland's autumn appearance rather than a literal fabric covering.
[c1-l029 >] *que no Outono parece fermoſo, & de varias co*
[c1-l029 note] The mountain looks beautiful in autumn, with varied colours. The row ends at co without a printed hyphen, even though cores continues below.
[c1-l030 >] *res em que ſe mudão as folhas.* S.
[c1-l030 note] Res completes cores, referring to the colours into which the leaves change. The entry closes with the upright written-usage label S.
[c1-l031] Qio. Suu. *Eſtar. Vt,* Qiouo ſaru. *Apar-*
[c1-l031 note] Qio concerns being or residing in a place; Qiouo ſaru means leaving that place. Only the opening Suu of the Japanese gloss is legible before an opaque ink blot, followed by the original period after the concealed ending. Suu is therefore a visible fragment, not a claim that the full word ends there; no hidden letters are supplied, and the OCR's Suqeta is not accepted.
[c1-l032 >] *tarſe do lugar, ou apoſento. ¶* Qiouo xi-
[c1-l032 note] Completes Apartarſe: departing from the place or lodging. The blot touches lugar, but the surviving ending and phrase support that word; the next Japanese example begins Qiouo xi-.
[c1-l033 >] muru. *Determinar lugar como choupana, &c.*
[c1-l033 note] Muru completes the Japanese xi-muru example for establishing a dwelling. The Portuguese speaks of determining a place such as a hut.
[c1-l034 >] *onde hum more.*
[c1-l034 note] The place is one in which someone may live. This completes the residence sense of Qio before the distinct falsehood entry.
[c1-l035] Qio. *Falſidade, ou mentira. Vt,* Qiouo
[c1-l035 note] This Qio means falsity or a lie. Qiouo begins an example whose verb, saying or speaking, is on the following row.
[c1-l036 >] yǔ. *Dizer mentiras. ¶ Item, Eſtar co-*
[c1-l036 note] Qiouo yǔ means telling lies. A further sense then begins, describing a condition lacking vigour or substance, with como divided after co-.
[c1-l037 >] *mo ſem vigor, ou ſustancia. Vt,* Qioxita
[c1-l037 note] Completes como and introduces Qioxita as the modifier in an example of a debilitated person. The condition described is weakness or emptiness of vitality.
[c1-l038 >] fito. *Homem que està debilitado, & ſem*
[c1-l038 note] Fito completes Qioxita fito. The Portuguese describes a weakened person and continues the lack-of-vigour explanation below.
[c1-l039 >] *vigor, ou ſuſtancia interior.* || {mark} *(* || {word}*do Rey.*
[c1-l039 note] The main text finishes the account of lacking inner vigour. At the far right, the separate printed '(do Rey.' completes the next row's capital/court definition; its physical placement is preserved rather than read as part of the weakened-person gloss or moved to another row.
[c1-l040] Qiǒ. Miyaco. *Cidade principal, ou corte*
[c1-l040 note] Qiǒ is equated with Miyaco, the principal city or royal court. The displaced 'do Rey.' is printed at the far right of the row above and supplies the end of this definition.
[c1-l041] Qiǒ. *Liuro. Vt,* Qiǒuo tozzuru. *En-*
[c1-l041 note] This Qiǒ means a book; Qiǒuo tozzuru is glossed as binding books. The long-o marks are carons, and En- starts the Portuguese binding verb.
[c1-l042 >] *quadernar liuros.*
[c1-l042 note] Completes Enquadernar liuros, 'bind books'. The row remains a separate continuation rather than being joined to the example above.
[c1-l043] Qiǒbaco. *Caixão de liuros.*
[c1-l043 note] Qiǒbaco is a box or chest for books. Its opening Q belongs to the headword despite the generous type spacing.
[c1-l044] Qiobô. Itçuuaritaru fô. *Ley falſa.*
[c1-l044 note] Itçuuaritaru fô explains a false law or doctrine. Both printed long-o marks are circumflexes; no accent is added to the first o of Qiobô.
[c1-l045] Qioca. Sannuru natçu. i, Suguixi natçu.
[c1-l045 note] The two Japanese paraphrases both refer to the past summer. This unaccented Qioca is distinct from the humorous-verse headword Qiǒca below.
[c1-l046 >] *Verão, ou estio paßado.*
[c1-l046 note] The Portuguese confirms 'last summer', using verão and estio as alternatives. The historical paßado is preserved.
[c1-l047] Qiǒca. Curui vta. *Verſos gracioſos, ou que*
[c1-l047 note] Qiǒca denotes playful or humorous verse, explained by Curui vta. It begins a new entry at the column's left alignment; the definition continues at the top of column 2.

[c2-l010 >] *preſenta boa, ou maa. ¶* Yoi qiǒchigia.
[c2-l010 note] The place appears good or bad; the scan prints maa with two a types. Yoi qiǒchigia gives the favourable case, 'it is a good location'.
[c2-l011 >] *He bõ lugar, ou chão pera fazer caſas, &c.*
[c2-l011 note] The favourable location is interpreted as good ground for building houses. The abbreviation bõ remains unexpanded.
[c2-l012] Qiǒchǔ. Cagamino naca. *Dentro do eſ-*

[c2-l013 >] *pelho.*

[c2-l014] Qiôchǔ. *Interior do homem, ou dentro do*
[c2-l014 note] This entry describes a person's inner being or the inside of the breast. The o mark is an upward circumflex, agreeing with the repeated form below and differing from the mirror entry's caron.
[c2-l015 >] *peito. Vt,* Fitono qiôchǔuo miſucaſu.
[c2-l015 note] Fitono qiôchǔuo miſucaſu means seeing through or discerning a person's inner thoughts. Peito completes the preceding definition before Vt introduces the example.
[c2-l016 >] *Ver, & penetrar o interior dalguem: às ve-*
[c2-l016 note] The translation speaks of seeing and penetrating someone's interior. Às vezes begins a usage qualification and is divided at ve-.
[c2-l017 >] *zes ſe diz iſto abatendo, ou moſtrando ter*
[c2-l017 note] Completes vezes and explains that the expression can be used disparagingly. The qualification continues to describe a low opinion of another person.
[c2-l018 >] *pouco conceito dalguem.*
[c2-l018 note] The speaker may be showing little regard for someone. This completes the social nuance attached to seeing through a person's interior.
[c2-l019] Qiǒcot. *Despropoſitos, ou couſas desconue-*
[c2-l019 note] Qiǒcot denotes improprieties or unsuitable conduct. The Portuguese adjective desconuenientes is divided after desconue-.
[c2-l020 >] *nientes, & que carecem de grauidade, & ma-*
[c2-l020 note] The conduct lacks seriousness and maturity. Ma- begins madureza, so the word on the next row is not an independent reference to hardness.
[c2-l021 >] *dureza.*
[c2-l021 note] Dureza completes madureza, maturity. This closes the noun entry before its adjectival form.
[c2-l022] Qiǒcotna. *Couſas fora de propoſito, leues, &c.*
[c2-l022 note] Qiǒcotna is the adjectival form, describing frivolous or inappropriate things. The printed final na is part of the Japanese form, not Portuguese material.
[c2-l023 >] *¶* Qiǒcotna fito. *Homem leue, & pou-*
[c2-l023 note] Qiǒcotna fito describes a frivolous person. The scan has both the paragraph mark and Qiǒ-, correcting the OCR's missing mark and Quǒ-.

[c2-l024 note] Co completes pouco, followed by the separately printed maduro. The person is described as lacking maturity; the OCR's comaduro fused the continuation with the next word.
[c2-l025] Qiǒcotni. *Adu.*
[c2-l025 note] Qiǒcotni is the adverb corresponding to the preceding rash or immature behavior. The scan has t followed by n, not the OCR's m; Adu. labels the adverbial form without repeating the definition.
[c2-l026] Qiocu. *Paßos de garganta, ou ſom ſingular*
[c2-l026 note] Qiocu denotes vocal runs or a distinctive musical sound. The definition continues with the instrument on the next row; the inherited spelling Paßos is retained.
[c2-l027 >] *de inſtrumento muſico. ¶* Qiocuuo tçu-
[c2-l027 note] The musical-instrument qualification completes the first sense. A pilcrow introduces Qiocuuo tçu-cuſu, an expression for bringing an art to a skillful execution; the printed division follows tçu-.
[c2-l028 >] cuſu. *Fazer deſtramente, & com graça al-*
[c2-l028 note] This completes tçucuſu and begins the explanation, performing something skillfully and gracefully. The Portuguese word for some is physically divided al-gũa across the next boundary.
[c2-l029 >] *gũa arte, como de bailar, tanger, de voltear,*
[c2-l029 note] The arts offered as examples are dancing, playing music, and tumbling or turning. The initial gũa completes algũa, with the printed nasal abbreviation left unexpanded.
[c2-l030 >] *& outras couſas ſemelhantes. ¶* Qiocu-
[c2-l030 note] Other similar accomplishments finish the skillful-performance sense. The next example starts Qiocu-uo yǔ and retains the compositor's word division rather than being joined onto this row.
[c2-l031 >] uo yǔ. *Dizer graças, ou couſas que cau-*
[c2-l031 note] Qiocuuo yǔ means saying witty things or things that cause laughter. The printed yǔ has its length mark, and the Portuguese verb is divided cau-ſão.
[c2-l032 >] *ſão riſo.*
[c2-l032 note] This short continuation completes cauſão riſo, cause laughter, and closes the humorous-speech example rather than beginning a separate entry.
[c2-l033] Qiǒcu. Curǔ inu. *Cão que brinca. ¶* Qiǒ-
[c2-l033 note] Qiǒcu is explained by Curǔ inu, a dog that plays or frolics. The example repeats the headword across a line division after Qiǒ-, distinct from the preceding musical Qiocu.

[c2-l039] Qiocuchǔ. *Dentro do cubiculo.* S.
[c2-l039 note] Qiocuchǔ means inside a chamber or cubicle. The final S. is a visibly upright usage label rather than part of the italic Portuguese sentence.

[c2-l043 >] ru. *Fazer couſas que não diueram fazerſe.*

[c2-l044] Qiocurocu. *Cadeira.*
[c2-l044 note] Qiocurocu denotes a chair. The one-word Portuguese gloss is complete on this physical row, with no missing continuation.
[c2-l045] Qiocuxei. Magaru coye. *Voz, ou paßo*
[c2-l045 note] Qiocuxei is paraphrased as a bending or modulating voice, Magaru coye. The Portuguese gives a voice or vocal run, completed by the throat qualification below.
[c2-l046 >] *de garganta.*
[c2-l046 note] This short line completes the phrase paßo de garganta, a vocal run or modulation, and belongs to Qiocuxei rather than the following entry.
[c2-l047] Qiocuxet. *Couſas apraziueis que ſão acõmo-*
[c2-l047 note] Qiocuxet introduces pleasing things appropriate to each occasion, continued on the next page. The initial is Q, not D; the damaged but visible nasal mark belongs to acõmo-, which continues as dadas, also printed below as the catchword.
