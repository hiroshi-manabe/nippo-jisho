[c1-l001] Itame. *Veas largas da taboa quando he ſerrada*
[c1-l001 note] Itame names the broad visible grain of a board. The Portuguese begins a description of how the board is sawn; the uppercase I is visible, and this headword is anticipated by the preceding page’s catchword.
[c1-l002 >] *direitamente, & de maneira que ficam as ve-*
[c1-l002 note] The board is sawn so that its grain remains intact. The printed ve- begins veas across the physical line break.
[c1-l003 >] *as inteiras. ¶* Maſame. *Veas da taboa cor-*
[c1-l003 note] As completes veas. The contrasting term masame introduces another treatment of the board’s grain; the source’s own description is retained rather than rewritten to match modern woodworking definitions.
[c1-l004 >] *tadas, ou ſerradas por meo de maneira que não*
[c1-l004 note] Tadas completes cortadas: the grain is cut or sawn through the middle. The following negative clause specifies the resulting appearance.
[c1-l005 >] *pareção as veas inteiras, & largas.*
