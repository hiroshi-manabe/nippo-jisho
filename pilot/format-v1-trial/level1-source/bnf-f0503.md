[c1-l001] Tericacayaqi, u, aita. *Reſplandecer.*
[c1-l001 note] Terikakayaki, with the supplied principal forms, means shining or gleaming. The Portuguese gives the single verb resplandecer; the following military example illustrates the visible brilliance.
[c1-l002 >] *¶* Vma, cura, yoroi, cabuto, tachi, ca-
[c1-l002 note] The example lists a horse, saddle, armour, helmet, long sword, and the beginning of katana. The final ca- continues on the next physical line; these are Japanese items rather than Portuguese gloss words.
[c1-l003 >] tana made tericacayaqu fodoni det vareta.
[c1-l003 note] Tana completes katana, and the following phrase describes everything shining brilliantly. The final abraded det vareta is retained provisionally; its exact verb spelling is less secure than the preceding list and the Portuguese account of an impressive emergence.
[c1-l004 >] Feiq. *Lib. 2. Sahio tambem veſtido, &*
[c1-l004 note] Feiq. is the Japanese source label, followed by the book reference in italic. The gloss begins an account of coming out so well dressed; enlargement supports Sahio rather than OCR Sabio. The worn tambem is retained without imposing modern word separation.
[c1-l005 >] *luſtroſo com caualo ajaezado, armas, &c. que*
[c1-l005 note] The Portuguese continues with splendid appearance, an adorned horse, and weapons. The relative que leads into the emitted brilliance on the next row and supports the Japanese shining example.
[c1-l006 >] *deitaua de ſi reſplandor.*
[c1-l006 note] The figure gives off radiance: this completes the Portuguese explanation of terikakayaku. The short row belongs to the preceding example, not to the following fabric headword.
[c1-l007] Terifu. *Certa laya de linho fino, ou teada da*
[c1-l007 note] Terifu names a kind of fine linen or fabric. The initial has the same T type as neighbouring headwords, correcting OCR Ferifu; the printed teada is retained rather than silently changed to a modern textile expression.
[c1-l008 >] *Cǒria.*
[c1-l008 note] Coria identifies Korea as the source of the cloth. The isolated vowel mark is a downward caron in this impression, so Cǒria replaces the OCR tilde while preserving the unusual printed form.
[c1-l009] Terivatari, ru, atta. *Reſplandecer, ou relu-*
[c1-l009 note] Teriwatari means shining over an area or in many places. The Portuguese alternatives resplandecer and reluzir give the same luminous sense; relu- continues into the following row.
[c1-l010 >] *zir por muitas partes.*
[c1-l010 note] Zir completes reluzir, followed by 'in many places'. This spatial extension distinguishes teriwatari from the simpler shining verb at the top of the column.
[c1-l011] Tenſacu. *Couſa que hum plantou, ou ſemeou.*
[c1-l011 note] Tensaku denotes something one has planted or sown. The scan supports the n and long s; the form is not shortened to an expected modern compound merely because the next gloss concerns work done by hand.
[c1-l012 >] *¶ Item, Couſa que hum faz por ſuas mãos.*
[c1-l012 note] The second sense is something made with one's own hands. It broadens the previous cultivation sense to personal handiwork; the paragraph sign introduces a meaning within the same entry.
[c1-l013] Teſaqi. *Diante das mãos. ¶* Teſaqinogin.
[c1-l013 note] Tesaki is first glossed as what lies before the hands. Tesaki no jin then supplies a military use, with the following Portuguese row identifying the front of an army.
[c1-l014 >] *Parte dianteira do exercito. ¶ Item, Cordas*
[c1-l014 note] The first phrase translates tesaki no jin as the foremost part of the army. A new sense starts with ropes, whose length and use in a large net are completed below.
[c1-l015 >] *compridas da rede grande que puxão dambas*
[c1-l015 note] These are long ropes attached to a large net, pulled from both sides. Dambas remains the printed contraction; the two-sided description continues in the short following row.
[c1-l016 >] *as partes.*
[c1-l016 note] As partes completes dambas as partes, 'from both sides'. This is the end of the fishing-net sense of tesaki and must remain a distinct physical continuation.
[c1-l017] Teſocudai. l, texocudai. *Caſtiçal de ferro.*
[c1-l017 note] Tesokudai and teshokudai are alternative forms for an iron candlestick. The lower-case l is the alternative marker, while the later Texocudai entry supplies the reciprocal ordering.
[c1-l018] Teſſa. Curoganeno cuſari. *Cadea de ferro. S.*
[c1-l018 note] Tessa is explained by kurogane no kusari, an iron chain. The final S is an upright written-register label, distinct from the italic Portuguese translation.
[c1-l019] Tetare. *Peßoa deſtra em algũa couſa de mãos.*
[c1-l019 note] Tetare denotes a person skilled in some manual activity. The Portuguese specifies dexterity of the hands; it does not identify a particular trade or craft.
[c1-l020] Tetori. *Homẽ deſtro na luta. ¶ Item, Sa-*
[c1-l020 note] Tetori first means a skilful wrestler, then a shrewd or elusive person. The clearly italic Sa- begins sagaz on the next row, correcting the OCR's broken font markup inside the word.
[c1-l021 >] *gaz, & ſutil em trapaças, &c. eſcapolin-*
[c1-l021 note] Gaz completes sagaz, and the gloss describes subtlety in tricks and escaping. Escapolin- continues its verb on the next line; the passage extends the dexterity sense figuratively.
[c1-l022 >] *do, ou liurandoſe facilmente de qualquer*
[c1-l022 note] Do completes the escaping verb; the alternative explains freeing oneself easily from any difficulty. The object follows below, so the physical line is a continuation of the same character description.
[c1-l023 >] *embaraço, ou negocio, &c.*
[c1-l023 note] Embarrassment or a difficult affair completes the description of someone who extricates himself deftly. The visible ascender supports embaraço, correcting OCR emoaraço.
[c1-l024] Tetba. *Caualo feito de ferro.*
[c1-l024 note] Tetba is an iron horse, explicitly a horse made of iron. The bare t before b belongs to the historical romanization and is not expanded into an extra syllable.
[c1-l025] Tetbǒ. Curoganeno bǒ. *Bordão, ou baſtão*
[c1-l025 note] Tetboo is an iron staff or cudgel, also expressed by kurogane no boo. The initial is T rather than OCR r, and both long-o marks visibly point downward.
[c1-l026 >] *de ferro.*
[c1-l026 note] De ferro completes both Portuguese alternatives for the iron staff. The material is essential to the headword and its Japanese explanatory phrase.
[c1-l027] Tetçugui. *Succeßão de hũs a outros, ou paßar*
[c1-l027 note] Tetsugi means succession from one person to another, or passing onward. The Portuguese description continues into the hand-to-hand formulation below.
[c1-l028 >] *de mão em mão. ¶* Tetçugui ſǒtenno
[c1-l028 note] The gloss completes the idea of passing from hand to hand. Tetçugui ſǒtenno begins the transmitted-doctrine example; the print appears to have t in ſǒtenno, retained although the context suggests the familiar transmission word sōden.
[c1-l029 >] voxiye. *Doutrina que vem por tradição, ou*
[c1-l029 note] Voxiye completes the Japanese phrase for a teaching handed down through succession. The Portuguese directly explains a doctrine received through tradition; its alternative is completed below.
[c1-l030 >] *ſucceßão de huns a outros.*
[c1-l030 note] This completes the explanation as succession from some people to others. The scan supports the familiar ß-shaped type in ſucceßão and the t in outros, correcting OCR ſuccejão and ouros.
[c1-l031] Tetçuzzura. *Ceſto o canaſtra pequena de vi-*
[c1-l031 note] Tetsuzura denotes a small basket or hamper. The damaged but legible canaſtra pequena replaces the garbled OCR; the printed conjunction appears to be only o and is retained without supplying an expected u.
[c1-l032 >] *mes, ou certa era.*
[c1-l032 note] Mes completes vimes, the basket-making material; the gloss also mentions a certain era, an ivy-like plant term. The printed era is preserved without adding the h of a familiar later spelling or naming a precise species.
[c1-l033] Tetdai. *Ajuda, ou ajudante. ¶* Tetdai
[c1-l033 note] Tetdai denotes help or a helper. The repeated form begins the verbal construction on the next line, distinguishing the noun from the act of assisting.
[c1-l034 >] ſuru. *Dar ajuda a outro, ou ſer ajudante.*
[c1-l034 note] Suru completes tetdai suru, to give another help or act as an assistant. The Portuguese supplies both the action and role, so this is one example rather than a new headword.
[c1-l035] Tete. l, chichi. *Pay.*
[c1-l035 note] Tete and chichi are alternative Japanese words for father. The dictionary gives only the concise Portuguese pay and does not add a social or regional distinction here.
[c1-l036] Tetgiǒ. Curoganeno tçuye. *Bordão de fer-*
[c1-l036 note] Tetjoo is equated with kurogane no tsuye, an iron staff. The final long-o mark is abraded but retained as the baseline caron; the Portuguese ferro divides across the row boundary.
[c1-l037 >] *ro, ou baſtão. S.*
[c1-l037 note] Ro completes ferro, followed by the alternative 'staff'. The upright S is a written-register label and is kept outside the italic gloss.
[c1-l038] Tetguio. *Peixe que fica de pois de paßar o car-*
[c1-l038 note] Tetgyo describes a fish remaining after a cart has passed. The T-initial headword is supported by the repeated example below; the separate de pois is preserved as printed.
[c1-l039 >] *ro no caminho em algũa pouca dagoa, ou lama.*
[c1-l039 note] Ro completes carro. The fish remains in a little water or mud on the road, establishing the literal scene from which the following figurative comparison is drawn.
[c1-l040 >] *Traz ſe por comparação do que eſcapa dalgum*
[c1-l040 note] The dictionary applies the fish by comparison to someone escaping a danger. The relative construction continues below; the figurative explanation belongs to the same headword.
[c1-l041 >] *grande perigo, &c. Vt,* Tetguio mizzuuo
[c1-l041 note] The danger is a great one. Tetguio mizzuuo begins the Japanese example of the fish obtaining water; visible guio replaces the OCR guto and agrees with the headword.
[c1-l042 >] Vruga gotocu yorocôda. *Alegrou ſe como*
[c1-l042 note] Uru ga gotoku yorokonda completes the example: he rejoiced like that fish obtaining water. The historical contracted form yorocôda remains unexpanded, and the capital V is preserved.
[c1-l043 >] *peixe que paßado o carro acha algũa agoa com*
[c1-l043 note] The Portuguese specifies a fish that finds water after the cart passes. This makes the relief or escape comparison explicit; com leads into the pleasure described on the next row.
[c1-l044 >] *que folga.*
[c1-l044 note] Que folga completes the account of the water in which the fish finds relief. The short continuation closes the figurative tetgyo example.
[c1-l045] Tetguiǔ. Curoganeno vxi. *Boy, ou vaca*
[c1-l045 note] Tetgyuu is an iron ox or cow, explained by kurogane no ushi. Enlargement supports Tetguiǔ rather than OCR Fetgurǔ; the headword is also identified in NINJAL's supplied 202510 lexical index, without treating that index as authority for the printed marks.
[c1-l046 >] *feita de ferro.*
[c1-l046 note] Made of iron completes the preceding ox-or-cow definition. The feminine agreement follows vaca and is retained rather than altered to agree with the earlier alternative boy.
[c1-l047] Tetguàn. Curoganeno marucaxi. *Pilou-*
[c1-l047 note] Tetgwan is an iron ball or projectile, paraphrased as kurogane no marukashi. The abraded headword is supported by the gloss and NINJAL's Tetguan entry; a small grave-like stroke above a is retained provisionally as Tetguàn.
[c1-l048 >] *ro, ou bola de ferro.*
[c1-l048 note] Ro completes pilouro, a shot or ball, and bola de ferro supplies the plain alternative. The row closes the iron-ball entry and is genuine body text above the bottom rule.

[c2-l001] Tetjin. *Homem perfeito no estudo, & letras,*
[c2-l001 note] Tetjin denotes a man accomplished in study and letters. The visible T corrects the OCR r; the description continues with moral integrity on the next line, distinguishing this entry from the surrounding iron compounds.
[c2-l002 >] *& juntamente inteiro, & de boa vida.*
[c2-l002 note] The learned man is also upright and of good life. This completes the definition as both intellectual and moral excellence rather than mere scholarship.
[c2-l003] Tetmon. Curoganeno mon. *Porta de ferro.*
[c2-l003 note] Tetmon is an iron gate, paraphrased by kurogane no mon. Both the Japanese explanation and Portuguese porta de ferro make the material sense explicit.
[c2-l004] Tetrin. Curoganeno va. *Roda, ou circulo*
[c2-l004 note] Tetrin is an iron wheel or ring, with kurogane no wa as its Japanese explanation. The Portuguese alternatives continue with the material on the next row.
[c2-l005 >] *de ferro.*
[c2-l005 note] De ferro completes the wheel-or-circle definition. The two r letters, one weak, support ferro rather than OCR ferto.
[c2-l006] Tettai. Canagutçu. *Ferraduras.*
[c2-l006 note] Tettai is equated with kanagutsu and translated as horseshoes. The dictionary's concise equivalence is retained without importing other possible meanings of the Japanese explanatory word.
[c2-l007] Tettçui. *Martelo de ferro.*
[c2-l007 note] Tettsui is an iron hammer. The tç sequence belongs to the historical romanization, while the Portuguese gives the implement and its material directly.
[c2-l008] Tettei. Soconi touoru. *Penetrar interior-*
[c2-l008 note] Tettei is explained as soko ni tooru, passing through to the bottom or interior. The Portuguese interior- is abraded at o, but its residual curve and the following mente support interiormente rather than OCR interiermente.
[c2-l009 >] *mente. S.*
[c2-l009 note] Mente completes interiormente, 'inwardly' or 'into the interior'. The final S is visibly upright and marks written usage rather than belonging to the italic adverb.
[c2-l010] Tettǒ. *Catana de ferro. S.*
[c2-l010 note] Tettoo is an iron sword, described by the Portuguese noun catana. Ferro corrects the OCR ferto; the downward long-o mark and upright written-register S are preserved.
[c2-l011] Tevaqe. *Diſtribuição do exercito, ou eſqua-*
[c2-l011 note] Tewake is the distribution of an army or its squadrons. The abraded but coherent exercito replaces OCR exreito; esqua- continues into drões below.
[c2-l012 >] *drões, ou de outra gente pera diuerſas partes.*
[c2-l012 note] Drões completes esquadrões. The definition also covers other people being assigned to different places; the visible t in outra restores the OCR's oura.
[c2-l013 >] *Vt,* Tevaqeuo ſuru.
[c2-l013 note] Tewake wo suru is the Japanese example for making that distribution or division of people. Vt is italic, while the example is upright.
[c2-l014] Tevataxi. *Entrega de algũa couſa preſencial.*
[c2-l014 note] Tewatashi means handing something over personally, as the Portuguese presencial specifies. The initial is the same T type as its repeated example, correcting OCR revataxi.
[c2-l015 >] *Vt,* Tevataxiuo ſuru.
[c2-l015 note] Tewatashi wo suru is the example for making a personal delivery or handover. It remains a separate physical line even though the short example has no additional Portuguese translation.
[c2-l016] Teuoi, ô, ôta. l, teuo vô. *Ser ferido.*
[c2-l016 note] Teoi and the alternative te wo ou mean receiving a wound or being wounded. The principal forms and circumflexes are retained as printed; they are not modernized to a present-day inflection.
[c2-l017 >] *¶* Iǒchǔno tçuuamonoua imada ichinin
[c2-l017 note] Joochuu no tsuwamono wa imada ichinin begins the example about soldiers within a fortress: not even one had yet been wounded. The negative construction is completed on the next row.
[c2-l018 >] mo teuo vouazu. Taif. *Lib. 16. Nẽ*
[c2-l018 note] Mo te wo owazu completes the statement that not one soldier was wounded. Taif. is the visibly upright source label; Lib. 16 and Nẽ begin the italic reference and Portuguese rendering. The OCR's mixed font fragments inside Taif. are removed.
[c2-l019 >] *hum ſò foi ferido dos ſoldados q̃ estauão dentro*
[c2-l019 note] Nẽ hum states that not a single one of the soldiers was wounded. The q̃ abbreviation remains unexpanded, and the phrase identifying where the soldiers were continues below.
[c2-l020 >] *da fortaleza.*
[c2-l020 note] Da fortaleza completes 'inside the fortress', matching joochuu in the Japanese example. This short continuation closes the quoted military illustration.
[c2-l021] Teuoi. *Ferido.*
[c2-l021 note] Teoi is now entered separately as 'wounded'. Its concise adjectival or nominal use is distinct from the preceding entry's verb and principal forms.
[c2-l022] Tevotori. *Inferior em algũa couſa.*
[c2-l022 note] Teotori means inferior in some matter or skill. The following paired entry sets it against temasari, giving explicit comparative context.
[c2-l023] Tevotori, temaſari. *Inferior, & eminente*
[c2-l023 note] Teotori and temasari are paired as inferior and outstanding. They are contrasting terms here, not two interchangeable spellings; the shared field of comparison follows below.
[c2-l024 >] *em algũa couſa.*
[c2-l024 note] Em algũa couſa completes the comparison as inferiority or superiority in some particular matter. It applies to both terms in the preceding row.
[c2-l025] Tevouoxi. *Peſſoa aque hum fez homem, ou*
[c2-l025 note] Teooshi denotes someone whom another person has brought up or made into a man. The scan prints the closely joined aque; the explanation continues with being raised beside that person.
[c2-l026 >] *criou junto de ſi.*
[c2-l026 note] Criou junto de si completes the description of someone brought up in one's own care. The sense concerns personal upbringing or formation, not simply physical proximity.
[c2-l027] Tevye. *Superior, ou auẽtajado em algũa couſa.*
[c2-l027 note] Teue means superior or more advanced in some respect. The Portuguese auẽtajado retains its printed abbreviation tilde rather than being expanded to modern spelling.
[c2-l028] Tevyena. *Idem. l, couſa ſuperior, ou auen-*
[c2-l028 note] Teuena is the corresponding adjectival expression, glossed as something superior or advantaged. The clearly pointed v replaces OCR u; the Portuguese auen- continues on the next line.
[c2-l029 >] *tajada.*
[c2-l029 note] Tajada completes auentajada, agreeing with cousa in the preceding row. The single-word continuation is part of the adjectival gloss rather than an independent item.
[c2-l030] Tevyeni. *Adu. Auentajadamente.*
[c2-l030 note] Teueni gives the adverbial form, 'in an advantageous or superior manner'. The headword again has visible v, while Adu. and the Portuguese gloss are italic.
[c2-l031] Texa. *Eſgrimidor destro.*
[c2-l031 note] Tesha denotes a skilful swordsman or fencer. The Portuguese describes dexterity in fencing, without specifying a school or rank; its provisional st typography is retained.
[c2-l032] Texebai. *Peßoa de pequeno coração, ou de*
[c2-l032 note] Tesebai describes a narrow-hearted person. The gloss introduces a parallel image of short hands, clarified below in terms of giving and undertaking substantial acts.
[c2-l033 >] *mãos curtas pera dar, ou pera fazer outras cou*
[c2-l033 note] The short hands signify limited willingness to give or do other great things. The line ends with cou without a visible hyphen; none is supplied merely because the word continues.
[c2-l034 >] *ſas grandes.*
[c2-l034 note] Sas completes cousas grandes, 'great things'. It closes the figurative account of narrow-heartedness and limited generosity or undertaking.
[c2-l035] Texiuo. *Sal que ſe poem na meſa. ¶* Te-
[c2-l035 note] Teshio is salt placed on the table. The example begins Te- in upright Japanese type; the OCR had incorrectly included its T inside the italic Portuguese span.
[c2-l036 >] xiuouo voqu. *Por ſal na meſa.*
[c2-l036 note] Xiuouo completes teshio wo, followed by oku: putting salt on the table. The Portuguese por is the infinitive 'to put', retained without adding a modern distinguishing accent.
[c2-l037] Texocudai. l, teſocudai. *Castiçal de ferro.*
[c2-l037 note] Teshokudai and tesokudai are alternative forms for an iron candlestick, reversing the order of the earlier Teſocudai entry. The printed provisional st form in Castiçal is left unchanged.
[c2-l038] Texxa. Curoganeno iſago. *Area da praya*
[c2-l038 note] Tessha is iron sand, paraphrased as kurogane no isago. The Portuguese first identifies sand from a beach, then describes its colour or metallurgical use below.
[c2-l039 >] *de cor de ferro, ou de que ſe faz ferro. Item,*
[c2-l039 note] The sand is iron-coloured or yields iron. Item introduces another sense, iron filings, on the following row; the dictionary's alternatives are preserved without imposing a modern mineral classification.
[c2-l040 >] *Limaduras de ferro.*
[c2-l040 note] Iron filings provide the additional tessha sense. This short Portuguese row completes the same entry rather than introducing another Japanese headword.
[c2-l041] Texxen. Curoganeno fune. *Embarcação for-*
[c2-l041 note] Tessen is an iron-covered vessel, explained by kurogane no fune. The scan joins Curoganeno rather than the OCR's separated Curogane no; for- begins the description of its cladding.
[c2-l042 >] *rada, & chapeada de ferro.*
[c2-l042 note] Rada completes forrada, and chapeada specifies iron plating. The entry describes a vessel lined or clad with iron, without asserting that every part of its structure is iron.
[c2-l043] Texxeqi. Curogane, ixi. *Ferro, & pedra.*
[c2-l043 note] Tesseki is explained by the paired Japanese nouns kurogane and ishi, iron and stone. Their comma and the Portuguese coordination preserve the two-part explanation.
[c2-l044] Texxi, ſuru, ita. *Paßar auante, penetrar, ou*
[c2-l044 note] Tesshi, tessuru, tesshita means advancing or penetrating through something. The scan supports penetrar with t, correcting OCR peneirar; the fuller scholarly application continues below.
[c2-l045 >] *chegar ao cabo nas letras, ou qual quer eſtudo.*
[c2-l045 note] The gloss extends to reaching the end or full mastery of letters or any study. Letras and qual quer correct garbled OCR while preserving the visible space within qual quer.
[c2-l046 >] *¶* Gacumonni teſſuru. *Estar muito auã-*
[c2-l046 note] Gakumon ni tessuru means attaining thorough accomplishment in learning. The Portuguese begins 'to be very advanced'; its abbreviated auã- continues into te on the next physical line.
[c2-l047 >] *te, & conſumado no estudo, ou ter bem pe-*
[c2-l047 note] Te completes the description as advanced and accomplished in study. The alternative ends at pe-, with the separately set catchword netrado anticipating the continuation of penetrado beyond this page.
