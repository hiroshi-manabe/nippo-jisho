[c1-l001 >>] *ſe dira,* Futaauaxe, miauaxe.
[c1-l001 note] Continuing f199's prohibition, the dictionary says not to form Futaauaxe or miauaxe. The negative context is essential: these are rejected analogies, not recommended expressions.
[c1-l002] Fitobanare. *O eſtar afastado dos homẽs.*
[c1-l002 note] Hitobanare means separation from other people. The following example concerns residence away from human company.
[c1-l003 >] *¶* Fitobanarenaru ſumaiuo ſuru. *Habi-*
[c1-l003 note] Hitobanare naru sumai wo suru is living in an isolated dwelling. Habi begins the Portuguese inhabiting verb below.
[c1-l004 >] *tar em deſerto, ou apartado dos homẽs, &c.*
[c1-l004 note] Living in a wilderness or apart from people completes the example. Deserto need not mean a modern sandy desert here.
[c1-l005] Fitobito. *Homẽs.*
[c1-l005 note] Hitobito is the plural or collective people. Homẽs is the corresponding Portuguese plural with nasal abbreviation.
[c1-l006] Fitobitoxij, l, fitor*a*xij. *Homem que he*
[c1-l006 note] Hitobitoshii or hitorashii denotes someone properly human in conduct. He is the Portuguese copula, not OCR's be.
[c1-l007 >] *propriamente homem no ſaber, corteſias, &c.*
[c1-l007 note] Knowledge and courtesies exemplify that proper conduct. Corteſias restores the damaged letter without interpreting the adjective as merely biological humanity.
[c1-l008] Fitobõ. *Hum bacio, ou prato de pao com fruita,*
[c1-l008 note] Hitobon is one tray or dish sent with fruit. The mark is a tilde in Fitobõ, not a long-o caron; the tray sense and NINJAL corroborate bon.
[c1-l009 >] *ou outra couſa que ſe manda de preſente.*
[c1-l009 note] Other things may also be sent as the gift. This completes the tray-and-contents description.
[c1-l010] Fitocado. i, Iccado. *Adu. Grande, &*
[c1-l010 note] Hitokado is equated with ikkado, notably or considerably. The doubled c in Iccado is supported by the scan and lexical relationship.
[c1-l011 >] *notauelmente.*
[c1-l011 note] Notauelmente completes the adverbial sense. The adjective on the next line is a separate derived form.
[c1-l012] Fitocadono. *Couſa grande, & notauel.*
[c1-l012 note] Hitokado no means great or notable. The no construction turns the preceding expression into a modifier.
[c1-l013] Fitocari. *Hũa ſaida à caça.*
[c1-l013 note] Hitokari is one hunting outing. The prefix hito counts the excursion rather than describing a person hunting.
[c1-l014] Fitocata. i, Ippǒ. *Hũa parte. ¶ Item,*
[c1-l014 note] Hitokata is ippō, one side or part. Hũa repairs the damaged initial in the Portuguese equivalent.
[c1-l015 >] *De hũa laya junto com eſte verbo,* Naru, *no*
[c1-l015 note] Another construction uses naru in the negative. Naru is Japanese embedded in the Portuguese grammatical explanation and is eligible for typeface toggling.
[c1-l016 >] *negatiuo. Vt,* Fitocatanaranu vomoigia.
[c1-l016 note] Hitokata naranu omoi ja introduces varied or extraordinary concerns. The dictionary explicitly requires the negative, so do not normalize the phrase to affirmative naru.
[c1-l017 >] *São varios, & diuerſos cuidados, ou penſa-*
[c1-l017 note] São varios e diversos translates the plurality of cares. São repairs OCR's impossible Sãs; penſa begins thoughts below.
[c1-l018 >] *mentos triſtes.*
[c1-l018 note] Mentos completes penſamentos, followed by sad. The compressed printed spacing is retained rather than used to change the meaning.
[c1-l019] Fitodama. *Exalação aceſa que aparece no ar à*
[c1-l019 note] Hitodama is described as a burning exhalation appearing in the air. This is the dictionary's account of a luminous apparition, not a scientific identification supplied by the review.
[c1-l020 >] *maneira de bolla em ſinal de alguem auer de*
[c1-l020 note] It resembles a ball and is taken as a sign concerning someone's death. Alguem restores the spurious final i in the OCR.
[c1-l021 >] *morrer logo como cuidão os gentios.*
[c1-l021 note] The death is expected soon, according to the belief attributed to non-Christians. The attribution is part of the source's viewpoint, not an endorsement.
[c1-l022] Fitodane. i, Fitono tane. *Gèração, ou ſe-*
[c1-l022 note] Hitodane is hito no tane, human seed or lineage. The Portuguese pairs generation with seed, continued below.
[c1-l023 >] *mente dos homẽs. ¶* Fitodanega tçuqu-
[c1-l023 note] The example hitodane ga tsuku-ru concerns the ending of human lineage. The final ru completes the historical verb form on the next line.
[c1-l024 >] ru. *Acabarſe todo o genero humano.*
[c1-l024 note] The Portuguese gloss is the end of the entire human race. This supports exhaustion of the lineage rather than producing or making it.
[c1-l025] Fitode. *Mãos do homem. Vt,* Fitodeni
[c1-l025 note] Hitode is another person's hands. Hito de ni kakaru requires ni, restored from OCR's ri and supported by the scan.
[c1-l026 >] cacaru. *Cair nas mãs de alguem.*
[c1-l026 note] Kakaru completes falling into someone's hands. The printed mãs is retained without supplying the vowel expected in modern mãos.
[c1-l027] Fitofari. *Hum ponto, ou hũa agulhada das*
[c1-l027 note] Hitohari is one stitch or needle puncture. Ponto corrects the damaged vowel in the Portuguese equivalent.
[c1-l028 >] *que dão por mezinha em Iapão.*
[c1-l028 note] The needle puncture may be administered as treatment in Japan. This is a historical definition, not present-day medical advice.
[c1-l029] Fitofata. *Vt,* Fitofata tatçuru. *Fazer*
[c1-l029 note] Hitohata tatsuru means accomplishing a distinguished deed. The literal banner image is developed in the next lines.
[c1-l030 >] *algũa obra aſsinalada como quẽ aleuanta hũa*
[c1-l030 note] An outstanding deed is compared to raising a banner. The worn word before bandeira is hũa, not the OCR's biã.
[c1-l031 >] *bandeira na fortaleza dos inimigos, &c.*
[c1-l031 note] The banner is raised on an enemy fortress. This completes the martial example of distinction.
[c1-l032] Fitofatabari. *Hum pano, ou hũa largura de*
[c1-l032 note] Hitohatabari is one width of cloth. The missing a is supported by the scan and the headword reference; the interior dot-like damage is not retained as punctuation.
[c1-l033 >] *pano, canga, &c.*
[c1-l033 note] Pano and canga name cloth examples. The gloss measures a cloth width rather than introducing another garment headword.
[c1-l034] Fitofazzure. *O deſcrepardos outros, ou não*
[c1-l034 note] Hitohazure is deviation from other people. The following definition gives a disapproving social sense, not simply being physically outside a crowd.
[c1-l035 >] *ſer de laya dos outros homens por reſpeito dal-*
[c1-l035 note] The person is unlike others because of a defect or serious offence. The phrase de laya dos outros expresses belonging to their kind.
[c1-l036 >] *gum defeito, ou crime notauel.*
[c1-l036 note] Defeito and crime notauel finish that explanation. Defeito repairs the OCR c while preserving the source's judgement.
[c1-l037] Fitofiqi. i, Fitofiqino vma. *Caualo brauo*
[c1-l037 note] Hitohiki no uma is a difficult horse that resists its handler. The headword's t is confirmed by the repeated example and scan.
[c1-l038 >] *que não obedece ao freo, nem aquem o toma*
[c1-l038 note] The horse obeys neither the bridle nor the person taking hold of it. Freo is the printed period term.
[c1-l039 >] *pollo freo.*
[c1-l039 note] Pollo freo completes holding it by the bridle. The line closes the horse description rather than starting a new instruction.
[c1-l040] Fitofôdai. *Meliùs,* fitoxidai. *A vontade dos*
[c1-l040 note] Hitohōdai means at others' will; hitoshidai is explicitly preferred. Preserve both rather than replace the less-preferred headword.
[c1-l041 >] *outros. Vt,* Fitofôdaina monogia. *He*
[c1-l041 note] Hitohōdai na mono ja describes a person governed by others. The translation's He begins its predicate below.
[c1-l042 >] *homem que ſe rege pellos outros, ou faz o que*
[c1-l042 note] He follows others or does what they want. Homem restores the OCR b while the definition concerns dependence, not generosity.
[c1-l043 >] *os outros querem.*
[c1-l043 note] Os outros querem completes the others' wishes. It closes the explanation of hitohōdai.
[c1-l044] Fitofôdaini. *Adu. Vt,* Fitofôdaini ſu-
[c1-l044 note] Hitohōdai ni is the adverbial form. The repeated ni below supports restoring the damaged final ni in the headword.
[c1-l045 >] ru. *Fazer à vontade do que os outros querẽ.*
[c1-l045 note] Suru completes acting at others' will. Vontade and outros restore meaningful Portuguese letters without changing its phrasing.
[c1-l046] Fitofude. *Hũa penada, ou couſa eſcrita*
[c1-l046 note] Hitofude is one stroke or a very short written item. Ou couſa eſcrita restores the conjunction and missing c in the draft.
[c1-l047 >] *muito breue.*
[c1-l047 note] Muito breue completes the short-written-item sense. It is the final body line of this column.

[c2-l001] Fitogamaxij. *Oparecer homem, ou varão nas*
[c2-l001 note] Hitogamashii is appearing manly or socially substantial in one's actions. The next lines qualify that this can be excessive.
[c2-l002 >] *obras, corteſias, &c. às vezes ſe toma por*
[c2-l002 note] Conduct and courtesies are the relevant qualities. Às vezes introduces a less favourable interpretation.
[c2-l003 >] *moſtrarſe muito homẽ com algũ exceſſo, &c.*
[c2-l003 note] It can mean making an excessive show of being a man. Homẽ corrects the damaged initial while preserving the abbreviated ending.
[c2-l004] Fitogara. *Feição, ou compoſtura de homem.*
[c2-l004 note] Hitogara denotes a person's character or bearing. The Portuguese feição and compoſtura cover both disposition and presentation.
[c2-l005] Fitogatachi. *Figura, ou feição de homem.*
[c2-l005 note] Hitogatachi is human form or appearance. It differs from the broader character sense of hitogara.
[c2-l006 >] *Vt,* Fitogatachimonai. *Não tem jà figu-*
[c2-l006 note] Hitogatachi mo nai says the person no longer has a human-looking form. The physical reasons are given below.
[c2-l007 >] *ra de homem por eſtar muito magro, ou des*
[c2-l007 note] Extreme thinness or exhaustion accounts for the loss of appearance. Des begins desfeito on the next line.
[c2-l008 >] *feito, ou todo cortado, &c.*
[c2-l008 note] Being worn away or badly cut completes the example. The description concerns bodily appearance, not a moral judgement.
[c2-l009] Fitogocochi. *Coração, ou ſentido humano.*
[c2-l009 note] Hitogokochi is human feeling or consciousness. Coração restores the cedilla-type consonant from OCR's long s.
[c2-l010 >] *Vt,* Xiniitte fitogocochimo nacatta.
[c2-l010 note] Shiniitte hitogokochi mo nakatta describes being near death or unconscious. The doubled i in Xiniitte is present and is not collapsed to the draft's Xinitte.
[c2-l011 >] *Eſtando com accidente mortal, ou eſmorecido*
[c2-l011 note] The Portuguese describes a mortal attack or faintness. This supports the loss-of-consciousness reading rather than completed death.
[c2-l012 >] *não tinha jà ſentido nem coração de homem.*
[c2-l012 note] The person no longer had human awareness. Jà is the Portuguese adverb, not ſà, and the terminal mark is a period rather than a continuation hyphen.
[c2-l013] Fitogoto. *Cada peſſoa, ou cada homem.*
[c2-l013 note] Hitogoto is every person in this entry. Cada peſſoa explicitly gives the distributive sense, not someone else's affairs.
[c2-l014] Fitoguemonai. *Não ſer hum homem no pri-*
[c2-l014 note] Hitoge mo nai denotes a lack of proper human refinement. The gloss's pri begins primor below.
[c2-l015 >] *mor, corteſia, &c.*
[c2-l015 note] Primor and corteſia complete the qualities lacking. This is a social-conduct expression, not physical absence of people.
[c2-l016] Fitoi. *Hum dia. Vt,* Fitoi, futçucano
[c2-l016 note] Hitoi means one day. The example compares a span of one or two days, retaining the historical hitoi form.
[c2-l017 >] aida. *Em eſpaço de hum, ou dous dias.*
[c2-l017 note] Aida completes the interval expression. Dias repairs the OCR's atas in the Portuguese translation.
[c2-l018 >] *¶ Item, Os dias atras.*
[c2-l018 note] Another sense refers to days past. The dictionary does not specify a fixed number of days in this short gloss.
[c2-l019] Fitoigi, l, fifitoigi. *Caminho de hum dia, ou*
[c2-l019 note] Hitoiji or fihitoiji is a day's journey. Preserve the visibly unusual initial repetition of the alternative, also recorded by NINJAL.
[c2-l020 >] *hũa jornada.*
[c2-l020 note] Hũa jornada restates a single day's travel. It closes the distance or journey sense.
[c2-l021] Fitojichi. *Refens. ¶* Fitojichiuo toru.
[c2-l021 note] Hitojichi is a hostage; hitojichi wo toru means taking hostages. Reſens uses an s-type, not the f read by OCR.
[c2-l022 >] *Tomar refens. ¶* Fitojichiuo idaſu.
[c2-l022 note] Hitojichi wo idasu means providing hostages. The repeated o restores the draft's Fitajichi without altering the particle boundary.
[c2-l023 >] *Dar refens. ¶* Fitojichiuo cayeſu. *Tor-*
[c2-l023 note] Hitojichi wo kaesu means returning the hostages. Tor begins tornar in the Portuguese gloss below.
[c2-l024 >] *nar os refens.* Fitojichiuo ſutçuru. *Não*
[c2-l024 note] After returning hostages, hitojichi wo sutsuru introduces abandoning them. The negation at the right begins the explanation of disregarding their fate.
[c2-l025 >] *ter deuer com os refens deixando os catiuar, ou*
[c2-l025 note] The hostages are disregarded, allowing capture or worse. The text's deuer construction is preserved rather than rephrased in modern Portuguese.
[c2-l026 >] *matar, &c.*
[c2-l026 note] Matar completes the possible fate, killing. This is a historical lexical example, not an instruction.
[c2-l027] Fitoiqi. *Hum folego. Vt,* Fitoiqini mo-
[c2-l027 note] Hitoiki is one breath. Hitoiki ni mono wo suru begins an example of acting in one sustained effort.
[c2-l028 >] nouo ſuru. *Fazer hũa couſa de hum fole-*
[c2-l028 note] Mono wo suru completes doing a thing in one breath. Fazer and folego repair OCR's two initial-letter errors.
[c2-l029 >] *go, ou impeto ſem deſcanſar. ¶* Fitoiqi-
[c2-l029 note] The act proceeds without a rest. A second hitoiki ni example introduces sleeping in one stretch.
[c2-l030 >] ni nuru. *Leuar anoite, ou tempo que hum*
[c2-l030 note] Hitoiki ni nuru means sleeping continuously. Hum is the generic sleeper in the Portuguese clause, not tum.
[c2-l031 >] *dorme de hum ſono.*
[c2-l031 note] De hum ſono completes one uninterrupted sleep. The definition covers a night or whatever sleeping period is involved.
[c2-l032] Fitomane. *O arremedar homens. Vt,* Fito
[c2-l032 note] Hitomane is imitating other people. Hito begins the repeated example, divided across the row.
[c2-l033 >] maneuo ſuru.
[c2-l033 note] Mane wo suru completes imitation. The visible e is restored where OCR gave c in mancuo.
[c2-l034] Fitomato. *Homem aquem atirão com ſetas*
[c2-l034 note] Hitomato is a person used as an arrow target. Homem and atirão restore a coherent Portuguese sentence from the damaged draft.
[c2-l035 >] *como à aluo.*
[c2-l035 note] Como à aluo completes the comparison to a target. Preserve the printed grave and preposition rather than modernize them.
[c2-l036] Fitome. *Olhos do homem. ¶* Fitomeuo
[c2-l036 note] Hitome is other people's eyes or notice. The o in the headword is supported by the repeated example below.
[c2-l037 >] fazzuru. *Ter pejo de ſer viſto. ¶* Fito-
[c2-l037 note] Hitome wo hazzuru is being ashamed to be seen. The historical verb form is preserved rather than replaced by modern hajiru.
[c2-l038 >] meuo tçutçumu, l, xinobu. *Eſconderſe*
[c2-l038 note] Hitome wo tsutsumu or shinobu is hiding from others' sight. The m in tçutçumu is supported by the scan and the concealment gloss.
[c2-l039 >] *pera que não ſeja viſto.*
[c2-l039 note] The purpose is not being seen. This completes the hiding example rather than introducing a separate passive form.
[c2-l040] Fitomi. *Menina dos olhos.*
[c2-l040 note] Hitomi is the pupil of the eye. Menina dos olhos is the Portuguese idiom, not a literal girl.
[c2-l041] Fitomichi. i. Ichizzu. *Palaura com que ſe*
[c2-l041 note] Hitomichi is equated with ichizu, resolute singleness of purpose. The following explanation supplies its verbal applications.
[c2-l042 >] *da à entender algũa couſa que ſe faz, ou*
[c2-l042 note] It describes something done or ordered decisively. The conjunction is ou, not the OCR's cu.
[c2-l043 >] *manda executar reſolutamente, como amorte,*
[c2-l043 note] Execution of an order, including a death sentence, illustrates this resolve. The historical example is preserved as source content.
[c2-l044 >] *ou juſtiça dalguem. ¶* Fitomichini vô-
[c2-l044 note] Justice or execution of someone completes the example. Hitomichi ni ō-se begins the honorific order expression.
[c2-l045 >] xe tçuqeraruru. *Mandar reſolutamente*
[c2-l045 note] Ōse tsukeraruru is issuing a firm command. Tçuqe, not tçiqe, fits both the visible vowel and the familiar honorific construction.
[c2-l046 >] *matar a alguem. ¶* Fitomichini cujina-
[c2-l046 note] The first order is to kill someone; the next hitomichi ni kuji na-do concerns bringing a matter to completion. The damaged ni and compound spacing are restored.
[c2-l047 >] douo fataſu. *Concluir, ou acabar algũa*
[c2-l047 note] Kuji nado wo hatasu means concluding such business. The scan supports fataſu as one verb rather than a split before ſu.
