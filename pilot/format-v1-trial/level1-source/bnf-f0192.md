[c1-l005 >] *¶* Cuſauo fiqu. *Arrancar eruas com a mão.*

[c1-l007 >] canauo fiqu. *Repartir pellos que estão à me-*
[c1-l007 note] Sakana o hiku is distributing food to those at table, rather than the unrelated modern reading 'pull a fish'.

[c1-l010] Fiqi, u, ijta. *Diminuir nas contas o contrario*

[c1-l011 note] A further application is deducting debts; di- begins dividas below. Item introduces this additional sense.
[c1-l012 >] *uidas, &c. Meliùs,* Saxifiqi.

[c1-l013] Fiqi, u, ijta. *Serrar. Vt,* Quo fiqu.
[c1-l013 note] Another hiku means sawing. Quo is the printed object form; the following wood/tree gloss guides interpretation without silently changing it to another romanization.

[c1-l014 note] Sawing wood or a tree is followed by an additional sense of working material on a lathe.
[c1-l015 >] *ao torno. ¶* Iuzuuo fiqu. *Fazer contas*

[c1-l016 >] *ao torno.*
[c1-l016 note] Ao torno finishes the bead-turning example; its short independent crop is not a heading or catchword.
[c1-l017] Fiqiacu. *Correo.*
[c1-l017 note] Hikyaku is a courier, corresponding to the compact Portuguese correo gloss.
[c1-l018] Fiqiagari, u, atta. *Retiraremſe os ſolda-*
[c1-l018 note] Hikiagaru here describes soldiers withdrawing to higher ground, rather than merely any upward pull.
[c1-l019 >] *dos pera algum lugar alto.*

[c1-l020] Fiqiague, uru, eta. *Puxar pera riba.*

[c1-l021] Fiqiai, ǒ, ǒta. *Puxarem dous cada hum pe-*
[c1-l021 note] Hikiau is reciprocal pulling, each person drawing toward their own side. The inflectional long vowels are preserved from the marked types.
[c1-l022 >] *ra ſua banda. ¶ Item, Peſar igualmente*
[c1-l022 note] Each pulls toward their side; the additional sense concerns equal weight, continuing onto the following line.
[c1-l023 >] *o fato dambas as partes. ¶* Fiqiauanu ni

[c1-l025] Fiqiaqe, uru, eta. *Puxando abrir. Vt,*

[c1-l029] Fiqiatçume, uru, eta. *Puxando ajuntar.*
[c1-l029 note] Hikiatsumeru is gathering by pulling things together; ajuntar is one Portuguese infinitive, not an article plus verb.
[c1-l030] Fiqiate, tçuru, eta. *Puxando por ſobre algũa*
[c1-l030 note] Hikiateru is drawing something against another surface. The cited example concerns a sword blade.

[c1-l047 note] The load is arranged so that it weighs equally; igualmẽ continues with te at the top of column 2.

[c2-l017] Fiqicaje. *Doença que procede do àr, ou vento.*

[c2-l023 >] *ſobre os joelhos. &c. ¶* Sacamoguiuo

[c2-l024 >] fiqicaquru. *Porpaos, ou aruores com ra-*

[c2-l026 >] *ou noutra parte pera não paſſarem os imigos.*
[c2-l026 note] The barrier prevents enemies from passing. Imigos is retained as the historical spelling, not modernized to inimigos.

[c2-l027 note] Mi ni hikikakete chisō serareta describes generous treatment as of oneself. Chiſô, not chilô, supplies the recognizable hospitality noun.
[c2-l028 >] *me gaſalhado como a ſua propria peſſoa.*
[c2-l028 note] The host treated me as his own person. Gasalhado expresses that welcome, completing fez-me across the line break.
[c2-l029] Fiqicatamuqe, uru, eta. *Inclinar algũa*

[c2-l031 >] *clinar a alguem.*

[c2-l032] Fiqicaye, uru, eta. *Trocar, ou mudar. Per*

[c2-l037] Fiqicayexi, u, eita. *Puxando virar, ou tor-*

[c2-l039] Fiqicazzuqi, u, uita. *Puxando cubrirſe.*

[c2-l041] Fiqichigaye, uru, eta. *Por a parte dian-*
