## catchword [catchword] Printed catchword

[u2b-l001 >>] Tate-
[u2b-l001 note] Read the catchword as Tate-. The worn middle letter is chosen as t by comparison with the neighbouring Tate- headwords, although its incomplete foot makes it look r-like. The raised terminal mark is a hyphen, not a baseline full stop; the following page was not supplied for confirmation.
