[c1-l001 note] Kyasha describes something clean, refined, and pleasing to look at. This headword repeats the preceding page’s catchword Qiaxa, confirming that the latter is furniture rather than another body entry.

[c1-l002 note] Kyashana has the same sense; kyashana hito applies it to a person. Per begins a metaphorical-use label whose met. is printed on the next row.

[c1-l004 >] *certa, & atauia bem. ¶ Item, Homem q̃*
[c1-l004 note] Completes the description of someone who arranges and dresses himself well, then introduces another sense concerning a person’s abilities. The final q̃ is not expanded.
