## Uncertainties and observations

- The ownership stamp obscures part of the final entry in column 1; `Zzumen` and its Japanese gloss are provisional.
- `Zzunguiri`, `Zzurçû`, and several diacritics require a second visual pass.
- Printed `ſ`, abbreviations, line-end division, headers, page number, terminus, and ornament are represented as distinct phenomena rather than flattened into one text stream.
- The stamp is later evidence about the physical copy and is not part of the 1603–1604 dictionary text.
- There is no catchword on the final page.
