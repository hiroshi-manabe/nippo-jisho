# Wikisource comparison: bnf-f0014

## Outcome

Wikisource is useful as a **secondary collation witness** on this page, but the current material is neither accurate nor complete enough to serve as the project's transcription base.

Across the independently transcribed zones, Wikisource:

- agrees with two corrections already found directly from the scan before Wikisource exposure;
- supplies four additional scan-supported corrections;
- contains three clear textual or glyph errors of its own;
- omits or normalizes physical lineation, one repeated running header, and the catchword;
- preserves a potentially useful roman/italic distinction that format version 0 does not yet encode.

The frozen draft remains unchanged. The occurrence-level record is [bnf-f0014.csv](bnf-f0014.csv).

## Blinded checkpoint and scope

The comparison uses the selected zones in the frozen [bnf-f0014 draft](../transcription-v0/bnf-gallica/f0014.md), corresponding to Gallica `f14` and Commons PDF page 16. The project had not opened the corresponding Wikisource text before freezing this draft.

Two readings—`Aburamigaqi` and `acepilhada`—had already been corrected by a direct second inspection at the project owner's prompting. They are preserved in [Pre-Comparison Corrections](../precomparison-corrections.md) and therefore count only as independent confirmation, not value added by Wikisource.

The draft covers selected zones rather than the full page. This trial can evaluate character readings, cross-column continuation, repeated headers, and the final catchword, but it is not a full-page error-rate estimate.

## Coverage survey

As accessed on 2026-07-28, Portuguese Wikisource has page records only for PDF pages 1–16 and 646–653. Of those 24 records:

- 19 are level 0 and contain no transcription;
- four—pages 13–16—are level 1, not proofread;
- one title page—page 11—is level 2, marked problematic;
- none is proofread or validated;
- the tested dictionary page f0248/PDF 250 has no Wikisource page record.

Thus only five of the 653 PDF pages currently contain contributed text, and only pages 15–16 reach dictionary entries. The usable overlap with a blinded frozen pilot is page 16, because page 15 had been seen before the blinded procedure was adopted.

This survey is a snapshot, not a permanent statement about Wikisource; coverage and revisions may change.

## Method

1. Preserved the frozen independent draft and its pre-comparison correction log.
2. Retrieved the exact Wikisource revision and ProofreadPage status.
3. Compared the overlapping selected zones, classifying meaningful differences before adjudication.
4. Checked each disagreement against the native-resolution Gallica `f0014` image, using enlarged crops where necessary.
5. Recorded page-structure and typography differences as well as lexical characters.

The focused scan-adjudication pass ran from `2026-07-28T13:54:22Z` to `2026-07-28T13:56:23Z`, an elapsed time of 2 minutes 1 second. This excludes discovery of the Wikisource page, initial reading of its text, preparation of the comparison record, and the untimed original transcription. It must not be presented as total correction time or as evidence of time saved.

## Principal findings

### New corrections suggested by Wikisource and confirmed from the scan

| Location | Independent reading | Scan-confirmed reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Following `Aburamigaqi` | `Aburamono` | `aburamono` |
| `Aburaqeno mono` example | `Aburaqe ga ſuru` | `Aburaqega ſuru` |
| `Aburicaraxi` definition | `ſeccar` | `ſecar` |
| Final entry | `Aburicauaraque` | `Aburicauarague` |

These are genuine benefits from collation. None should be accepted merely because it appears in Wikisource; each was accepted only after inspection of the scan.

### Cases where the independent draft is better

The independent reading correctly preserves `Barata bicha`, where Wikisource has `bicho`; `Aſſar`, where Wikisource uses the anachronistic capital sharp-s form `Aẞar`; the long `ſ` and printed division in `mi-` / `ſturado`; both running-header instances; and the existence and function of the final catchword.

Wikisource also joins all physical lines. That is useful for continuous reading but cannot replace a page-diplomatic layer designed to preserve printed divisions and continuations.

### Format evidence

The catchword is printed as `Aburico` without a hyphen. The frozen draft successfully identifies it as a divided catchword but stores `Aburico-`, thereby adding a character not visible in the source. Version 1 should store the visible string and the fact of division separately.

Wikisource's italics broadly track the page's distinction between roman Japanese forms and italic Portuguese explanations. Format version 0 currently discards this distinction. A wider sample should determine whether typeface is semantically stable enough to preserve explicitly or can be regenerated safely from entry structure.

## Evaluation and licensing implication

The present evidence does not justify adopting Wikisource as the base transcription:

1. Its contributed-text coverage is extremely small.
2. The available dictionary pages are not proofread or validated.
3. It supplies useful corrections, but it also introduces wrong letters and glyph substitutions.
4. Its continuous-text representation omits structures the project needs in Stage 1.
5. Because the original first-pass work was not timed, this trial cannot establish a productivity gain.

The recommended working policy is therefore:

- continue scan-first independent transcription;
- do not include Wikisource in routine transcription or review;
- consult an external transcription only exceptionally, after an independent checkpoint, when a reading remains unresolved;
- record exact provenance and verify every externally suggested difference against the scan;
- do not copy whole Wikisource pages into the canonical transcription;
- keep the project's final outbound license decision open until the canonical data model is settled, while isolating and attributing any future imported CC BY-SA material.

This policy preserves the option of a permissive license for independently produced project data. It is an editorial recommendation, not legal advice or a final licensing decision.
