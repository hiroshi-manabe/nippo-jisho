# Production review: bnf-f0017

## State

The complete page was transcribed directly from the Gallica master without consulting Wikisource or another running-text transcription. It is `scan_confirmed` after independent comparison, Japanese, Portuguese, bilingual-context, reverse-order glyph, mark, and right-edge passes. NINJAL's 40 source-order headwords (`003a01`–`003b23`) were checked only after the visual record existed and all are represented. Both columns and the page furniture remain pending for human review.

## Review method

1. A first reading was made from the full page and overlapping enlarged column tiles.
2. The page was reread in reverse segment order and compared line by line with the working transcription.
3. Japanese morphology and Jesuit romanization, historical Portuguese, and bilingual coherence were checked separately.
4. Base letters, long `ſ`, abbreviations, diacritics, spacing, and punctuation were inspected independently.
5. Every physical right edge was checked without assuming a division sign from continuation on the next line.
6. All proposed changes were returned to tight enlargement. A fresh final sweep produced no new candidate.
7. The NINJAL inventory was then used as a coverage check, not as diplomatic text.
