# Production Transcription: bnf-f0015

## Purpose and state

`bnf-f0015` is the first sequential production page after the human confirmation of `f14`. It tests a simple change requested after the earlier page's high human correction yield: more genuinely separate verification passes over the same direct-from-scan transcription.

The canonical evidence is the Gallica master `f0015.jpg` identified by the checksum in the Level 1 source. Wikisource was not consulted. NINJAL version 202510 was opened only after an independent visual reading existed and was used to check source-order headword coverage and suspicious forms, not to supply the running text.

The page is `scan_confirmed` and awaits the project owner's column-by-column human check in the review interface.

## Passes performed

1. **Initial visual transcription.** Read both columns in physical order from overlapping sixth-column views, using quarter-column and full-page views for layout and continuations.
2. **Forward token alignment.** Compared every recorded token, punctuation mark, typeface change, and line ending with enlarged source crops.
3. **Japanese and romanization pass.** Parsed headwords, inflections, examples, and particles, then returned every suspicious form to the scan.
4. **Portuguese pass.** Read every gloss for historical spelling, abbreviations, syntax, and line-end continuation without modernizing the print.
5. **Bilingual-context pass.** Compared Japanese and Portuguese meanings to expose readings that were graphically possible but linguistically implausible.
6. **Reverse-order glyph pass.** Worked upward from the final line, separating base-letter decisions from marks and checking `ſ`/`f`, `u`/`v`, `g`/`q`, `i`/`l`, abbreviation marks, tildes, and carons.
7. **Headword coverage pass.** Compared the 37 NINJAL source-order records for `f15` (`002a01`–`002b20`) with the already transcribed page. Every expected entry has a visible counterpart; the scan decided all diplomatic spellings.
8. **Fresh final visual pass.** Repeated the complete scan comparison from the beginning after all amendments. This pass produced no new correction candidate.

## Result

The page contains 93 dictionary-text lines, two running-header lines, one signature line, and one catchword line: 97 physical lines in total. It extends the committed Level 1 production corpus to seven pages and 623 physical lines.

More passes materially improved this page before human review. That is evidence in favor of the simple repeated-pass method, but not yet evidence that it meets the working target of no more than two or three human corrections per page. The next measurement is the project owner's independent check of each f15 column and its furniture.
