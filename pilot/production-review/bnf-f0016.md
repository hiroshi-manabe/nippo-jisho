## Independent-comparison method

This page is the first trial of the procedure adopted after f15 remained above the human-correction target.

1. The first reading was made from the full page and overlapping enlarged column segments.
2. The page was reread in reverse segment order without consulting the first text.
3. The two readings were compared line by line. Differences became candidates rather than automatic edits.
4. Japanese, historical Portuguese, bilingual context, glyph, mark, and spacing passes were run on the resulting text.
5. Every accepted candidate was returned to a tight enlargement; a final full-page sweep produced no new candidate.
6. NINJAL's 36 source-order headwords (`002c01`–`002d19`) were then checked for coverage. The dataset did not determine diplomatic spelling or running text.

## Human-review corrections and renewed full-page audit

The project owner identified the first error, `fa-` for `ſa-`, from Japanese *acamiga ſaſu*. A renewed page-wide audit then compared every Japanese phrase, every `ſ`/`f` occurrence, and every physical line ending against tight enlargements. It produced seven occurrence-level corrections across seven lines:

| Line | Pre-review reading | Confirmed print | Principal error mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `c1-l002` | `acamiga fa-` | `acamiga ſa-` | Long `ſ` was read as `f`; Japanese *akami ga sasu* should have triggered reinspection. |
| `c1-l023` | `dẽpres-` | `dẽpres` | A line-division hyphen was supplied where the right edge is blank. |
| `c1-l039` | `tol-` | `tol` | A line-division hyphen was supplied where none is printed. |
| `c1-l045` | `claridade.` | `claridade` | A terminal period was supplied where none is printed. |
| `c2-l009` | `ſel-` | `ſel` | A line-division hyphen was supplied where none is printed. |
| `c2-l015` | `Fito acaſu` | `Fiuo acaſu` | Japanese *fi uo acaſu* and enlargement expose `t`/`u` confusion. |
| `c2-l046` | `Tçuyi-` | `Tçuyi,` | A comma was interpreted as a line-division hyphen. |

The audit rechecked the remaining apparent divisions rather than globally removing or changing them. It retained, among others, printed division signs in `A-`, `cla-`, `des-`, `empreſta-`, `bõ-`, `tẽ-`, `enterra-`, and `ama-`; final punctuation on `c1-l047`; and the anomalous `Sõ-` / `fir` sequence. It initially proposed changing italic `A-` and `cla-` to `A=` and `cla=`, but this was unnecessary under the current convention: the equals-like impression is provisionally treated as a typeface-conditioned form of the same line-division sign and encoded as `-`. This hypothesis must be tested against later occurrences rather than asserted as a volume-wide fact.

## Benchmark status

Before human review, the independent rereading proposed two changes; both survive the renewed scan audit, so proposal precision is provisionally 2/2. Human review and the ensuing audit found seven additional occurrence-level corrections, already above the working target of no more than two or three. The renewed audit also proposed two unnecessary `-` to `=` changes, useful evidence that extra passes can introduce errors through typographic overinterpretation. Recall remains provisional until the corrected columns are rechecked, but the current result shows that independent rereading alone did not provide enough disciplined comparison of Japanese morphology and right-edge marks.
