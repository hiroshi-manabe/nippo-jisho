## Independent-comparison method

This page is the first trial of the procedure adopted after f15 remained above the human-correction target.

1. The first reading was made from the full page and overlapping enlarged column segments.
2. The page was reread in reverse segment order without consulting the first text.
3. The two readings were compared line by line. Differences became candidates rather than automatic edits.
4. Japanese, historical Portuguese, bilingual context, glyph, mark, and spacing passes were run on the resulting text.
5. Every accepted candidate was returned to a tight enlargement; a final full-page sweep produced no new candidate.
6. NINJAL's 36 source-order headwords (`002c01`–`002d19`) were then checked for coverage. The dataset did not determine diplomatic spelling or running text.

This hypothesis must be tested against later occurrences rather than asserted as a volume-wide fact.

## Benchmark status

The initial 2/2 proposal-precision estimate is superseded. Both proposals contained a useful observation—the space before `yuqu` and marked `Sõ-`—but neither complete proposed reading survived final column review. Future experiments must record atomic character or spacing proposals rather than bundle several claims into one candidate. Human review and the ensuing audits have now found twenty-nine occurrence-level corrections: seven at the first checkpoint and twenty-two during completed column review and its repetition sweep, far above the working target of two or three. The audit also proposed two unnecessary `-` to `=` changes, further evidence that extra passes can introduce errors. Recall remains provisional until the regenerated columns are rechecked, but independent comparison alone clearly failed the quality target.
