# Production Re-review: bnf-f0014

## Purpose and state

`bnf-f0014` is the first page processed end to end with the strengthened pre-human-review procedure adopted after the high correction yield on `f13`. This report records the procedure and result without claiming human approval. Column 1, column 2, and page furniture remain `pending` in the human-review progress file.

The canonical evidence is the Gallica master `f0014.jpg` identified by the checksum in the Level 1 source. Wikisource was not consulted during this re-review. NINJAL version 202510 was used only after the scan-derived checkpoint for entry coverage and suspicious-form comparison.

## Passes performed

1. Compared all 94 dictionary-text lines, both running headers, and the catchword with the native master through all twelve overlapping sixth-column tiles.
2. Used larger targeted crops wherever the default tile left a letter, mark, or spacing judgment uncomfortable.
3. Parsed the Japanese forms and examples for plausible words, particles, and inflectional endings, then returned every suspicious sequence to the scan.
4. Read every Portuguese gloss for syntactic and lexical coherence, historical spelling, abbreviations, and line-end continuation.
5. Compared the Japanese and Portuguese meanings to expose readings plausible in only one language.
6. Rechecked confusable glyph families, including long `ſ`/`f`, `u`/`v`, `g`/`q`, `n`/`u`, `m`/`u`, `i`/`l`, consonant counts, tildes, and accents.
7. Compared the 31 expected NINJAL source-order entries (`001c01`–`001d18`) after the independent text existed. No entry candidate was missing.
8. Adjudicated every candidate against enlargement, then repeated a complete scan sweep from the beginning. The final sweep produced no new candidate.

| `c1-l012` | `mino xita laqi.` | `mino xita ſaqi.` | Japanese *shita saki* made the apparent `l` suspicious; enlargement confirms long `ſ`. |

| `c1-l028` | `q̃ uſão` | `q̃ vſão` | Dedicated `u`/`v` audit confirms printed initial `v`. |

Several plausible suspicions were rejected after enlargement. In particular, the print supports `aburaqitta fito`, anomalous `Aburaguitra`, `funuuru`, `Aburicauarague`, and the source's existing word divisions and punctuation. Context was used to decide where to reinspect, not to regularize these forms.

## Result

The Level 1 page now has production status `scan_confirmed`: every line and page-furniture item has passed the strengthened checks, and a fresh final sweep found no further correction candidate. This status remains provisional with respect to the independent human checkpoint. The practical test is the correction yield when the user reviews each generated column; the working target is no more than two or three corrections per page.
