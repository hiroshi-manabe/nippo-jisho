On 2026-09-12, all 94 body lines received individual substantive English
comments during direct reading of native-resolution Bodleian scan crops,
followed by a separate complete second scan pass. Generated kana readings
were consulted, including failed conversions that exposed OCR errors.
After the independent first pass, NINJAL entries 090a01–090b22 were compared
for lexical coverage and identity, not for diplomatic typography.

Every actual body crop was inspected for its assigned row, first and last
words, accents, ascenders, descenders and right-rule context. The existing
full-column-width rectangles include the complete rows; none required
coordinate changes. Their review state is now recorded explicitly.

Fiǔna, were enlarged. Weak impressions remain qualified in the line notes.
